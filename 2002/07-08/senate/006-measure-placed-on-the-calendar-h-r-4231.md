# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 4231

```

  Mr. REID. It is my understanding H.R. 4231 is at the desk and due for 
its second reading.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator is correct.
  Mr. REID. I ask that H.R. 4231 be read for a second time, and I would 
then object to any further proceedings on this matter.

for the second time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 4231) to improve small business advocacy, and

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection having been heard, the bill will 
be placed on the calendar.
```

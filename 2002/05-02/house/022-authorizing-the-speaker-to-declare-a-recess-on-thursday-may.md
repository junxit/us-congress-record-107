# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, MAY 9, 2002, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING FORMER MEMBERS OF CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Blunt, Roy (B000575) [R-MO]

```
 AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, MAY 9, 2002, 
        FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING FORMER MEMBERS OF CONGRESS

  Mr. BLUNT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may be in 
order on Thursday, May 9, 2002, for the Speaker to declare a recess 
subject to the call of the Chair for the purpose of receiving in this 
Chamber former Members of Congress.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Missouri?
  There was no objection.
```

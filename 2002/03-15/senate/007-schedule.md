# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, shortly the Senate will vote on the 
confirmation of Executive Calendar No. 704, David Bury of Arizona, to 
be United States District Judge for the District of Arizona. Following 
that, we will return to the energy bill. The managers will be ready to 
accept amendments. We hope there can be some done today between the two 
managers. There will be no further rollcall votes. The majority leader 
announced last night we will come in, it appears, at about 3 o'clock on 
Monday, and further information will be given before we adjourn today.
  The leader has also announced we will have at least one vote 
beginning at 6 o'clock Monday. There could be more than one vote.

                          ____________________
```

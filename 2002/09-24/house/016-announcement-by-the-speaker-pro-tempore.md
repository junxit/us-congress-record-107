# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE.

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8, rule XX, the Chair 
will postpone further proceedings today on motions to suspend the rules 
on which a recorded vote or the yeas and nays are ordered or on which 
the vote is objected to under clause 6 of rule XX.
  Record votes on motions to suspend the rules ordered prior to 6:30 
p.m. may be taken today.
  Record votes on remaining motions to suspend the rules will be taken 
tomorrow.

                          ____________________
```

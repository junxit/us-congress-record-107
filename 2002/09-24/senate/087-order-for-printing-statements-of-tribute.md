# ORDER FOR PRINTING STATEMENTS OF TRIBUTE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                ORDER FOR PRINTING STATEMENTS OF TRIBUTE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Members have 
until Friday, October 4, at 12 noon to submit statements of tribute to 
Senator Strom Thurmond and the tributes then be printed as a Senate 
document.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```

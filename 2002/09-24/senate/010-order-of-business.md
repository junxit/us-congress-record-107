# ORDER OF BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           ORDER OF BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mrs. Carnahan). The Senator from Nevada.
  Mr. REID. Madam President, it is my understanding we are now going to 
proceed to a period of time to offer tributes to our friend, the 
distinguished Senator from South Carolina; is that true?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate is open for morning business for 
that purpose.

speaker be the majority leader, the second speaker be the Republican 
leader, followed by Senators Hollings, Stevens, Byrd, and a Republican, 
to be named at a later time.
```

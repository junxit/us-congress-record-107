# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, we will shortly conclude the debate on the 
confirmation. The two Senators from Alaska are going to speak about the 
new judge they are going to get in Alaska.
  I also note that we are moving along with the energy bill. We have 
some important amendments pending. We have a number of important 
issues. There is now talk of being able to complete this bill by a week 
from this Friday.
  As everyone knows, the important issues that remain--there are a lot 
of important issues, but the matters that appear to be quite 
contentious are those dealing with CAFE standards, ANWR drilling, and 
electricity regulation. So it appears that there is light at the end of 
the tunnel and that we can finish this legislation. We certainly hope 
so. But Members are going to have to be willing to come and offer 
amendments, and Members are going to have to be ready to vote at all 
times of the day and night.

                          ____________________
```

# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. So, Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed to a period of morning business, with Senators allowed to 
speak therein for a period of 10 minutes each, until 12:30 p.m., when, 
under the previous order, we will recess for the weekly party 
conferences.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Alaska.

                          ____________________
```

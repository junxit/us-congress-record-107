# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2146, TWO STRIKES AND YOU'RE OUT CHILD PROTECTION ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 366, H.R. 2146

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2146, TWO 
              STRIKES AND YOU'RE OUT CHILD PROTECTION ACT

  Mr. DIAZ-BALART (during special order of Mr. McInnis) from the 
Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 107-374) 
on the resolution (H. Res. 366) providing for consideration of the bill 
(H.R. 2146) to amend title 18 of the United States Code to provide life 
imprisonment for repeat offenders who commit sex offenses against 
children, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```

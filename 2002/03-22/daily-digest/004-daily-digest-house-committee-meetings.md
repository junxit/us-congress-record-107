# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
ELECTRONIC SURVEILLANCE IN THE NATION'S CAPITAL
Committee on Government Reform: Subcommittee on the District of 
Columbia held a hearing on ``Privacy vs. Security: Electronic 
Surveillance in the Nation's Capital.'' Testimony was heard from the 
following officials of the District of Columbia: Kathleen Patterson, 
member, Council and Chairwoman, Committee on the Judiciary; Margaret 
Nedelkoff Kellems, Deputy Mayor, Public Safety and Justice; and Charles 
Ramsey, Chief, Metropolitan Police Department; John Parsons, Associate 
Regional Director, National Capital Region, National Parks Service, 
Department of the Interior; and public witnesses.
```

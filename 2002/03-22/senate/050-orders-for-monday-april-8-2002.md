# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 8, 2002

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 8, 2002

  Mr. DODD. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until the hour of 3 p.m. Monday, April 8; 
that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
resume consideration of the energy reform bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

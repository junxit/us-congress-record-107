# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, at the request of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess awaiting the call of 
the Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 1:50 p.m., recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair and reassembled at 3:34 p.m. when called to 
order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Johnson).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Connecticut.

                          ____________________
```

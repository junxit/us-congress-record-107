# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess 
subject to the call of the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 4:17 p.m., recessed until 5:40 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Dayton).
```

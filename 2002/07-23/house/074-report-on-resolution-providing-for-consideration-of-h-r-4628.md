# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4628, INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2003

**Measures:** H.Res. 497, H.R. 4628

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4628, 
          INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2003

  Mrs. Myrick, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-607) on the resolution (H. Res. 497) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4628) to authorize appropriations 
for fiscal year 2003 for intelligence and intelligence-related 
activities of the United States Government, the Community Management 
Account, and the Central Intelligence Agency Retirement and Disability 
System, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```

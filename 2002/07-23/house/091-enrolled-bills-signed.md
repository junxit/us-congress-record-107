# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 1209, H.R. 2175, H.R. 3487

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Mr. Trandahl, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly enrolled 
bills of the House of the following titles, which were thereupon signed 
by the Speaker:

       H.R. 1209. An act to amend the Immigration and Nationality 
     Act to determine whether an alien is a child, for purposes of 
     classification as an immediate relative, based on the age of 
     the alien on the date the classification petition with 
     respect to the alien is filed, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 2175. An act to protect infants who are born alive.
       H.R. 3487. An act to amend the Public Health Service Act 
     with respect to health professions regarding the field of 
     nursing.
```

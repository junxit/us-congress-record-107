# IN HONOR AND REMEMBRANCE OF DEVOTED FAMILY MAN, PAUL VOINOVICH

```
     IN HONOR AND REMEMBRANCE OF DEVOTED FAMILY MAN, PAUL VOINOVICH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. DENNIS J. KUCINICH

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 23, 2002

  Mr. KUCINICH. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor and remembrance of 
Paul Voinovich, devoted husband, father and grandfather, successful 
businessman, and trusted friend to many.
  Following his graduation from Ohio University, Mr. Voinovich, 
followed in his father's footsteps by taking over the family 
architectural business, once known as the Voinovich Companies.
  Mr. Voinovich was an intuitive and savvy businessman, and was highly 
adept at the art of the deal. He was a loyal colleague and business 
mentor to many, and a treasured

[[Page 14394]]

friend as well. He warmly embraced life, and possessed a generous 
spirit. Mr. Voinovich was known to frequently help others in need, and 
did so in a quiet way away from the spotlight.
  Mr. Speaker, Mr. Paul Voinovich will be remembered as a devoted 
husband, father and grandfather, and trusted friend to many. Although 
he will be deeply missed, his devotion to family, kind nature, generous 
spirit, and great zest for life will live on through all who knew him 
well.

                          ____________________
```

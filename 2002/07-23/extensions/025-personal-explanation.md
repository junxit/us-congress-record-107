# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. XAVIER BECERRA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 23, 2002

  Mr. BECERRA. Mr. Speaker, on Monday, July 22, 2002, I was unable to 
cast my floor vote on rollcall Nos. 324, and 325. The votes I missed 
include rollcall vote 324 on the Motion to Suspend the Rules and Agree 
to H. Con. Res. 439, Honoring Corinne ``Lindy'' Claiborne Boggs; and 
rollcall vote 325 on the Motion to Suspend the Rules and Agree to H. 
Res. 492, Expressing Gratitude for the 10-month World Trade Center 
Cleanup and Recovery Efforts.
  Had I been present for the votes, I would have voted ``aye'' on 
rollcall votes 324 and 325.
```

# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ANTHONY D. WEINER

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 13, 2002

  Mr. WEINER. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably detained in my District on 
Tuesday, March 12, 2002, and I would like the Record to indicate how I 
would have voted had I been present.
  For rollcall vote No. 53, the bill to expand the class of 
beneficiaries who may apply for adjustment of status under section 
245(i) of the Immigration and Nationality Act by extending the deadline 
for classification petition and labor certification filings, I would 
have voted ``aye.''
```

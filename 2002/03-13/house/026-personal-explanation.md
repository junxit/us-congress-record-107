# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Measures:** H.R. 1885, H.R. 3090

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Ms. JACKSON-LEE of Texas. Mr. Speaker, on March 7 I had to return to 
my district on official business. On Rollcall No. 51, if I had been 
present, I would have voted no.
  On Rollcall No. 52, H.R. 3090, the economic stimulus package to 
increase the unemployment benefits for laid-off workers, I would have 
voted aye.
  On March 12, 2002, Rollcall No. 53, H.R. 1885, Enhanced Border 
Security and Visa Entry Reform Act of 2002, I was unavoidably detained 
in my district. If I had been present, I would have voted aye.
  Mr. Speaker, my final one, today, March 13, 2002, on Rollcall No. 54, 
the Journal vote, I was delayed because of air travel. I was coming 
from my district. If I had been present, I would have voted aye.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 25, 2002

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 25, 2002

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m., Thursday, April 25; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
to have expired; the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day; and the Senate proceed to executive session under the 
previous order; that there be 6 hours remaining under cloture on the 
Daschle-Bingaman substitute amendment, and that time consumed in 
executive session count against cloture.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


  

                          ____________________
```

# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER

```
                      ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER

  The SPEAKER. The Chair desires to announce that the practice of 
reserving seats prior to the joint session by placard or otherwise will 
not be allowed. Members may reserve their seats by physical presence 
only following the security sweep of the Chamber.

                          ____________________
```

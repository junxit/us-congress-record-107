# JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--THE STATE OF THE UNION ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--THE STATE OF THE UNION ADDRESS BY THE 
                     PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Presiding 
Officer of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the part 
of the Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the House of 
Representatives to escort the President of the United States into the 
House Chamber for the joint session to be held tonight, Tuesday, 
January 29, 2002, at 9 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.
  The Senator from Nebraska is recognized.

                          ____________________
```

# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2143, PERMANENT DEATH TAX REPEAL ACT OF 2001

**Measures:** H.Res. 435, H.R. 2143

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2143, 
                 PERMANENT DEATH TAX REPEAL ACT OF 2001

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 107-494) on the resolution (H. Res. 435) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2143) to make the repeal 
of the estate tax permanent, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```

# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I have spoken to both the majority and the 
Republican leader and told them that we were going to go into a period 
for morning business for the rest of the evening, and they both are 
aware of what we were going to do. Therefore, I ask unanimous consent 
that the Senate now proceed to a period for morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for a period up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```

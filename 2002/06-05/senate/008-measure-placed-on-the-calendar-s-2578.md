# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2578

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 2578

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2578

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I understand that S. 2578 is at the desk 
and is due for its second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator is correct.
  Mr. REID. I ask that S. 2578 be read a second time, and then I would 
object to any further proceeding.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the bill for 
the second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2578) to amend title 31 of the United States 
     Code to increase the public debt limit.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection having been heard, under 
the rule the bill will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```

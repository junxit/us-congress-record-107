# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       55. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the City of 
     Tamarac, Florida, relative to Resolution No. R-2001-333 
     petitioning the

[[Page H1411]]

     United States Congress to express condolences on behalf of 
     all Tamarac residents to the families of victims of the 
     September 11th terrorist attacks; expresses support to the 
     citizens of New York in their rebuilding efforts; expresses 
     confidence in the Nation, President Bush, the administration 
     and the United States Congress in their war against 
     terrorism; and encourages the citizenry to bind together in 
     the promises for the future of this Nation; which was 
     referred jointly to the Committees on the Judiciary and 
     Government Reform.
```

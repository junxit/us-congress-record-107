# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I will yield momentarily to my colleague, 
the junior Senator from Texas, whose pastor is our guest Chaplain 
today.
  The Senate will be in a period for morning business until 11 a.m. 
today. At 11 a.m., we will again begin consideration of the energy 
reform bill, which will be the 15th day we have been on this 
legislation. The Senate will recess, as we normally do on Tuesdays, 
from 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. for the weekly party conferences.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Texas.
```

# PLAN COLOMBIA SEMI-ANNUAL OBLIGATION REPORT--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES (H. DOC. NO. 107-198)

```
PLAN COLOMBIA SEMI-ANNUAL OBLIGATION REPORT--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT 
               OF THE UNITED STATES (H. DOC. NO. 107-198)

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on Appropriations and the Committee on International 
Relations and ordered to be printed:

To the Congress of the United States:
  Pursuant to section 3204(e), of Public Law 106-246, I am providing a 
report prepared by my Administration detailing the progress of spending 
by the executive branch during the last two quarters of Fiscal Year 
2001 in support of Plan Colombia.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, April 9, 2002.

                          ____________________
```

# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 363, H.Res. 377, H.R. 3958

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair 
will now put the question on motions to suspend the rules on which 
further proceedings were postponed earlier today.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  Journal vote, de novo;
  House Resolution 377, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 3958, by the yeas and nays;
  House Resolution 363, by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.

                          ____________________
```

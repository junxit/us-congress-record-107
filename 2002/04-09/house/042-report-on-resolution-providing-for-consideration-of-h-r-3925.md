# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3925, DIGITAL TECH CORPS ACT OF 2002

**Measures:** H.Res. 380, H.R. 3925

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3925, DIGITAL 
                         TECH CORPS ACT OF 2002

  Mrs. MYRICK, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-393) on the resolution (H. Res. 380) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3925) to establish an exchange 
program between the Federal Government and the private sector in order 
to promote the development of expertise in information technology 
management, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```

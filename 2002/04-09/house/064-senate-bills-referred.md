# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 1222, S. 1499

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 1222. An act to redesignate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 89 River Street in Hoboken, 
     New Jersey, as the ``Frank Sinatra Post Office Building'', to 
     the Committee on Government Reform.
       S. 1499. An act to provide assistance to small business 
     concerns adversely impacted by the terrorist attacks 
     perpetrated against the United States on September 11, 2001, 
     and for other purposes to the Committee on Small Business.

                          ____________________
```

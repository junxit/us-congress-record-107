# SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING INDICATORS 2002--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING INDICATORS 2002--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF 
                           THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on Science:

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by 42 U.S.C. 1863(j)(1), I am pleased to submit to the 
Congress a report prepared by the National Science Board entitled, 
``Science and Engineering Indicators--2002.'' This report represents 
the fifteenth in the series examining key aspects of the status of 
science and engineering in the United States.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, April 9, 2002.

                          ____________________
```

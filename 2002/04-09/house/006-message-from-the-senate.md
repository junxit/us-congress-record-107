# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed bills of the following titles in 
which the concurrence of the House is requested.

       S. 1222. An act to redesignate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 89 River Street in Hoboken, 
     New Jersey, as the ``Frank Sinatra Post Office Building''.
       S. 1321. An act to authorize the construction of a Native 
     American Cultural Center and Museum in Oklahoma City, 
     Oklahoma.
       S. 1499. An act to provide assistance to small business 
     concerns adversely impacted by the terrorist attacks 
     perpetrated against the United States on September 11, 2001, 
     and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```

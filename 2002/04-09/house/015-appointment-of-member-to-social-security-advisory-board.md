# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO SOCIAL SECURITY ADVISORY BOARD

```
        APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO SOCIAL SECURITY ADVISORY BOARD

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to section 
703 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 903) as amended by section 
103 of Public Law 103-296, the Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment of the following member on the part of the House to the 
Social Security Advisory Board to fill the existing vacancy thereon:
  Mrs. Dorcas R. Hardy, Spotsylvania, Virginia.
  There was no objection.
```

# ORDERS FOR TOMORROW, FEBRUARY 13, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                 ORDERS FOR TOMORROW, FEBRUARY 13, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Wednesday, February 13; that following the prayer and pledge 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the farm bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

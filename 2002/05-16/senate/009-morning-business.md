# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 10 a.m. Under the previous order, half the 
time until 10 a.m. shall be under the control of the majority leader or 
his designee.
  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I ask to be advised when 5 minutes remain 
on our time.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Chair will so advise.

                          ____________________
```

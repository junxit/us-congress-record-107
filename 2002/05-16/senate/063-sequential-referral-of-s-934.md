# SEQUENTIAL REFERRAL OF S. 934

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 934

```
                     SEQUENTIAL REFERRAL OF S. 934

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that S. 934, the Rocky Boy's/North 
Central Montana Regional Water System Act of 2001 be discharged from 
the Committee on Indian Affairs and then referred to the Committee on 
Energy and Natural Resources; further, that if and when the Committee 
on Natural Resources reports S. 934, then the measure be referred to 
the Committee on Indian Affairs.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

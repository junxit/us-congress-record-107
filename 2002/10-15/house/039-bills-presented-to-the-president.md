# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on October 11, 2002 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills.

       H.J. Res 122. Making further continuing appropriations for 
     the fiscal year 2003, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 2121. To make available funds under the Foreign 
     Assistance Act of 1961 to expand democracy, good governance, 
     and anti-corruption programs in the Russian Federation in 
     order to promote and strengthen democratic government and 
     civil society, etc.
       H.R. 4085. To increase, effective as of December 1, 2002, 
     the rates of compensation for veterans with service-connected 
     disabilities and the rates of dependency and indemnity 
     compensation for the survivors of certain disabled veterans.
       H.R. 5531. To facilitate famine relief efforts and a 
     comprehensive solution to the war in Sudan.

                          ____________________
```

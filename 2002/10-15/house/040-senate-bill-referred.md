# SENATE BILL REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 1632

```
                          SENATE BILL REFERRED

  A bill of the Senate of the following title was take from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 1632. An act to amend the Robert T. Stafford Disaster 
     Relief and Emergency Assistance Act to extend the deadline 
     for submission of State recommendations of local governments 
     to receive assistance for predisaster hazard mitigation and 
     to authorize the President to provide additional repair 
     assistance to individuals and households; to the Committee on 
     Transportation and Infrastructure.
```

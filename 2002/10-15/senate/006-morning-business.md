# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will now begin a period of morning business not to extend beyond 
the hour of 11 a.m., with Senators allowed to speak therein for up to 
10 minutes each.
  The order for the quorum call is rescinded. In my capacity as a 
Senator from Nevada, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.

                          ____________________
```

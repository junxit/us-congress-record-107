# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, the hour of 3 o'clock will be here in a 
minute or so. I ask unanimous consent that morning business be extended 
for an additional 30 minutes, with Senators permitted to speak therein, 
with the exception of Senator Kennedy. I ask that he be granted 15 
minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Massachusetts is recognized.

                          ____________________
```

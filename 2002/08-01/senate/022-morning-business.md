# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate now 
proceed to a period of morning business with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. It is my understanding Senator Baucus is recognized as in 
morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct. The Senator from 
Montana is recognized for a period of 10 minutes.
```

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to 
executive session to consider the following nominations: Executive 
Calendar Nos. 846, 847, 848, 849, 850, 876, 906, 907, 908, 909, 923, 
924, 925, 927, 928, 929, 930, 937, 938, 939, 940, 941, 942, 943, 944,

971, 972, 973, 974, 999, 1002, 1003. I ask unanimous consent that the 
nominations be confirmed, the motions to reconsider be laid on the 
table, the President be immediately notified of the Senate's action, 
any statements be printed in the Record, and that the Senate now return 
to legislative session, with the preceding all occurring without any 
intervening action or debate.

Republican leader, we read off a bunch of names, but these are people 
who have been waiting, some for a long time. Even though we did not 
read off all the names, this is very important, and these names refer 
to people who will play an important part in running our country.

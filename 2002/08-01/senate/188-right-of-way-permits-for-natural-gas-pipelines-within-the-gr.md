# RIGHT-OF-WAY PERMITS FOR NATURAL GAS PIPELINES WITHIN THE GREAT SMOKY MOUNTAINS NATIONAL PARK

**Measures:** H.R. 3380

```
 RIGHT-OF-WAY PERMITS FOR NATURAL GAS PIPELINES WITHIN THE GREAT SMOKY 
                        MOUNTAINS NATIONAL PARK

  The bill (H.R. 3380) to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to 
issue right-of-way permits for natural gas pipelines within the 
boundary of Great Smoky Mountains National Park, was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```

# AMENDMENTS TO THE CLEAR CREEK COUNTY, COLORADO, PUBLIC LANDS TRANSFER ACT OF 1993

```
 AMENDMENTS TO THE CLEAR CREEK COUNTY, COLORADO, PUBLIC LANDS TRANSFER 
                              ACT OF 1993

  The bill (H.R. 223) to amend the Clear Creek County, Colorado, Public 
Lands Transfer Act of 1993 to provide additional time for Clear Creek 
County to dispose of certain lands transferred to the county under the 
Act, was considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, 
and passed.
```

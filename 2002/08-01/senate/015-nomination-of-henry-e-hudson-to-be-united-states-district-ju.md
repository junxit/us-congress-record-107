```
 NOMINATION OF HENRY E. HUDSON TO BE UNITED STATES DISTRICT JUDGE FOR 
                    THE EASTERN DISTRICT OF VIRGINIA

Hudson, of Virginia, to be United States District Judge for the Eastern 
District of Virginia.
```

# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, this morning the Senate will begin 
consideration of the farm conference report. There is a unanimous 
consent agreement that there will be 12 hours of debate: 6 today, 6 
tomorrow. The Senate will recess from 12:30 to 2:15 today for party 
conferences. The leaders at this time are trying to decide whether 
there will be a vote after debate is completed today.
```

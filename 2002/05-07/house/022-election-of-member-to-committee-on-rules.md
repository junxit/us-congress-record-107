# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON RULES

**Speaking:** Frost, Martin (F000392) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 413

```
                ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON RULES

  Mr. FROST. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 413), and I ask 
unanimous consent for its immediate consideration in the House.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will report the resolution.
  The Clerk read as follows:

                              H. Res. 413

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and is 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committee of the 
     House of Representatives:
       Committee on Rules: Mr. McGovern of Massachusetts to rank 
     immediately after Mrs. Slaughter of New York.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```

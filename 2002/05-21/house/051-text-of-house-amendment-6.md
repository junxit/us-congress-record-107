# Text of House Amendment 6

**Speaking:** Callahan, Sonny (C000052) [R-AL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4775

```
H.R. 4775

                        Offered By: Mr. Callahan

       Amendment No. 6: In chapter 6 of title I, in the second 
     paragraph under the heading ``economic support fund'', insert 
     after the third proviso the following:

     ``: Provided further, That the funds appropriated in this 
     paragraph shall be available for obligation only if the 
     President determines and certifies to the Congress that 
     Israel and the Palestinian Authority are engaged in formal 
     negotiations on a peace treaty''
```

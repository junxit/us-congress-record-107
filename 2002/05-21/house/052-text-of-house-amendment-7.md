# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Callahan, Sonny (C000052) [R-AL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4775

```
H.R. 4775

                        Offered By: Mr. Callahan

       Amendment No. 7: In chapter 6 of title I, in the second 
     paragraph under the heading ``economic support fund''--
       (1) after the aggregate dollar amount, insert ``(increased 
     by $134,000,000)''; and
       (2) after the third proviso, insert the following: ``: 
     Provided further, That $134,000,000 of the funds appropriated 
     in this paragraph shall be made available for assistance for 
     Egypt''.
```

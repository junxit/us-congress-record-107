# Text of House Amendment 10

**Speaking:** Strickland, Ted (S001004) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 4775

```
H.R. 4775

                       Offered By: Mr. Strickland

       Amendment No. 10: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                TITLE III--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 3001. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the Department of Veterans Affairs to require a 
     copayment in excess of $2 for any 30-day or less supply of 
     medication provided to a veteran by the Department.
```

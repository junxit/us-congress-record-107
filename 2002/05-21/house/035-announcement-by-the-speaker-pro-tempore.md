# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Isakson). Pursuant to clause 8 of rule 
XX, the Chair will postpone further proceedings today on each motion to 
suspend the rules on which a recorded vote or the yeas and nays are 
ordered, or on which the vote is objected to under clause 6 of rule XX.
  Record votes on postponed questions may be taken in two groups, the 
first occurring before the debate has concluded on all motions to 
suspend the rules, and the second after the debate has concluded on the 
remaining motions.
```

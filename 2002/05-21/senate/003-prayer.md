# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Lloyd John Ogilvie, offered the following prayer:
  Dear God, You give the day and show the way. You guide what we are to 
do and say and help us without delay. Whatever challenges we must face, 
You promise us Your strength and grace. You never give us more than we 
can take, and guide the decisions we must make. Help us to look for 
vision from above and rejoice in Your unlimited love. When this day 
comes to an end, may we praise You for being our Father and our Friend. 
Amen.

                          ____________________
```

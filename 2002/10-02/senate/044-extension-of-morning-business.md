# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, it is my understanding the time for morning 
business has expired.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent the Senate now proceed to a period 
of morning business with Senators allowed to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each until 6:30 this evening.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Louisiana.

                          ____________________
```

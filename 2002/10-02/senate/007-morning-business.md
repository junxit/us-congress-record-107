# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 11:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
for up to 10 minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the time until 10 a.m. shall be under the 
control of the majority leader or his designee.
  Under the previous order, the first 20 minutes shall be under the 
control of the Senator from Vermont, Mr. Jeffords.
  The Senator from Vermont.

                          ____________________
```

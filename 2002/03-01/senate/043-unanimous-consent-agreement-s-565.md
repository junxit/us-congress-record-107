# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 565

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 565

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 565

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the motion to 
proceed to the motion to reconsider be agreed to; that the motion to 
reconsider be agreed to; and that at 6 p.m., Monday, March 4, there be 
15 minutes for debate, equally debated and controlled by Senators Dodd 
and McConnell, or their designees; that at 6:15 p.m., the Senate vote 
on the motion to invoke cloture on S. 565; further, that Senators have 
until 5:15 p.m. on Monday to file second-degree amendments.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

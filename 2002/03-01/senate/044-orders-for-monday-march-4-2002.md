# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 4, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 4, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 4 p.m., 
Monday, March 4. I further ask unanimous consent that following the 
prayer and the pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and there be a period for 
morning business until 6 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up 
to 10 minutes each, with the time equally divided between the two 
leaders, or their designees; further, at 6 p.m., the Senate resume 
consideration of the election reform bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

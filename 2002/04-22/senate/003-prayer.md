# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Lloyd John Ogilvie, offered the following prayer:
  Everloving God, we thank You for the rest and renewal of the weekend, 
for the promise that comes with this new week, and the hope we feel. We 
ask you to implant the eyes of our minds with trifocal lenses so that 
we may behold Your signature in the natural world around us, see the 
needs of people so we can care for them with sensitivity, and visualize 
the work that we must do. With minds alert and hearts full of 
gratitude, we honor You as our Sovereign. Thank You for meeting all the 
needs of our bodies, souls, and spirits so that we can serve You with 
renewed dedication. Now, as You hover around us as we pray, grant us 
wisdom throughout this day. In the name of Him who is our amazing 
grace. Amen.

                          ____________________
```

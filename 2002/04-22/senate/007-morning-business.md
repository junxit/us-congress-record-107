# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will be a period for the transaction of morning business not to extend 
beyond the hour of 2 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 
10 minutes each, with the time to be equally divided between the two 
leaders or their designees.
```

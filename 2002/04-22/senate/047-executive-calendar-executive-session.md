# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```

proceed to executive session to consider Calendar Nos. 774, 775, and 
782 through 787; that the nominations be confirmed; that the motions to 
reconsider be laid upon the table; that the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action; that any statements relating to the 
nominations be printed in the Record; and that the Senate return to 
legislative session, without any intervening action or debate.
```

# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 17, 2002

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 17, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., Wednesday, 
July 17; that following the prayer and the pledge, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and the Senate resume consideration of the motion to proceed to S. 812 
regarding affordable pharmaceuticals, under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

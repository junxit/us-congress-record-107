# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MARK UDALL

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 16, 2002

  Mr. UDALL of Colorado. Mr. Speaker, on July 15th I was unavoidably 
detained in returning to Washington, D.C. from Colorado. As a result, I 
was unable to vote on three measures considered that day.
  Had I been present, on rollcall No. 296, passage of H.R. 3482, the 
Cyber Security Enhancement Act, I would have voted ``yes.''
  I also would have voted ``yes'' on both rollcall No. 297, passage of 
H.R. 4755, and rollcall No. 298, passage of H.R. 3479.
```

proceed to executive session to consider Executive Calendar No. 1009 
through No. 1030 and all nominations on the Secretary's desk, the

table, the President be immediately notified of the Senate's action; 
that any statements appear at the appropriate place in the Record; and 
that the Senate then return to legislative session, with the preceding 
all occurring without any intervening action or debate.

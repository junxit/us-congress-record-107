# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 7, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 45

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 7, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today--maybe I should say tonight--it adjourn 
until the hour of 12 noon, Monday, October 7, 2002; that on Monday, 
immediately following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, and 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that there be a period for morning business until 1 p.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the first 
half under the control of Senator Wyden, and the second half under the 
control of the Republican leader or his designee; that at 1 p.m., the 
Senate resume consideration of S.J. Res. 45, with the time until 4 p.m. 
equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with Senators permitted to speak up to 15 minutes each.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```

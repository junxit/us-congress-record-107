# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
October 4, 2002, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nomination:
       ROBERT J. BATTISTA, OF MICHIGAN, TO BE A MEMBER OF THE 
     NATIONAL LABOR RELATIONS BOARD FOR THE TERM OF FIVE YEARS 
     EXPIRING AUGUST 27, 2006, VICE PETER J. HURTGEN, WHICH WAS 
     SENT TO THE SENATE ON JUNE 13, 2002.
```

# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 10, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 10, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it recess until 10 a.m., Friday, May 10; 
that following the prayer and the pledge, the time for the two leaders 
be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate be in a 
period of morning business until 11 a.m., with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally divided between 
the majority leader and the Republican leader or their designees; 
further, at 11 a.m., the Senate resume consideration of the trade bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

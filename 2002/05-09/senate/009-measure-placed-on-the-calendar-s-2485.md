                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2485

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I understand S. 2485 is at the desk and is 
due for a second reading.

  Mr. REID. I ask that the bill be read for a second time, and I will 
then object to any further proceedings at this time.

be recognized for up to 7 minutes. That will be out of Senator 
Stabenow's time.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

National Construction Safety Team Act: The House passed H.R. 4687, to 
provide for the establishment of investigative teams to assess building 
performance and emergency response and evacuation procedures in the 
wake of any building failure that has resulted in substantial loss of 
life or that posed significant potential of substantial loss of life by 
a yea-and-nay vote of 338 yeas to 23 nays, Roll No. 295. 
                                                         Pages H4557-64
  Pursuant to the rule the Committee on Science amendment in the nature 
of a substitute now printed in the bill (H. Rept. 107-530) was 
considered as an original bill for the purpose of amendment. 
                                                             Page H4561
Agreed to:
  Boehlert amendment that makes technical changes and defines team 
members who are not Federal Employees as Federal Government 
contractors; clarifies that nothing in the act shall be construed to 
confer authority on the National Institute of Standards and Technology 
to require the adoption of building standards, codes, or practices; and 
removes limitation on the amounts authorized to be appropriated. 
                                                             Page H4563
Meeting Hour--Monday, July 15: Agreed that when the House adjourns

hour debate.

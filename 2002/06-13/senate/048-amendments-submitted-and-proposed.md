       SA 3839. Mr. HATCH proposed an amendment to the bill S. 
     2600, supra.
       SA 3840. Mr. NELSON, of Florida submitted an amendment

     which was ordered to lie on the table.
       SA 3841. Mr. HOLLINGS submitted an amendment intended to be

       SA 3844. Mr. ENSIGN proposed an amendment to amendment SA

       SA 3845. Mr. REID (for Mrs. Feinstein) proposed an 
     amendment to the bill S. 672, to amend the Immigration and 
     Nationality Act to provide for the continued classification 
     of certain aliens as children for purposes of that Act in 
     cases where the aliens ``age-out'' while awaiting immigration 
     processing, and for other purposes.
       SA 3846. Mr. REID (for Mrs. Feinstein) proposed an 
     amendment to the bill H.R. 1209, to amend the Immigration and 
     Nationality Act to determine whether an alien is a child, for 
     purposes of classification as an immediate relative, based on 
     the age of the alien on the date the classification petition 
     with respect to the alien is filed, and for other purposes.

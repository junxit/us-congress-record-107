# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       57. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the LaSalle County 
     Board, Illinois, relative to Resolution No. 02-48 petitioning 
     the United States Congress that the LaSalle County Board 
     opposes any changes to the 800 MHz Band, and is in opposition 
     to the Federal Communications Commission Notice of Proposed 
     Rule Making (FCC 02-81) WT Docket No. 02-55; to the Committee 
     on Energy and Commerce.
       58. Also, a petition of the LaSalle County Board, Illinois, 
     relative to Resolution No. 02-47 petitioning the United 
     States Congress that LaSalle County endorses a federal 
     subsidy for passenger rail service and a high speed passenger 
     rail line through LaSalle County and Northern Illinois; to 
     the Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure.
```

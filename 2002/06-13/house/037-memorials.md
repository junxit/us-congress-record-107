# MEMORIALS

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:
       290. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature of 
     the State of New Hampshire, relative to House Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 21 memorializing the United States Congress 
     urging the state attorneys general and the Federal Trade 
     Commission to enforce the Telemarketing Sales Rule and urging 
     Congress to adopt the Know Your Caller Act of 2001; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce.
       291. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     New Hampshire, relative to House Concurrent Resolution No. 27 
     memorializing the United States Congress and the Department 
     of Justice to complete its inquiry into the mistreatment of 
     Italian-Americans during World War II with all due speed and 
     release the results of such inquiry to the public; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
```

# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 3763, H.R. 5121

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate agrees to the report of the committee of 
conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendment 
of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 3763) ``An Act to protect investors by 
improving the accuracy and reliability of corporate disclosures made 
pursuant to the securities laws, and for other purposes.''.
  The message also announced that the Senate has passed with amendments 
in which the concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of the House 
of the following title:

       H.R. 5121. An act making appropriations for the Legislative 
     Branch for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2003, and for 
     other purposes.

  The message also announced that the Senate insists upon its 
amendments to the bill (H.R. 5121) ``An Act making appropriations for 
the Legislative Branch for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2003, 
and for other purposes,'' requests a conference with the House on the 
disagreeing votes of the two Houses thereon, and appoints Mr. Durbin, 
Mr. Johnson, Mr. Reed, Mr. Byrd, Mr. Bennett, Mr. Stevens, and Mr. 
Cochran, to be the conferees on the part of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```

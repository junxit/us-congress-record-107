# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 24, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 24, 2002

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that, when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m., Monday, 
June 24; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate be in a period for morning business until 4 p.m., 
with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
time equally divided between the two leaders or their designees; that 
at 4 p.m., the Senate resume consideration of the Department of Defense 
authorization bill, with Senator Smith of New Hampshire or his designee 
recognized to offer his amendment regarding abaya.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

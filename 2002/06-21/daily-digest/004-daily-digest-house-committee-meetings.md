# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
COMMERCE, JUSTICE, STATE AND JUDICIARY APPROPRIATIONS
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Commerce, Justice, State 
and Judiciary held a hearing on FBI Reorganization. Testimony was heard 
from Robert Mueller, Director, FBI, Department of Justice; David M. 
Walker, Comptroller General, GAO; Richard Thornburgh, FBI Project Panel 
Chair, National Academy of Public Administration; and Nancy Savage, 
President, FBI Agents Association.
MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH MEASURES
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Ordered reported the following: 
Medicare Prescription Drug Benefit, amended; Provisions Relating to 
Part B, amended; Medicare+Choice Revitalization and Medicare+Choice 
Competition Program; Medicare Benefits Administration; Internet 
Pharmacies; and Disproportionate Share Hospital (DSH) Payments.
```

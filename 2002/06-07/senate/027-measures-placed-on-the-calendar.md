# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

**Measures:** H.R. 4800, H.R. 4823

```
                    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

  The following bills were read the second time, and placed on the 
calendar:

       H.R. 4800. An act to repeal the sunset of the Economic 
     Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 with respect 
     to the expansion of the adoption credit and adoption 
     assistance programs.
       H.R. 4823. An act to repeal the sunset of the Economic 
     Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 with respect 
     to the exclusion from Federal income tax for restitution 
     received by victims of the Nazi Regime.

                          ____________________
```

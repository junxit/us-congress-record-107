# REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO COORDINATING COUNCIL ON JUVENILE JUSTICE AND DELINQUENCY PREVENTION

```
REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO COORDINATING COUNCIL ON JUVENILE JUSTICE AND 
                         DELINQUENCY PREVENTION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Kerns). Without objection, pursuant to 
Section 206 of the Juvenile Justice and Delinquency Prevention Act of 
1974 (42 U.S.C. 5616) and upon the recommendation of the minority 
leader, the Chair announces the Speaker's reappointment of the 
following member on the part of the House to the Coordinating Council 
on Juvenile Justice and Delinquency Prevention to a 2-year term:
  Mr. Gordon A. Martin, Roxbury, Massachusetts.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

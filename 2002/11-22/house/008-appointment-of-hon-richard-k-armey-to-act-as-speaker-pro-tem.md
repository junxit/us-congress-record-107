# APPOINTMENT OF HON. RICHARD K. ARMEY TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS TODAY

```
 APPOINTMENT OF HON. RICHARD K. ARMEY TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO 
            SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS TODAY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
Communication from the Speaker:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, November 22, 2002.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Richard K. Armey to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions on this day.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

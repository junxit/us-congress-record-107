# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 1577

```
Committee Meetings
FEDERAL PRISON INDUSTRIES--UNFAIR COMPETITION WITH SMALL BUSINESSES
Committee on Small Business: On November 21, the Committee held a 
hearing on Federal Prison Industries' Unfair Competition with Small 
Businesses: Potential Interim Administrative Solutions, with discussion 
of H.R. 1577, Federal Prison Industries Competition in Contracting Act 
of 2002. Testimony was heard from Angela B. Styles, Administrator, 
Office of Federal Procurement Policy, OMB; Kenneth R. Rocks, Chairman, 
Board of Directors, Federal Prison Industries, Inc.; and public 
witnesses.
```

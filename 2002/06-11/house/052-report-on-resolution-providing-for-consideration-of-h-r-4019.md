# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4019, PERMANENT MARRIAGE PENALTY RELIEF ACT OF 2002

**Measures:** H.Res. 440, H.R. 4019

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4019, 
             PERMANENT MARRIAGE PENALTY RELIEF ACT OF 2002

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 107-504) on the resolution (H. Res. 440) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4019) to provide that the 
marriage penalty relief provisions of the Economic Growth and Tax 
Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 shall be permanent, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```

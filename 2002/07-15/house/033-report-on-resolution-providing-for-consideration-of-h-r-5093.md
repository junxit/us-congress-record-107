# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5093, DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2003

**Measures:** H.Res. 483, H.R. 5093

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5093, 
  DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 
                                  2003

  Mr. Hastings of Washington (during the Special Order of Mr. McInnis), 
from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 
107-577) on the resolution (H. Res. 483) providing for consideration of 
the bill (H.R. 5093) making appropriations for the Department of the 
Interior and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
2003, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```

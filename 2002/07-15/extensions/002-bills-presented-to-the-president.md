# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on July 12, 2002 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills.
     Number and Title
  H.J. Res 87. Approving the site at Yucca Mountain, Nevada, for the 
development of a repository for the disposal of high-level radioactive 
waste and spent nuclear fuel, pursuant to the Nuclear Waste Policy Act 
of 1982.
  H.R. 2362. To establish the Benjamin Franklin Tercentenary 
Commission.
  H.R. 3871. To provide for an independent investigation of Forest 
Service firefighter deaths that are caused by wildfire entrapment or 
burnover.

                          ____________________
```

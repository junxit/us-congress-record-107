# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

**Measures:** H.R. 4635, H.R. 4954, H.R. 5017

```
                    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

  The following bills were read the second time, and placed on the 
calendar:

       H.R. 4635. An act to amend title 49, United States Code, to 
     establish a program for Federal flight deck officers, and for 
     other purposes.
       H.R. 4954. An act to amend title XVIII of the Social 
     Security Act to provide for a voluntary program for 
     prescription drug coverage under the Medicare Program, to 
     modernize and reform payments and the regulatory structure of 
     the Medicare Program, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 5017. An act to amend the Temporary Emergency Wildfire 
     Suppression Act to facilitate the ability of the Secretary of 
     the Interior and the Secretary of Agriculture to enter into 
     reciprocal agreements with foreign countries for the sharing 
     of personnel to fight wildfires.
```

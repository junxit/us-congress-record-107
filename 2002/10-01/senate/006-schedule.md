# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, the Chair will shortly announce a period of 
morning business with the first half under the control of the 
Republicans and the second half under the control of the majority. I 
ask unanimous consent that after the Chair's ruling the Senator from 
New Mexico be recognized for up to 5 minutes to speak, as the guest 
Chaplain is from the State of New Mexico. I ask unanimous consent that 
the time not be charged against either the Democrats or the Republicans 
in that morning business.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  The Senator from New Mexico is recognized.
```

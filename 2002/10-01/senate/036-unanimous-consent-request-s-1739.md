# UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--S. 1739

**Speaking:** Cleland, Max (C001034) [D-GA], Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

**Measures:** S. 1739

```
                   UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--S. 1739

  Mr. CLELAND. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of calendar No. 544, S. 1739; 
that the Cleland amendment at the desk be agreed to, the bill, as 
amended, be read three times and passed, and the motion to reconsider 
be laid upon the table without further intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. BOND. Madam President, I object on behalf of Members on this 
side.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  Mr. CLELAND. Madam President, I yield the floor.
  Mr. BOND. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BOND. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent the order for the 
quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

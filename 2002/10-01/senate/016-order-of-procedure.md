# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, the majority has 21 minutes. I am going to 
use a few minutes. Following my remarks, I ask unanimous consent that 
the Senator from Missouri, Mrs. Carnahan, have 6 minutes; the Senator 
from Washington, Ms. Cantwell, have 5 minutes; and Senator Kennedy have 
10 minutes. And if we use extra time, that would just be counted 
against the time we have before the cloture vote. We each have a half 
hour on that.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

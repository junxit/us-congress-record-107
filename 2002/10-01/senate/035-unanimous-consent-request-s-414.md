# UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--S. 414

**Speaking:** Cleland, Max (C001034) [D-GA], Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

**Measures:** S. 414

```
                   UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--S. 414

  Mr. CLELAND. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of calendar No. 487, S. 414; 
that the committee-reported amendments be agreed to, the bill, as 
amended, be read three times and passed, and the motion to reconsider

side.
```

# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 1646

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on September 30, 2002 
he presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bill.

       H.R. 1646. To authorize appropriations for the Department 
     of State for fiscal year 2003, to authorize appropriations 
     under the Arms Export Control Act and the Foreign Assistance 
     Act of 1961 for security assistance for fiscal year 2003, and
```

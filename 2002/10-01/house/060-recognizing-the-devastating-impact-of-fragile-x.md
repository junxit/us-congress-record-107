# RECOGNIZING THE DEVASTATING IMPACT OF FRAGILE X

**Measures:** H.Res. 398

```
            RECOGNIZING THE DEVASTATING IMPACT OF FRAGILE X

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The pending business is the question of 
suspending the rules and agreeing to the resolution, H. Res. 398.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Illinois (Mr. Shimkus) that the House suspend the rules 
and agree to the resolution, H. Res. 398.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```

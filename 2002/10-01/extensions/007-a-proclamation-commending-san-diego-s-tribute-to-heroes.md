# A PROCLAMATION COMMENDING SAN DIEGO'S TRIBUTE TO HEROES

```
        A PROCLAMATION COMMENDING SAN DIEGO'S TRIBUTE TO HEROES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 1, 2002

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, Whereas, San Diego's Tribute to Heroes was 
established to honor and support local heroes and their organizations; 
and
  Whereas, San Diego's Tribute to Heroes should be commended for its 
worthwhile efforts for servicemen and their families, emergency 
workers, and children's health care; and
  Whereas, San Diego's Tribute to Heroes ball will be held October 5, 
2002 at the San Diego Aerospace Museum;
  Therefore, I join with the residents of the entire 18th Congressional 
District in recognizing San Diego's Tribute to Heroes organization for 
its dedication.

                          ____________________
```

# CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2714

**Speaking:** DeMint, Jim (D000595) [R-SC]

**Measures:** H.R. 2714

```
            CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2714

  Mr. DeMINT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 2714, a bill originally 
introduced by the gentleman from Oklahoma (Mr. Largent), for the 
purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting reprints pursuant to clause 
7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Isakson). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from South Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```

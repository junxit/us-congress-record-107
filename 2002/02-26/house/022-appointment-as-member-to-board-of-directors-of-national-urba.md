# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NATIONAL URBAN AIR TOXICS RESEARCH CENTER

```
   APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NATIONAL URBAN AIR 
                         TOXICS RESEARCH CENTER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to section 
112 of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7412), the Chair announces the 
Speaker's appointment of the following member on the part of the House 
to the Board of Directors of the National Urban Air Toxics Research 
Center to fill the existing vacancy thereon:
  Mr. Hans P. Blaschek, Champaign, Illinois.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

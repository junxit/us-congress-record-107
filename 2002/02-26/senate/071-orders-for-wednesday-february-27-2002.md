# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 27, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 27, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 a.m. on 
Wednesday, February 27. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Wednesday, immediately following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; further, that the Senate resume consideration of the election 
reform bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDER OF PROCEDURE (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

**Measures:** S. 565

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the previous 
order with respect to the Jorgenson nomination be vitiated; that 
immediately following the first vote today with respect to the 
amendment to S. 565, the Senate proceed to executive session to 
consider the nomination of Cindy Jorgenson; that once the nomination is 
reported, the Senate, without further intervening action, proceed to a 
vote on confirmation; that upon confirmation the motion to reconsider 
be laid upon the table, the President be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action, and the Senate return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that it be in 
order to request the yeas and nays on the nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second? There is a 
sufficient second.
  The yeas and nays were ordered.

                          ____________________
```

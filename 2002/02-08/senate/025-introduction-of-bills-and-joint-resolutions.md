# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1924, S. 1925, S. 1926, S. 1927

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. LIEBERMAN (for himself, Mr. Santorum, Mr. Bayh, 
             Mr. Brownback, Mr. Nelson of Florida, Mr. Cochran, 
             Mrs. Carnahan, Mr. Lugar, Mrs. Clinton, and Mr. 
             Hatch):
       S. 1924. A bill to promote charitable giving, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. KERRY (for himself, Mr. Kennedy, and Mr. Gregg):
       S. 1925. A bill to establish the Freedom's Way national 
     Heritage Area in the States of Massachusetts and New 
     Hampshire, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Energy 
     and Natural Resources.
           By Mr. KERRY (for himself and Mr. Hollings):
       S. 1926. A bill to improve passenger automobile fuel 
     economy and safety, reduce greenhouse gas emissions, reduce 
     dependence on foreign oil, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
           By Mr. DAYTON:
       S. 1927. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to freeze the highest Federal income tax rate at 38.6 
     percent; to the Committee on Finance.

                          ____________________
```

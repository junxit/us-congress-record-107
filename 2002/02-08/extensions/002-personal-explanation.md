**Speaking:** Maloney, Carolyn B. (M000087) [D-NY]

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. CAROLYN B. MALONEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, February 8, 2002

honoring the contributions of Catholic schools.

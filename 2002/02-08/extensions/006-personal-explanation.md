# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Maloney, Carolyn B. (M000087) [D-NY]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. CAROLYN B. MALONEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, February 8, 2002

  Mrs. MALONEY of New York. Mr. Speaker, on February 7, 2002, I was

12 was on agreeing to the resolution to providing for consideration of 
H.R. 3394, the Cyber Security Research and Development Act.
```

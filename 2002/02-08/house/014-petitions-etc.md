# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       49. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Office of the 
     Vice Mayor, Municipality of San Rafael, Republic of the 
     Philippines, relative to Municipal Resolution No. 2001-103 
     petitioning the United States Congress that the Sangguniang 
     Bayan members express sympathy and offer prayers to the 
     innocent victims of the September 11, 2001 terrorist

[[Page 875]]

     attacks of the World Trade Center and the Pentagon while at 
     the same time condemning in its strongest terms the dastardly 
     acts against humanity causing untold misery, anguish and 
     trauma to the soul and spirit; to the Committee on Government 
     Reform.
```

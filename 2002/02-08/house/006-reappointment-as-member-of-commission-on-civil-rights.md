# REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER OF COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS

```
         REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER OF COMMISSION ON CIVIL RIGHTS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to section 
2(b) of Public Law 98-183, amended by Public Law 103-419, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's reappointment on the part of the House of Dr. 
Abigail N. Thernstrom, of Lexington, Massachusetts, to the Commission 
on Civil Rights for a 6-year term beginning on February 12, 2002.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

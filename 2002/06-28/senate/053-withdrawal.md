# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on June 
28, 2002, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       Francis L. Cramer, III, of New Hampshire, to be a Judge of 
     the United States Tax Court for a term expiring fifteen years 
     after he takes office, which was sent to the Senate on 
     November 28, 2001.
```

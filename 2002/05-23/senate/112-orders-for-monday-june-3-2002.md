# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 3, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 118

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 3, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn under the provisions of S. 
Con. Res. 118 until 1 p.m., Monday, June 3; that following the prayer 
and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate be in a period for morning 
business until 2 p.m., with the Senators permitted to speak for up to 
10 minutes each; further, at 2 p.m. the Senate proceed under the 
previous consent; further, that if the House fails to adopt S. Con. 
Res. 118, the Senate convene Monday, May 27, at 10 a.m. for a pro forma 
session only and then adjourn until Thursday, May 30, at 10 a.m. for a 
pro forma session only and then adjourn until Monday, June 3, at 1 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# Text of House Amendment 26

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 4775

```
H.R. 4775

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson-Lee of Texas

       Amendment No. 26: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

                TITLE III--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 3001. None of the funds appropriated in this Act may 
     be used to prevent the Department of Justice from releasing 
     to the Congress the names of those detained pursuant to the 
     September 11, 2001 terrorist attacks or providing an 
     explanation to the Congress for not releasing such names.
```

# Text of House Amendment 29

**Speaking:** Sherman, Brad (S000344) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4775

```
H.R. 4775

                        Offered By: Mr. Sherman

       Amendment No. 29: Page 138, after line 12, insert the 
     following:

                    TITLE III--ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 3001. None of the funds made available in this Act for 
     humanitarian and refugee assistance for the West Bank and 
     Gaza shall be made available for the United Nations Relief 
     and Works Agency.
```

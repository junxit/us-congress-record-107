# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10:30 A.M. MONDAY, NOVEMBER 4, 2002

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10:30 A.M. MONDAY, NOVEMBER 4, 2002

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will stand adjourned until 10:30 a.m., Monday, November 4, 2002.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10:33 and 50 seconds, adjourned until 
Monday, November 4, at 10:30 a.m.N O T I C E

Effective January 1, 2003, the subscription price of the 
Congressional Record will be $434 per year or $217 for six months. 
Individual issues may be purchased for $6.00 per copy. 
Subscriptions in microfiche format will be $141 per year with 
single copies priced at $1.50. This price increase is necessary 
based upon the cost of printing and distribution.
```

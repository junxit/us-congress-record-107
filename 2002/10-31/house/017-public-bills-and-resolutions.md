# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 596

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII,

       Mr. LARSEN of Washington introduced a resolution (H. Res. 
     596) recognizing Senator Henry Jackson, commemorating the 
     30th anniversary of the introduction of the Jackson-Vanik 
     Amendment, and reaffirming the commitment of the House of 
     Representatives to combat human rights violations worldwide; 
     which was referred to the Committee on Ways and Means, and in 
     addition to the Committee on International Relations, for a 
     period to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each 
     case for consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
```

# PRINTING OF PROCEEDINGS OF SPECIAL CEREMONIAL MEETING OF UNITED STATES CONGRESS HELD IN FEDERAL HALL, NEW YORK, NEW YORK, ON SEPTEMBER 6, 2002

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

```
                              {time}  1915
PRINTING OF PROCEEDINGS OF SPECIAL CEREMONIAL MEETING OF UNITED STATES 
CONGRESS HELD IN FEDERAL HALL, NEW YORK, NEW YORK, ON SEPTEMBER 6, 2002

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that proceedings of 
the special ceremonial meeting of the United States Congress held in 
Federal Hall, New York, New York, on September 6, 2002, be printed in 
the Record, and that all Members have 5 legislative days to insert 
their remarks on the topic of the ceremonial meeting.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Isakson). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```

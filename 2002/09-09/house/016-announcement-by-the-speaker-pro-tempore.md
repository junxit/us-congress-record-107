# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair 
announces that he will postpone further proceedings today on motions to 
suspend the rules on which a recorded vote or the yeas and nays are 
ordered, or on which the vote is objected to under clause 6 of rule XX.

[[Page H6090]]

  Any record votes on postponed questions will be taken after debate 
has concluded on all motions to suspend the rules but not before 6:30 
p.m. today.

                          ____________________
```

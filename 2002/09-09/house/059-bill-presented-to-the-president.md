# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 5012

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on September 9, 2002 
he presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bill.
       H.R. 5012. To amend the John F. Kennedy Center Act to 
     authorize the Secretary of Transportation to carry out a 
     project for construction of a plaza adjacent to the John F. 
     Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts, and for other 
     purposes.
```

# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 5319

**Speaking:** McInnis, Scott (M000477) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 5319

```
          REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 5319

  Mr. McINNIS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to have the 
gentleman from Florida (Mr. Hastings) removed as a cosponsor of H.R. 
5319. The record should reflect that the gentleman from Washington (Mr. 
Hastings) was intended to be an original cosponsor of H.R. 5319.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Isakson). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Colorado?
  There was no objection.
```

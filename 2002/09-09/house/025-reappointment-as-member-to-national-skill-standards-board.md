# REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO NATIONAL SKILL STANDARDS BOARD

```
       REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO NATIONAL SKILL STANDARDS BOARD

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, pursuant to section 
503(b)(3) of the National Skill Standards Act of 1994 (20 U.S.C. 5933), 
and upon recommendation of the minority leader, the Chair announces the 
Speaker's reappointment of the following member on the part of the 
House to the National Skill Standards Board for a 4-year term:
  William E. Weisgerber, Iona, Michigan.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

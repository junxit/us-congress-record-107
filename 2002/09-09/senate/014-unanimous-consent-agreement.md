# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

**Speaking:** Leahy, Patrick J. (L000174) [D-VT]

```
                      UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Mr. LEAHY. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent regarding the 
time of the chairman and the ranking member of the Judiciary Committee

the expiration of that time.
```

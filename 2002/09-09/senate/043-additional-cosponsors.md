                                 S. 155

  At the request of Mr. Bingaman, the name of the Senator from Illinois 
(Mr. Durbin) was added as a cosponsor of S. 155, a bill to amend title 
5, United States Code, to eliminate an inequity in the applicability of 
early retirement eligibility requirements to military reserve 
technicians.

  At the request of Ms. Collins, the name of the Senator from Minnesota 
(Mr. Dayton) was added as a cosponsor of S. 561, a bill to provide that

  At the request of Mr. Nelson of Florida, his name was added as a

Medicare, Medicaid, and SCHIP Benefits Improvement and Protection Act 
of 2000.


                                 S. 611

  At the request of Ms. Mikulski, the name of the Senator from 
Minnesota (Mr. Dayton) was added as a cosponsor of S. 611, a bill to 
amend title II of the Social Security Act to provide that the reduction 
in social security benefits which are required in the case of spouses 
and surviving spouses who are also receiving certain Government 
pensions shall be equal to the amount by which two-thirds of the total 
amount of the combined monthly benefit (before reduction) and monthly 
pension exceeds $1,200, adjusted for inflation.

# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING JONATHAN W. WEISS

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
              A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING JONATHAN W. WEISS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 9, 2002

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, Whereas, Jonathan Weiss has devoted himself to 
serving others through his membership in the Boy Scouts of America; and
  Whereas, Jonathan Weiss has shared his time and talent with the 
community in which he resides; and
  Whereas, Jonathan Weiss has demonstrated a commitment to meet 
challenges with enthusiasm, confidence and outstanding service; and
  Whereas, Jonathan Weiss must be commended for the hard work and 
dedication he put forth in earning the Eagle Scout Award;
  Therefore, I join with Troop 401, the residents of Jefferson County, 
and the entire 18th Congressional District in congratulating Jonathan 
W. Weiss as he receives the Eagle Scout Award.

                          ____________________
```

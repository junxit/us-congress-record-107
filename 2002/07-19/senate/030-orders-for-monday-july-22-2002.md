# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 22, 2002

**Speaking:** Lincoln, Blanche L. (L000035) [D-AR]

**Measures:** S. 812

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 22, 2002

  Mrs. LINCOLN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 2 
p.m., Monday, July 22; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 812, with the 
time until 6 p.m. equally divided between the two managers or their 
designees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

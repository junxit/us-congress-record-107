# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MARK FOLEY

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 18, 2002

  Mr. FOLEY. Mr. Speaker, I wish to inform you that yesterday I 
inadvertently misvoted on rollcall No. 97 on final passage of H.R. 476, 
the Child Custody Protection Act. I have supported this legislation in 
the past and continue to do so and my intention was to vote in support 
of it yesterday. I did not realize until after the voting had closed 
that I had mistakenly voted otherwise. I regret any confusion this may 
have caused and want the Record to reflect my support for H.R. 476.
```

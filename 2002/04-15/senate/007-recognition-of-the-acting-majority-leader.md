# RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

```
               RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Nevada is 
recognized.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, as the Chair has announced, there will be a 
period of morning business until 2 p.m. Senator Dorgan, by virtue of a 
previous order, is going to use 30 minutes of that time. At 2 p.m., the 
Senate will resume consideration of the Border Security Act. There will 
be a rollcall vote this afternoon at 5:30 in relation to the Border 
Security Act or an Executive Calendar nomination.
```

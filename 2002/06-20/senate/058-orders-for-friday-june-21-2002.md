# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 21, 2002

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 21, 2002

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on Friday, June 21; that 
following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
resume consideration of the Department of Defense authorization bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

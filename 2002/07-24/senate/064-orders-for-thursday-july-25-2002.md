# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 25, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 812

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 25, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 9:30 a.m., 
Thursday, July 25; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate be in a period for morning business until 
10:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, 
with the first half of the time under the control of the Republican 
leader or his designee and the second half of the time under the 
control of the Democratic leader or his designee; that at 10:30 a.m., 
the Senate resume consideration of S. 812.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I apologize to the Presiding Officer. I 
indicated we would be finished by 7 p.m. and we missed that by 35 
minutes.

                          ____________________
```

# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 4775

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:03 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the report of the committee of conference on the disagreeing 
votes of the two Houses on the amendment of the Senate to the bill 
(H.R. 4775) making supplemental appropriations for further recovery 
from and response to terrorist attacks on the United States for the 
fiscal year ending September 30, 2002, and for other purposes.
```

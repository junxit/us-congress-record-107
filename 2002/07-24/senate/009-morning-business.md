# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 11 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The first half of the time shall be under the control of the majority 
leader or his designee. The second half of the time shall be under the 
control of the Republican leader or his designee.
  The Senator from Minnesota is recognized.
  (Ms. STABENOW assumed the Chair.)

                          ____________________
```

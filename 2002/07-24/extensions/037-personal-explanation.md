# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** McCarthy, Carolyn (M000309) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.Res. 488, H.Res. 489, H.R. 5121

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CAROLYN McCARTHY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 23, 2002

  Mrs. McCARTHY of New York. Mr. Speaker, due to a death in my family, 
I was absent for votes on July 18, 2002. Had I been present, I would 
have opposed H. Res. 489, supported the amendment offered by Mr. Moran 
(VA), and supported final passage of H.R. 5121.
  I would have also opposed the previous question to H. Res. 488 and 
opposed H. Res. 488.
```

# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate agrees to the report of the committee of 
conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendment 
of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 4775) ``An Act making supplemental 
appropriations for further recovery from and response to terrorist 
attacks on the United States for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
2002, and for other purposes.''.
```

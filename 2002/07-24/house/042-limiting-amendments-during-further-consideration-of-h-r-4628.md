# LIMITING AMENDMENTS DURING FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4628, INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT OF FISCAL YEAR 2003

```
                              {time}  0200
    LIMITING AMENDMENTS DURING FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4628, 
           INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT OF FISCAL YEAR 2003

  Mr. GOSS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during 
consideration of H.R. 4628 in the Committee of the Whole pursuant to 
House Resolution 497, no further amendment to the committee amendment 
in the nature of a substitute may be offered after the legislative day 
of July 24, 2002, except pro forma amendments offered by the chairman 
or ranking minority member of the Permanent Select Committee on 
Intelligence or their designees for the purpose of debate.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Simpson). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.
```

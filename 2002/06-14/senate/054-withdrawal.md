# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on June 
14, 2002, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:
       Cheryl Feldman Halpern, of New Jersey, to be a Member of 
     the Board of Directors of the Corporation for Public 
     Broadcasting for the remainder of the term expiring January 
     31, 2004, which was sent to the Senate on November 9, 2001.
```

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, we are going to be in a period of morning 
business until 9:35 a.m. Senator Murray has the first 20 minutes. The

important antiterrorism insurance legislation.
  We hope people will begin to move forward on this legislation. The 
majority leader indicated we are going to pass this legislation. It is 
just a question of whether we are going to do it with or without 
cloture.
```

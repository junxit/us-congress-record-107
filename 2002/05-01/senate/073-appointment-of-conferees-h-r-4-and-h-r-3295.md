```
             APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES--H.R. 4 AND H.R. 3295

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Chair 
appoint conferees on behalf of the Senate for H.R. 4
  There being no objection, the Presiding Officer appointed Senators

  There being no objection, the Presiding Officer appointed Mr. Dodd,

part of the Senate.
```

# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 2, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3009

```
                    ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 2, 2002

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Thursday, May 2; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and there be a period of morning business until 10 a.m., with the 
time under the control of Senator Daschle or his designee; and that at 
10 a.m. the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 3009, the Andean Trade 
Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

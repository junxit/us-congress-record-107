# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Holt, Rush (H001032) [D-NJ]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. HOLT. Mr. Speaker, on Thursday April 26, 2002, I was unavoidably 
detained in my congressional district in New Jersey, attending a 
memorial service for a close friend and former co-worker. Because of 
that, I missed record votes in the House. Had I been present, Mr. 
Speaker, I would have voted yes on rollcall 111, yes on rollcall 112, 
yes on rollcall 113, no on rollcall 114, no on rollcall 115, and yes on
```

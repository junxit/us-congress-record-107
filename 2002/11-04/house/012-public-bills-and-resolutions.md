# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII,

        Mr. NADLER (for himself, Ms. Norton, Mr. Serrano, Mrs. 
     McCarthy of New York, Mr. Meeks of New York, and Mr. Owens) 
     introduced A bill (H.R. 5704) to provide that Community 
     Development Block Grant funds relating to the recovery of New 
     York City from the September 11, 2001, terrorist attacks 
     shall not be subject to Federal taxation; which was referred
```

# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4598, HOMELAND SECURITY INFORMATION SHARING ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 458, H.R. 4598

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4598, HOMELAND 
                    SECURITY INFORMATION SHARING ACT

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 107-535) on the resolution (H. Res. 458) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 4598) to provide for the sharing of 
homeland security information by Federal intelligence and law 
enforcement agencies with State and local entities, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```

# REPORT ON H.R. 5011, MILITARY CONSTRUCTION APPROPRIATIONS ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2003

```
   REPORT ON H.R. 5011, MILITARY CONSTRUCTION APPROPRIATIONS ACT FOR 
                            FISCAL YEAR 2003

  Mr. HOBSON, from the Committee on Appropriations, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 107-533) on the bill (H.R. 5011) making 
appropriations for military construction, family housing, and base

year ending September 30, 2003, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the Union Calendar and ordered to be printed.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause I, rule XXI, all points 
of order are reserved on the bill.
```

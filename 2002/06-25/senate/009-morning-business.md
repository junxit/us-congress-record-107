# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to a period of morning business not to extend 
beyond the hour of 10:30, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 
minutes each, with the time under the control of the majority leader or 
his designee.
```

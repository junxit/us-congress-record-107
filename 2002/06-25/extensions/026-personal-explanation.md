# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Becerra, Xavier (B000287) [D-CA]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. XAVIER BECERRA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 25, 2002

  Mr. BECERRA. Mr. Speaker, on Monday, June 24, 2002, I was unable to 
cast my floor vote on rollcall Nos. 249, 250, 251, and 252. The votes I 
missed include rollcall vote 249, on the Motion to Suspend the Rules 
and Pass, As Amended, H.R. 3937; rollcall vote 250, on the Motion to 
Suspend the Rules and Pass, As Amended, H.R. 3786; rollcall vote 251, 
on the Motion to Suspend the Rules and Pass, H.R. 3971; and rollcall 
vote 252, on the Motion to Suspend the Rules and Pass, As Amended, H. 
J. Res. 95.
  Had I been present for the votes, I would have voted ``aye'' on 
rollcall votes 249, 250, 251, and 252.
```

  Rollcall No. 250, H.R. 3786--To revise the boundary of the Glen 
Canyon National Recreation Area in the States of Utah and Arizona, 
``yes.''
  Rollcall No. 251, H.R. 3971--To provide for an independent 
investigation of Forest Service firefighter deaths that are caused by 
wildfire entrapment, or burnover, ``yes.''
  Rollcall No. 252, H.J. Res. 95--To designate an official flag of the 
Medal of Honor and providing for presentation of that flag to each 
recipient of that Medal of Honor ``yes.''

# THOUGHTS AND PRAYERS FOR THE SENATE CHAPLAIN

```
              THOUGHTS AND PRAYERS FOR THE SENATE CHAPLAIN

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, our good Chaplain, Dr. Ogilvie, each day 
comes before the Senate and the Nation and prays for our comfort and 
progress, as he does for other nations.
  I think it is important for all of us to realize he is going through 
a very traumatic time himself. He has, for many months, been doing 
everything he can to help his very ill wife. She got out of intensive 
care, and she is back in intensive care now. Each of us in our 
individual thoughts and prayers should keep that in mind.

                          ____________________
```

# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on July 
10, 2002, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:
       STUART D. RICK, OF MARYLAND, TO BE A MEMBER OF THE MERIT 
     SYSTEMS PROTECTION BOARD FOR THE TERM OF SEVEN YEARS EXPIRING 
     MARCH 1, 2007, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON APRIL 9, 2002.
```

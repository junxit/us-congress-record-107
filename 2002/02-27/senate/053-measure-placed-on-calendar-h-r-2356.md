# MEASURE PLACED ON CALENDAR--H.R. 2356

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2356

```
                 MEASURE PLACED ON CALENDAR--H.R. 2356

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand H.R. 2356 is at the desk and is 
due for its second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The senior assistant bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2356) to amend the Federal Election Campaign
```

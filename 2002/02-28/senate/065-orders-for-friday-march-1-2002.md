# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MARCH 1, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MARCH 1, 2002

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:15 
a.m. on Friday, March 1; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the election reform 
bill, with the time until 9:45 a.m. equally divided between Senators 
Dodd and McConnell or their designees; further, that the Senate vote on 
cloture at 9:45 a.m., and that Senators have until 9:30 a.m. tomorrow 
to file second-degree amendments.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

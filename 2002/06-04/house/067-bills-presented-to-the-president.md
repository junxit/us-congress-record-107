# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 3167, H.R. 4592, H.R. 4608, H.R. 4782

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jeff Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on May 29, 2002 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills.

       H.R. 3167. To endorse the vision of further enlargement of 
     the NATO Alliance articulated by President George W. Bush on 
     June 15, 2001, and by former President William J. Clinton on 
     October 22, 1996, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 4592. To name the chapel located in the national 
     cemetary in Los Angeles, California, as the ``Bob Hope 
     Veterans Chapel''.
       H.R. 4608. To name the Department of Veterans Affairs 
     Medical and Regional Office Center in Wichita, Kansas, as the 
     ``Robert J. Dole Department of Veterans Affairs Medical and 
     Regional Office Center''.
       H.R. 4782. To extend the authority of the Export-Import 
     Bank until June 14, 2002.

                          ____________________
```

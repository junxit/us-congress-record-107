# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, we are going to be working today, tomorrow, 
and the next day on the Supplemental Appropriations Act. We hope to 
enter into agreements, one of which would require that all first-degree

about this. I understand he and Senator Stevens are in agreement that
```

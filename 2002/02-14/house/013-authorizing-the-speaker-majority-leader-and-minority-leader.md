# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER, MAJORITY LEADER, AND MINORITY LEADER TO ACCEPT RESIGNATIONS AND TO MAKE APPOINTMENTS AUTHORIZED BY LAW OR BY THE HOUSE, NOTWITHSTANDING ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Foley, Mark (F000238) [R-FL]

```
AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER, MAJORITY LEADER, AND MINORITY LEADER TO ACCEPT 
   RESIGNATIONS AND TO MAKE APPOINTMENTS AUTHORIZED BY LAW OR BY THE 
                   HOUSE, NOTWITHSTANDING ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. FOLEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that notwithstanding 
any adjournment of the House until Tuesday, February 26, 2002, the 
Speaker, majority leader, and minority leader be authorized to accept 
resignations, to make appointments authorized by law or by the House.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.
```

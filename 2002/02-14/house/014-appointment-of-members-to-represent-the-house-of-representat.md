# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO REPRESENT THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AT APPROPRIATE CEREMONIES FOR THE OBSERVANCE OF GEORGE WASHINGTON'S BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Foley, Mark (F000238) [R-FL]

```
  APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO REPRESENT THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AT 
   APPROPRIATE CEREMONIES FOR THE OBSERVANCE OF GEORGE WASHINGTON'S 
                                BIRTHDAY

  Mr. FOLEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it shall be in 
order for the Speaker to appoint two Members of the House, one upon the 
recommendation of the minority leader, to represent the House of 
Representatives at appropriate ceremonies for the observance of George 
Washington's birthday to be held on Friday, February 22, 2002.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

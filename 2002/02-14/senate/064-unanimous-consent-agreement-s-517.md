# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 517

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 517

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 517

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask consent that the majority leader, 
after consultation with the Republican leader, may at any time turn to 
consideration of Calendar No. 65, S. 517, a bill to authorize funding 
for the Department of Energy to enhance its mission areas through 
technology transfer and partnerships.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

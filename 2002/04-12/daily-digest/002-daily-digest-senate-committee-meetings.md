# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
BORDER SECURITY
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Immigration concluded hearings 
to examine the Enhanced Border Security and Visa Entry Reform Act, focusing 
on strengthening border security, improving the screening of foreign 
nationals, and deterring potential terrorists, after receiving testimony 
from Senator Byrd; MaryEllen Salamone, Families of September 11, Inc., North 
Caldwell, New Jersey; and Kathleen Campbell Walker, Kemp Smith, PC, El Paso, 
Texas, on behalf of the American Immigration Lawyers Association.
```

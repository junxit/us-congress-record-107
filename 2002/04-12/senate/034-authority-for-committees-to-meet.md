# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      Subcommittee on Immigration

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on the Judiciary subcommittee on Immigration be authorized to meet to 
conduct a hearing on ``The Enhanced border Security and Visa Entry 
Reform Act'' on Friday, April 12, 2002, at 9 a.m., in Dirksen 226.

     Witness List

  Panel I: Ms. MaryEllen Salamone, Director, Families of September 11, 
North Caldwell NJ, and Ms. Kathleen Campbell Walker, American 
Immigration Lawyers Association and Senior Shareholder and Chair of the 
Immigration Department, Kemp Smith, PC, El Paso, TX.
  Panel II: The Honorable Robert C. Byrd.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```

# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Kilpatrick, Carolyn C. (K000180) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.Res. 468, H.R. 3340, H.R. 5531

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Ms. KILPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, a flight delay prevented me from being 
present for legislative business scheduled for today, Monday, October 
7, 2002. Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on the 
following rollcall votes: H.R. 3340, to allow certain catch-up 
contributions to the Thrift Savings Plan to be made by participants age 
50 or over (rollcall No. 442); H.R. 5531, The Sudan Peace Act (rollcall 
No. 443); and H. Res. 468, The Transatlantic Security and NATO 
Enhancement Resolution of 2002 (rollcall No. 444).
```

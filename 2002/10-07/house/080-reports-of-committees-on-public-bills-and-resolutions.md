     the Union.

     State of the Union.
       Mr. SENSENBRENNER: Committee on the Judiciary. H.R. 5422. A 
     bill to prevent child abduction, and for other purposes;

       Mr. TAUZIN: Committee on Energy and Commerce. H.R. 5504. A

       Mr. TAUZIN: Committee on Energy and Commerce. H.R. 2037. A

     foreign commerce; with an amendment (Rept. 107-727 Pt. 1).

       Mr. TAUZIN: Committee on Energy and Commerce. H.R. 3580. A

     the Union.

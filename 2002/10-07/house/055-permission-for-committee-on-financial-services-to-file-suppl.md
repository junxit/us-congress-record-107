**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike J. (R000572) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 5400

```
  PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON FINANCIAL SERVICES TO FILE SUPPLEMENTAL 
    REPORT ON H.R. 5400, AGREEMENT BETWEEN UNITED STATES AND MEXICO 
 CONCERNING ESTABLISHMENT OF BORDER ENVIRONMENT COOPERATION COMMISSION
```

# ORDER OF BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           ORDER OF BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, there was a unanimous consent request 
earlier today, which has been approved, that the time from when we come 
in at 10 o'clock tomorrow to begin work on this resolution until 12:30 
be in 15-minute slots, and we would be happy to alternate back and 
forth. But it would be to everybody's advantage if those wishing to 
speak would notify their respective cloakrooms. What I will do in the 
morning, when we come in at 9 o'clock, is set that up so people will 
know when to come. We would set up an order of procedure for debate in 
this matter. I think that would save Senators a lot of time, and it 
would allow us to move along in the matter more quickly.

                          ____________________
```

# A PROCLAMATION HONORING WILLIAM JAKE OLSAVSKY

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
             A PROCLAMATION HONORING WILLIAM JAKE OLSAVSKY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 7, 2002

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, Whereas, William J. Olsavsky, known simply as 
Coach ``O'', was an accomplished football player and played as a 
Wheeling Ironman from 1962-1969; and
  Whereas, Coach ``O'' is an example to all who know him, especially 
his students, of steadfast character and loyal friendship; and
  Whereas Coach ``O'' is to be commended for his hard work and 
dedication to the Students of Wheeling Central High, Brilliant High, 
Bellaire High, and Union Local High School where he served as Head 
Football Coach from 1963-2002; and
  Whereas Coach ``O'' has received numerous awards and accolades 
testifying to his character, passion, dedication, and talent; and
  Therefore, I join with the residents of the entire 18th Congressional 
District in congratulating William J. Olsavsky on his retirement after 
40 years of brilliant service in education and coaching.

                          ____________________
```

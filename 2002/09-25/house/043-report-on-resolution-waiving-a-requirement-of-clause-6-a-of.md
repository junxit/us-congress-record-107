# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING A REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 6(a) OF RULE XIII WITH RESPECT TO SAME DAY CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 3295

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING A REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 6(a) OF RULE XIII 
     WITH RESPECT TO SAME DAY CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington (during consideration of the Motion to 
Instruct on H.R. 3295), from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 107-695) waiving a requirement of clause 
6(a) of rule XIII with respect to consideration of certain resolutions 
reported from the Committee on Rules, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```

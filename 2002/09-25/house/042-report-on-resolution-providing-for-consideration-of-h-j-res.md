# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.J. RES. 111, CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS, FISCAL YEAR 2003

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 111, H.Res. 550, H.R. 3295

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.J. RES. 111, 
              CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS, FISCAL YEAR 2003

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington (during consideration of the Motion to 
Instruct on H.R. 3295), from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 107-694) on the resolution (H. Res. 550) 
providing for consideration of the joint resolution (H.J.Res. 111)

other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
```

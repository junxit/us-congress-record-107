# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING GERALD BENSON

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
                A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING GERALD BENSON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2002

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, whereas, Gerald Benson became a citizen of the 
United States of America 25 years ago on November 8, 1977; and
  Whereas, Gerald Benson showed his responsible citizenship by also 
voting on the same November 8, 1977; and
  Whereas, Gerald Benson has demonstrated a commitment to his community 
of Cambridge, Ohio; and
  Whereas, Gerald Benson has worked hard to become an American success 
story;
  Therefore, I join with the residents of the entire 18th Congressional 
District of Ohio in congratulating Gerald Benson on the 25th 
Anniversary of his being a United States of America Citizen.

                          ____________________
```

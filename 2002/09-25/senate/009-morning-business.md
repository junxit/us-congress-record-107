# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Dayton, Mark (D000596) [D-MN]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 10:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each, and the time to be equally divided 
between the two leaders or their designees.
  The Senator from Minnesota.
  Mr. DAYTON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that I be 
permitted 15 minutes, if necessary, to complete my remarks.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```

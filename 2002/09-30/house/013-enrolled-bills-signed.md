# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 111, H.R. 1646

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Mr. Trandahl, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly a joint 
resolution of the House of the following title, which was thereupon 
signed by Speaker pro tempore James Hansen on September 27, 2002.

       H.J. Res. 111. Joint resolution making continuing 
     appropriations for the fiscal year 2003, and for other 
     purposes.

  On September 30, 2002, Speaker pro tempore Mac Thornberry signed the 
following bill.

       H.R. 1646. An act to authorize appropriations for the 
     Department of State for fiscal year 2003, to authorize 
     appropriations under the Arms Export Control Act and the 
     Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 for security assistance for 
     fiscal year 2003, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```

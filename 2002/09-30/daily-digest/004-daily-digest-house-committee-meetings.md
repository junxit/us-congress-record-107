# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
OVERSIGHT--COMMERCIAL ACTIVITIES PANEL FINDINGS
Committee on Government Reform: On September 27, the Subcommittee on 
Technology and Procurement Policy held a hearing titled ``An Oversight 
hearing to Review the Findings of the Commercial Activities Panel.'' 
Testimony was heard from David M. Walker, Comptroller General, GAO; 
Angela Styles, Director, Office of Federal Procurement Policy, OMB; 
Joseph Sikes, Director, Competitive Sourcing and Privatization, 
Department of Defense; and public witnesses.
```

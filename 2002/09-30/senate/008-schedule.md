# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, originally we had announced there would be a 
vote this afternoon, but there will not be a vote today. The first vote 
will be approximately 12 p.m. on Tuesday on cloture on the Gramm-Miller 
amendment on homeland security.
  I ask unanimous consent notwithstanding rule XXII, first degree 
amendments may be filed until 3 p.m. today and the live quorum with 
respect to the cloture motion filed be waived; further, the cloture 
vote on the Gramm-Miller amendment No. 4738 occur at 12 p.m. tomorrow, 
without further intervening action or debate.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```

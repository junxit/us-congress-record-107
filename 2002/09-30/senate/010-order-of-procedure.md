# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, Senator Landrieu is in the Chamber to report 
to the Senate on the devastation of the hurricane that struck her 
State. I ask unanimous consent she have the full 30 minutes, which 
would extend the time to 1:35 and then the minority have their full 30 
minutes.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there objection? Without 
objection, it is so ordered.
```

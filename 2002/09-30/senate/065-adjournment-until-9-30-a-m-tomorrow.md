# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M., TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Res. 331

```
                 ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M., TOMORROW

  Mr. REID. If there is no further business to come before the Senate, 
I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in adjournment under the 
parameters of S. Res. 331, as a further mark of respect to the memory 
of the deceased Patsy Mink.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 6:16 p.m., adjourned until 
Tuesday, October 1, 2002, at 9:30 a.m.
```

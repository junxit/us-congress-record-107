# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Nelson, Ben (N000180) [D-NE]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Madam President, it is my understanding we are in a period 
of morning business; is that correct?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The time for morning business was to have 
expired at 5:15.
  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the time be 
extended until 6:45 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. NELSON of Nebraska. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent the 
order for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

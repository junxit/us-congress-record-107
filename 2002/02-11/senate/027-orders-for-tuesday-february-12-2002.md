# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 12, 2002

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 12, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Tuesday, February 12; following the prayer and the pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 1731, the 
farm bill; further, that the Senate recess from 12:30 to 2:15 p.m. for 
the weekly party conferences.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

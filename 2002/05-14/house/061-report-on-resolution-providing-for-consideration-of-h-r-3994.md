# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3994, AFGHANISTAN FREEDOM SUPPORT ACT OF 2002

**Measures:** H.Res. 419, H.R. 3994

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3994, 
                AFGHANISTAN FREEDOM SUPPORT ACT OF 2002

  Mr. DREIER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-463) on the resolution (H. Res. 419) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3994) to authorize economic and 
democratic development assistance for Afghanistan and to authorize 
military assistance for Afghanistan and certain other foreign 
countries, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```

# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 10:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
for up to 10 minutes each. Under the previous order, the time until 10 
a.m. shall be under the control of the Republican leader or his 
designee.
  The Senator from Wyoming.

                          ____________________
```

# ANNOUNCEMENT OF INTENTION TO OFFER A MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 4546, BOB STUMP NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2003

```
 ANNOUNCEMENT OF INTENTION TO OFFER A MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON 
H.R. 4546, BOB STUMP NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 
                                  2003

  Mr. TAYLOR of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to clause 7(c)(1) of 
rule XXII, I hereby notify the House of my intention to offer a motion 
to instruct conferees on the national defense authorization bill which 
has been in conference since July 26, 2002.
  The form of the motion is as follows:

       Mr. Taylor of Mississippi moves that the managers on the 
     part of the House at the conference on the disagreeing votes 
     of the two Houses on the House amendment to the Senate 
     amendment to the bill H.R. 4546 be instructed to agree to the 
     provisions contained in section 641 of the Senate amendment 
     (relating to payment of retired pay and compensation to 
     disabled military retirees).

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 10, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 45

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 10, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 9:15 a.m., 
Thursday, October 10; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceeding be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of S.J. Res. 45, 
under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 21, 2002

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 21, 2002

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:45 a.m., Thursday, March 
21; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of the energy reform bill.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```

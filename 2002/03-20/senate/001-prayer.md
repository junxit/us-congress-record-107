# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Lloyd John Ogilvie, offered the following prayer:
  Lord God of truth, who calls us to absolute honesty in everything we 
say, we renew our commitment to truth. In a time in which people no 
longer expect to hear the truth, or what's worse, don't see the need 
consistently to speak it, make us straight arrows who hit the target of 
absolute honesty. Help us to be people on whom others always can depend 
for unswerving integrity.
  May the reliability of our words earn us the right to give righteous 
leadership. Thank You for the wonderful freedom that comes from a 
consistency between what we promise and what we do. You are present 
where truth is spoken. Thank You for reigning supreme in this Senate 
Chamber today. In the name of our Lord and Saviour. Amen.

                          ____________________
```

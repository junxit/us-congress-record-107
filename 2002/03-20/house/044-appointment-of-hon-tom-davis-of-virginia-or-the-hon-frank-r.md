# APPOINTMENT OF HON. TOM DAVIS OF VIRGINIA OR THE HON. FRANK R. WOLF TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH APRIL 9, 2002

```
APPOINTMENT OF HON. TOM DAVIS OF VIRGINIA OR THE HON. FRANK R. WOLF TO 
ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS 
                         THROUGH APRIL 9, 2002

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                   March 20, 2002.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Tom Davis or, if not 
     available to perform this duty, the Honorable Frank R. Wolf 
     to act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and 
     joint resolutions through April 9, 2002.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                           Speaker of the House of Representatives

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

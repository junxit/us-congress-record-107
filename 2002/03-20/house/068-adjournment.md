# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Davis, Jim (D000114) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 360

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. DAVIS of Florida. Madam Speaker, pursuant to House Concurrent 
Resolution 360, 107th Congress, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Accordingly, pursuant to the previous order 
of the House of today, the House stands adjourned until 2 p.m. on 
Friday, March 22, 2002, unless it sooner has received a message from 
the Senate transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 
360, in which case the House shall stand adjourned until 2 p.m. on 
Tuesday, April 9, 2002, pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  Thereupon (at 9 o'clock and 35 minutes p.m.), the House adjourned 
until 2 p.m. on Tuesday, April 9, 2002, pursuant to House Concurrent 
Resolution 360, or under the previous order of the House if not sooner 
in receipt of a message from the Senate transmitting its concurrence in 
House Concurrent Resolution 360.

                          ____________________
```

# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The hour of 12:55 p.m. having arrived, the 
Senate stands in recess until the hour of 2:15 p.m. today.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:55 p.m., recessed until 2:15 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mrs. 
Carnahan).
```

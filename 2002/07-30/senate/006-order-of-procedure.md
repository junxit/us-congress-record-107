# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 812

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the time from 
10:40 a.m. until 11:10 a.m. be under the control of Senator Byrd; that 
the next 35 minutes be under the Republicans' control for morning 
business; that the Senate resume consideration of S. 812 at 11:45 a.m., 
with the time until 12:45 equally divided between Senators Kennedy and 
McConnell or their designees; and that the previously ordered recess 
begin at 12:45 p.m. instead of 12:30 p.m.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. REID. Madam President, there are two cloture motions that were 
filed last evening--first on the Dorgan amendment and second on the 
generic drug bill. Therefore, Senators have until 12:45 p.m. today to 
file first-degree amendments.

                          ____________________
```

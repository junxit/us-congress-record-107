# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       52. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Legislature of 
     Franklin County, relative to Resolution No. 317 petitioning 
     the Congress of the United States to pass without delay and 
     for the President of the United States to sign into law ``The 
     State Budget Relief Act of 2001''; to the Committee on Energy 
     and Commerce.
       53. Also,a petition of the City Council of the City of 
     Groves, Texas, relative to Resolution 2000-17 petitioning the 
     Congress of the United States in expressing an collective 
     desire to rid the world of terrorism of global reach; jointly 
     to the Committees on International Relations and Armed 
     Services.
```

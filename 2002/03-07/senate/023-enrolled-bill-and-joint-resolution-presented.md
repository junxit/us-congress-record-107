# ENROLLED BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 32, S. 1857

```
              ENROLLED BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, March 7, 2002, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bill and joint resolution:

       S. 1857. An act to encourage the negotiated settlement of 
     tribal claims.
       S.J. Res. 32. A joint resolution congratulating the United 
     States Military Academy at West Point on its bicentennial 
     anniversary, and commending its outstanding contributions to 
     the Nation.

                          ____________________
```

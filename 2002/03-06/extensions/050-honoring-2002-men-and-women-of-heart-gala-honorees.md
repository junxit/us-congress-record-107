# HONORING 2002 MEN AND WOMEN OF HEART GALA HONOREES

**Speaking:** Lampson, Nick (L000043) [D-TX]

```
           HONORING 2002 MEN AND WOMEN OF HEART GALA HONOREES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. NICK LAMPSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 6, 2002

  Mr. LAMPSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 2002 Men and 
Women of Heart Gala honorees. The honorees have dedicated themselves to 
their communities and to service. I am proud to represent them here in 
Washington. The recipients are: Anita Fogtman, Colin Fox, Norman Frede, 
Kathleen Harlan, Bette Johnson, Bill Lowes, Victor Maria, Floyd H. 
Myers, Charlotte Tetter, and Paula Orcutt Thomas.
  These citizens were presented this award for their committed and 
caring service to their fellow Texans, and have helped make their 
communities better places for all.

                          ____________________
```

# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on March 
06, 2002, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       Frederick R. Heebe, of Louisiana, to be United States 
     Attorney for the Eastern District of Louisiana for the term 
     of four years, which was sent to the Senate on November 1, 
     2001.
```

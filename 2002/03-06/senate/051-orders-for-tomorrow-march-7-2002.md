# ORDERS FOR TOMORROW, MARCH 7, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 517

```
                   ORDERS FOR TOMORROW, MARCH 7, 2002

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 
a.m., Thursday, March 7; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 517, the energy 
bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

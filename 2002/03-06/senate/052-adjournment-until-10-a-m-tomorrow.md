# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Res. 217

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. REID. Madam President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent the Senate stand in 
adjournment under the provisions of S. Res. 217, as a mark of respect 
to the memory of the deceased Honorable Howard W. Cannon, a Senator 
from the State of Nevada.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 6:42 p.m., adjourned until 
Thursday, March 7, 2002, at 10 a.m.

                          ____________________
```

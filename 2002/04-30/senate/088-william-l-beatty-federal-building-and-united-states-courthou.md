# WILLIAM L. BEATTY FEDERAL BUILDING AND UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 3093

```
    WILLIAM L. BEATTY FEDERAL BUILDING AND UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

  The bill (H.R. 3093) to designate the Federal building and United 
States courthouse located at 501 Bell Street in Alton, Illinois, as the 
``William L. Beatty Federal Building and United States Courthouse,'' 
was considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```

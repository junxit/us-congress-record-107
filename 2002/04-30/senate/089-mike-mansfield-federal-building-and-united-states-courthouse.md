# MIKE MANSFIELD FEDERAL BUILDING AND UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 3282

```
      MIKE MANSFIELD FEDERAL BUILDING AND UNITED STATES COURTHOUSE

  The bill (H.R. 3282) to designate the Federal building and United 
States courthouse located at 400 North Main Street in Butte, Montana, 
as the ``Mike Mansfield Federal Building and United States 
Courthouse,'' was considered, ordered to a third reading, read the 
third time, and passed.
```

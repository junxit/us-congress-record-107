# ANNOUNCEMENT OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT ON H.R. 2646, FARM SECURITY ACT OF 2001

**Speaking:** Ackerman, Gary L. (A000022) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2646

```
  ANNOUNCEMENT OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT ON H.R. 2646, 
                       FARM SECURITY ACT OF 2001

  Mr. ACKERMAN. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to clause 7(c) of rule XXII, I 
hereby announce my intention to offer a motion to instruct conferees on 
H.R. 2646.
  The form of the motion is as follows:

       I move that the managers on the part of the House at the 
     conference on the disagreeing votes of the 2 Houses on the 
     Senate amendment to the bill H.R. 2646 be instructed to 
     insist on the provisions contained in section 945 of the 
     House bill relating to unlawful stockyard practices involving 
     nonambulatory livestock.

                          ____________________
```

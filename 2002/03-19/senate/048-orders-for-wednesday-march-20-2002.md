# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 20, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 20, 2002

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 a.m. tomorrow, 
Wednesday, March 20. I further ask consent that on Wednesday, 
immediately following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate proceed under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

adjustments to the boundary of the National Park of American Samoa to 
include certain portions of the islands of Ofu and Olosega within the park, 
and for other purposes.'';

Res. 368, commending the great work that the Pentagon Renovation Program and 
its contractors have completed thus far, in reconstructing the portion of

2001 (agreed to by a yea-and-nay vote of 413 yeas with none voting ``nay'', 
Roll No. 66);
  Pages H949-52, H973

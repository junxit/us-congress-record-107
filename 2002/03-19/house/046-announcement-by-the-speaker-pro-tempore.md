# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 339, H.Res. 368, H.R. 2509, H.R. 2804

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair 
will now put the question on approval of the Journal and on motions to 
suspend the rules on which further proceedings were postponed earlier 
today.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  The Journal, de novo;
  H. Res. 368, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 2509, by the yeas and nays; and
  H.R. 2804, by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.
  The vote on H. Res. 339 will be postponed until tomorrow.
```

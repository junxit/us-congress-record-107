# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5011, MILITARY CONSTRUCTION APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2003

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5011, MILITARY 
                 CONSTRUCTION APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2003

  Mrs. MYRICK, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-537) on the resolution (H. Res. 462) providing

military construction, family housing, and base realignment and closure

2003, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar
```

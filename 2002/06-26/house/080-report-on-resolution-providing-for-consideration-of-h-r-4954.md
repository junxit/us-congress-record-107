# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4954, MEDICARE MODERNIZATION AND PRESCRIPTION DRUG ACT OF 2002

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4954, MEDICARE 
            MODERNIZATION AND PRESCRIPTION DRUG ACT OF 2002

  Mr. DREIER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-553) on the resolution (H. Res. 465) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4954) to amend title XVIII of the 
Social Security Act to provide for a voluntary program for prescription 
drug coverage under the Medicare Program, to modernize and reform 
payments and the regulatory structure of the Medicare Program, and for 
other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
```

# RECITATION OF THE PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                 RECITATION OF THE PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, Senators are encouraged by both the majority 
leader and the Republican leader to be in the Senate Chamber promptly 
at 9:30 following the prayer that will be given by the Chaplain. They 
will recite the Pledge of Allegiance, based upon what occurred in the 
Ninth Circuit today, which has been a disappointment to the entire 
Senate.
```

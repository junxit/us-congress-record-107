# JUSTIN DART--AN INSPIRATION TO US ALL

**Speaking:** Wellstone, Paul D. (W000288) [D-MN]

```
                 JUSTIN DART--AN INSPIRATION TO US ALL

  Mr. WELLSTONE. Mr. President, I thank Senator Harkin for last night 
coming to the floor and talking about Justin Dart, probably one of the 
greatest 10 individuals I have met in my life, for what he has done for 
people with disabilities. His courage and leadership was quite 
unbelievable. He has inspired many of us.
  I send my love from the Senate floor to his family.
```

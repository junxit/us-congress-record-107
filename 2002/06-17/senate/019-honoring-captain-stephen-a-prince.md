# HONORING CAPTAIN STEPHEN A. PRINCE

**Speaking:** Hollings, Ernest F. (H000725) [D-SC]

```
                   HONORING CAPTAIN STEPHEN A. PRINCE

● Mr. HOLLINGS. Mr. President, I want to pay tribute to a native 
of Greenville, SC, Captain Stephen A. Price. He will soon be retiring 
after a distinguished 26-year career in the Navy, most recently as the 
division chief at the Defense Logistics Agency's Business Development 
and Supply Chain Integration, Fort Belvoir, VA.
  Captain Price has served in a number of challenging positions. At 
sea, he participated in the highly successful maiden deployment of the 
USS John C. Stennis to the Arabian Gulf. He also served as an officer 
on the USS Ashtabula and the USS John L. Hall. His shore assignments, 
in the Area of Supply, have taken him to four States and to Iceland. 
His personal awards include three Meritorious Service Medals; and from 
the Navy and Marine Corps, two Commendation and two Achievement Medals.
  We all appreciate Captain Prince's service to our Nation. I wish him, 
his wife Linda, and their two daughters, the very best; and I hope they 
have more opportunities to return home to South Carolina to visit 
Captain Prince's family currently residing in Myrtle Beach.●

                          ____________________
```

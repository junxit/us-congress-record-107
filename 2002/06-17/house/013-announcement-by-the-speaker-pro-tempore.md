# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair 
will postpone further proceedings today on each motion to suspend the 
rules on which a recorded vote or the yeas and nays are ordered or on 
which the vote is objected to under clause 6 of rule XX.
  Any record vote on postponed questions will be taken after debate has 
concluded on all motions to suspend the rules but not before 6:30 p.m. 
today.
```

# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 3937

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3937

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 3937

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 3937 has been read 
for the first time and is now awaiting its second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct.
  Mr. REID. I ask then, Mr. President, that H.R. 3937 be read for a 
second time, but I object to any further proceedings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the second 
time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3937) to revoke a Public Land Order with 
     respect to certain lands erroneously included in the Cibola 
     National Wildlife Refuge, California.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill will be 
placed on the calendar.
```

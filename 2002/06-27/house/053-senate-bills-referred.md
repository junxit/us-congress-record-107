# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 1041. An act to establish a program for an information 
     clearinghouse to increase public access to defibrillation in 
     schools; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce, in addition 
     to the Committee on Education and the Workforce for a period 
     to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case 
     for consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
       S. 1646. An act to identify certain routes in the States of 
     Texas, Oklahoma, Colorado, and New Mexico as part of the 
     Ports-to-Plains Corridor, a high priority corridor on the 
     National Highway System; to the Committee on Transportation 
     and Infrastructure.
       S. 2690. An act to reaffirm the reference to one Nation 
     under God in the Pledge of Allegiance; to the Committee on 
     the Judiciary.
```

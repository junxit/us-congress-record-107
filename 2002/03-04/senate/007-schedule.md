# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, the Chair will shortly announce we will be 
in a period of morning business until 6 p.m. tonight, with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each. It is my understanding

certainly appropriate.

bill with 15 minutes of debate prior to the 6:15 rollcall vote on

today to file second-degree amendments to the election reform bill.
```

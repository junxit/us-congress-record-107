# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 1206

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2001, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on March 4, 2002, during the recess of the 
Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives announcing 
that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 1206. An act to reauthorize the Appalachian Regional 
     Development Act of 1965, and for other purposes.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2001, 
the enrolled bill was signed today, March 4, 2002, by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Byrd).
```

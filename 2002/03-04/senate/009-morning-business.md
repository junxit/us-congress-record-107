# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 6 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for 
up to 10 minutes each, with the time to be equally divided between the 
two leaders or their designees.
  The Senator from Arizona.
  Mr. KYL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent to speak for 30 
minutes in morning business.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```

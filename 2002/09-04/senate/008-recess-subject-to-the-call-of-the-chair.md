# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

**Speaking:** Byrd, Robert C. (B001210) [D-WV]

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, with the approval of my colleague, Senator 
Conrad Burns, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess 
awaiting the return of the majority leader and/or the minority leader--
the return of those two leaders--and/or the whips on both sides.
  There being no objection, at 10:11 a.m. the Senate recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair and reassembled at 11:39 a.m. when called to 
order by the Presiding Officer (Mrs. Clinton).

                          ____________________
```

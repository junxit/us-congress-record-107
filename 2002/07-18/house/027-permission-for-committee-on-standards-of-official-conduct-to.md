PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON STANDARDS OF OFFICIAL CONDUCT TO HAVE UNTIL 
  MIDNIGHT, FRIDAY, JULY 19, 2002, TO FILE PRIVILEGED RESOLUTION AND 
                                 REPORT

# Text of House Amendment 20

**Speaking:** Flake, Jeff (F000444) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 5120

```
H.R. 5120

                         Offered By: Mr. Flake

       Amendment No. 20: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to enforce any restriction on remittances to 
     nationals of Cuba covered by section 515.570(a)(1)(i), 
     (a)(2), (b)(1)(i), or (b)(2) of title 31, Code of Federal 
     Regulations.
```

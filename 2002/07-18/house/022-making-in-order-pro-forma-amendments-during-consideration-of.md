```
MAKING IN ORDER PRO FORMA AMENDMENTS DURING CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5121, 
              LEGISLATIVE BRANCH APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2003

  Mr. DIAZ-BALART. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during the
```

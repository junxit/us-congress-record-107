# UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--H.R. 1606

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 1606

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--H.R. 1606

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Energy 
Committee be discharged from further consideration of H.R. 1606, and 
that the Senate proceed to its immediate consideration, the bill be 
read three times and passed, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon 
the table, without any intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I have to object on behalf of the 
Republicans.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```

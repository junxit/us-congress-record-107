# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 17, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 17, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 11 a.m., 
Thursday, October 17; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed to have expired, the Journal of the proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and that there be a period of morning business, 
with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, 
with the time until 12 noon under the control of the Republican leader 
or his designee, and the time from 12 noon to 1 p.m. under the control 
of Senator Daschle or his designee.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

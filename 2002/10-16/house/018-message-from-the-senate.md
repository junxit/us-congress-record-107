# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 3295

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate agrees to the report of the committee of 
conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendment 
of the Senate to the text of the bill (H.R. 3295) ``An Act to establish 
a program to provide funds to States to replace punch card voting 
systems, to establish the Election Assistance Commission to assist in 
the administration of Federal elections and to otherwise provide 
assistance with the administration of certain Federal election laws and 
programs, to establish minimum election administration standards for 
States and units of local government with responsibility for the 
administration of Federal elections, and for other purposes,'' and that 
the Senate recede from its amendment to the title of H.R. 3295.

                          ____________________
```

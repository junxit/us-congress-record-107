# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF VARIOUS LEGISLATIVE MEASURES

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

```
  AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF VARIOUS 
                          LEGISLATIVE MEASURES

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that, in the 
engrossment of the measures just passed, the Clerk be authorized to 
correct spelling, punctuation, numbering, and cross-references, and to 
make such other changes as may be necessary to reflect the actions of 
the House.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

# VOTE EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Wellstone, Paul D. (W000288) [D-MN]

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

  Mr. WELLSTONE. Mr. President, I regret that I was necessarily absent 
for the vote on the confirmation of Timothy Corrigan to the United 
States District Court in Florida due to my attending events in 
Minnesota commemorating the anniversary of the terrorist attacks of 
September 11. I would ask that the Record reflect that I would have 
voted yes on this nomination.
```

# APPOINTMENT OF THE HONORABLE CONSTANCE A. MORELLA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JUNE 4, 2002

```
APPOINTMENT OF THE HONORABLE CONSTANCE A. MORELLA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO 
 TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH JUNE 4, 
                                  2002

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                     May 23, 2002.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Constance A. Morella to act 
     as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through June 4, 2002.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

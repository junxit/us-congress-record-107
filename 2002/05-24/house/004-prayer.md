# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  O God, You have kept Your covenant with every generation. In a world 
shadowed by the many infidelities and many inconsistencies of frail 
humanity, grant us faith in Your enduring love and patience with us.
  Confirm the Members of the House of Representatives in Your power 
that they may accomplish the tasks You set before them.
  Since You have called them to serve this great Nation, grant them the 
gifts they need to discern Your holy will and accomplish deeds of 
justice and integrity, today and every day of their lives. Amen.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 a.m., 
Tuesday, February 5; that following the prayer and pledge the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and that there be a period of morning business until 10:15 a.m., 
with Senators permitted to speak for up to 5 minutes each; further, 
that the Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. for the weekly 
party conferences.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

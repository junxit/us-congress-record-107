# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed 
to executive session at 10 a.m. tomorrow to consider Executive Calendar 
No. 646, the nomination of Philip Martinez to be United States District 
Judge; that there be 15 minutes equally divided between the chairman 
and ranking member of the Judiciary Committee for debate on the 
nomination; that at 10:30 a.m. tomorrow the Senate vote on the 
nomination, the motion to reconsider be laid on the table, any 
statements be printed in the Record, the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action, and thereafter the Senate return to 
legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, as in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent it be in order to request the yeas and nays on the nomination 
at this time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There is a sufficient second.
  The yeas and nays were ordered.

                          ____________________
```

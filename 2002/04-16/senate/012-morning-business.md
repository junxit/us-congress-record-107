# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period of morning business not to extend beyond the hour 
of 12:30 with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each, and with the time to be equally divided between the two leaders, 
or their designees.
  The Senator from Kentucky.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Thank you, Mr. President.
```

# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 476, CHILD CUSTODY PROTECTION ACT

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 476, CHILD 
                         CUSTODY PROTECTION ACT

  Mrs. MYRICK, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-411) on the resolution (H. Res. 388) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 476) to amend title 18, United 
States Code, to prohibit taking minors across State lines in 
circumvention of laws requiring the involvement of parents in abortion 
decisions, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```

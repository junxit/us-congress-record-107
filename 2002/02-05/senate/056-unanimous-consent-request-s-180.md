# UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--S. 180

```
                   UNANIMOUS CONSENT REQUEST--S. 180

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent the Chair lay 
before the Senate a message from the House on S. 180; that the Senate 
disagree to the House amendment, agree to the request for conference on 
the disagreeing votes of the two Houses, and that the Chair be 
authorized to appoint conferees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. NICKLES. Madam President, I object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
```

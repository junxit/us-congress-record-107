# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 10:15 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 5 minutes each.
  The Chair recognizes the Senator from Nevada.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the next 10 
minutes be equally divided between the minority and majority.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask the Chair to inform me when I have 
used the 5 minutes.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Chair will do so.
```

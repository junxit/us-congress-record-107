  Mr. ACKERMAN changed his vote from ``nay'' to ``yea.''
  So (two-thirds having voted in favor thereof), the rules were 
suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  The title was amended so as to read: ``A bill to amend title 44, 
United States Code, to require any organization that is established for 
the purpose of raising funds for creating, maintaining, expanding, or 
conducting activities at a Presidential archival depository or any 
facilities relating to a Presidential archival depository to disclose 
the sources and amounts of any funds raised, and for other purposes.''.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

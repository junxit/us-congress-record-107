# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3394, CYBER SECURITY RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 343, H.R. 3394

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3394, CYBER 
                 SECURITY RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT ACT

  Mr. DIAZ-BALART, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-357) on the resolution (H.Res. 343) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 3394) to authorize funding for computer

programs, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House
```

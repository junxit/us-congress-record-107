# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Otter). Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, 
the Chair announces that he will postpone further proceedings today on 
motions to suspend the rules on which a recorded vote or the yeas and 
nays are ordered, or on which the vote is objected to under clause 6 of 
rule XX.
  Any record votes on motions to suspend the rules ordered prior to 
6:30 p.m. will be taken today. Record votes on remaining motions to 
suspend the rules will be taken tomorrow.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 12, 2002

**Speaking:** Bingaman, Jeff (B000468) [D-NM]

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 12, 2002

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10:30 
a.m. on Tuesday, March 12; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time of the two leaders be reserved for their use later in 
the day, and the Senate begin consideration of Executive Calendar No. 
706, as under the previous order; further, that the Senate recess from 
12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. for the weekly party conferences.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  The Chair hears none, and it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

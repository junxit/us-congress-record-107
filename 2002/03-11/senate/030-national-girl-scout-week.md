# NATIONAL GIRL SCOUT WEEK

```
                        NATIONAL GIRL SCOUT WEEK

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate proceed to the immediate consideration of S. Res. 225, which was 
introduced earlier today.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 225) designating the week of March 10 
     through March 16, 2002, as National Girl Scout Week.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. BINGAMAN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
resolution and the preamble be agreed to, the motion to reconsider be 
laid on the table, and that any statements regarding this matter be 
printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 225) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
  (The resolution, with its preamble, is printed in today's Record 
under ``Submitted Resolutions''.)

                          ____________________
```

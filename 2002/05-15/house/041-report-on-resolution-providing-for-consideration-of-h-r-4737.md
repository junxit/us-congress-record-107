# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4737, PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITY, WORK, AND FAMILY PROMOTION ACT OF 2002

**Measures:** H.Res. 422, H.R. 4737

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4737, PERSONAL 
         RESPONSIBILITY, WORK, AND FAMILY PROMOTION ACT OF 2002

  Ms. PRYCE of Ohio, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 107-466) on the resolution (H. Res. 422) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4737) to reauthorize and 
improve the program of block grants to States for temporary assistance 
for needy families, improve access to quality child care, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```

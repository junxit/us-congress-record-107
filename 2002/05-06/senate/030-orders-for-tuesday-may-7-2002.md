# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 7, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 7, 2002

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m., Tuesday, May 7; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate begin consideration of the farm bill conference 
report under the previous order; further, that the Senate recess from 
12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. for the weekly party conferences.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

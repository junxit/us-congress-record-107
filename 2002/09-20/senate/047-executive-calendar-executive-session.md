**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

proceed to executive session to consider Executive Calendar Nos. 1035, 
1036, 1039 and the nominations on the Secretary's desk; that the

table, the President be immediately notified of the Senate's action, 
and that any statements relating thereto be printed in the Record; and

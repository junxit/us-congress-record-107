# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                    SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Select 
Committee on Intelligence be authorized to meet during the session of 
the Senate on Friday, September 20, 2002 at 10:00 a.m. and 2:30 p.m. to 
hold a joint open hearing with the House Permanent Select Committee on 
Intelligence regarding the Joint Inquiry into the events of September 
11, 2001.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

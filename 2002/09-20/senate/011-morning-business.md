# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Nelson of Florida). Under the previous 
order, there will now be a period for the transaction of morning 
business not to extend beyond the hour of 12 noon, with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, and with the time to be 
equally divided between the two leaders or their designees.
  The Senator from New York.

                          ____________________
```

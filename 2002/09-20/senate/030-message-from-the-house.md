# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 337

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12:39 pm., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the following concurrent resolution, in which it requests the 
concurrence of the Senate:

       H. Con. Res. 337. Concurrent resolution recognizing the 
     teams and players of the Negro Baseball Leagues for their 
     achievements, dedication, sacrifices, and contributions to 
     baseball and the Nation.

                          ____________________
```

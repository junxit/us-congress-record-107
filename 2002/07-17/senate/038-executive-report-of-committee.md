# EXECUTIVE REPORT OF COMMITTEE

```
                     EXECUTIVE REPORT OF COMMITTEE

  The following executive report of committee was submitted:

       By Mr. KENNEDY for the Committee on Health, Education, 
     Labor, and Pensions.
       *Richard H. Carmona, of Arizona, to be Medical Director in 
     the Regular Corps of the Public Health Service, subject to 
     qualifications therefor as provided by law and regulations, 
     and to be Surgeon General of the Public Health Service for a 
     term of four years.

  *Nomination was reported with recommendation that it be confirmed 
subject to the nominee's commitment to respond to requests to appear 
and testify before any duly constituted committee of the Senate.
```

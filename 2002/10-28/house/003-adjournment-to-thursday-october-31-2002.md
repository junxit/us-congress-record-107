# ADJOURNMENT TO THURSDAY, OCTOBER 31, 2002

```
               ADJOURNMENT TO THURSDAY, OCTOBER 31, 2002

  The SPEAKER. Without objection, when the House adjourns today, it 
shall stand adjourned until 11 a.m. on Thursday, October 31, 2002; and 
further, when the House adjourns on that day, it shall stand adjourned 
until 11 a.m. on Monday, November 4, 2002.
  There was no objection.
```

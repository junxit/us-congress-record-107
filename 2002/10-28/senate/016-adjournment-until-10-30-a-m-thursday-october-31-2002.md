# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10:30 A.M., THURSDAY, OCTOBER 31, 2002

```
        ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10:30 A.M., THURSDAY, OCTOBER 31, 2002

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, and as a further 
mark of respect to Paul Wellstone, the Senate stands adjourned in his 
memory until the hour of 10:30 a.m. on Thursday, October 31, 2002.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11:11 a.m., adjourned until Thursday, 
October 31, 2002, at 10:30 a.m.
```

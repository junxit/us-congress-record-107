# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 3231

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12:22 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 3231. An act to replace the Immigration and 
     Naturalization Service with the Office of the Associate 
     Attorney General for Immigration Affairs, the Bureau of 
     Citizenship and Immigration Services, and the Bureau of 
     Immigration Enforcement, and for other purposes.
```

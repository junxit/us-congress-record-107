# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 29, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3009

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 29, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 1 p.m. 
Monday, April 29; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the motion to 
proceed to H.R. 3009, the Andean Trade Act, with the time until 6 p.m. 
equally divided between the proponents and opponents of the motion.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

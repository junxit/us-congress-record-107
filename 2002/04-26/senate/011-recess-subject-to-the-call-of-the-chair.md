# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Corzine, Jon S. (C001042) [D-NJ]

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate stand 
in recess pending the call of the Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 10:25 a.m., recessed until 
11:26 a.m. and reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Jeffords).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from New Jersey is recognized.
  Mr. CORZINE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to speak as in 
morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```

# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 9, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 9, 2002

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on Thursday, May 
9; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate be in a period of morning business until 10:30 a.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the time 
under the control of Senator Stabenow or her designee; further, at 
10:30 a.m. the Senate proceed to executive session under the previous 
order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```

# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4546, BOB STUMP NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2003

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4546, BOB 
     STUMP NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2003
```

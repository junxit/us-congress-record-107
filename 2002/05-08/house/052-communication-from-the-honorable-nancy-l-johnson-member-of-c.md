# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE NANCY L. JOHNSON, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
 COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE NANCY L. JOHNSON, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Nancy L. Johnson, Member of Congress:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                      Washington, DC, May 6, 2002.
     Hon. Dennis J. Hastert,
     Speaker, U.S. House of Representatives, Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to formally notify you, pursuant 
     to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of Representatives, 
     that I have been served with a civil subpoena for documents 
     and testimony issued by the United States District Court for 
     the District of Columbia.
       After consulting with the Office of General Counsel, I will 
     make the determinations required by Rule VIII.
           Sincerely,
                                                 Nancy L. Johnson,
     Member of Congress.

                          ____________________
```

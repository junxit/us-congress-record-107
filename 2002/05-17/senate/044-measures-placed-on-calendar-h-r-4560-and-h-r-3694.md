# MEASURES PLACED ON CALENDAR--H.R. 4560 AND H.R. 3694

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3694, H.R. 4560

```
          MEASURES PLACED ON CALENDAR--H.R. 4560 AND H.R. 3694

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand there are two bills at the 
desk, H.R. 4560 and H.R. 3694, that have been read the first time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that it be in order, en bloc, for 
these bills to receive a second reading, and then I will object to any 
further consideration of the legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered. The bills 
will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```

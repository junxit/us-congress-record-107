# RECOGNIZING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE MONTEREY PARK DEMOCRATIC CLUB

**Speaking:** Solis, Hilda L. (S001153) [D-CA]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE MONTEREY PARK DEMOCRATIC CLUB

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. HILDA L. SOLIS

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 16, 2002

  Ms. SOLIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 50th 
anniversary of the Monterey Park Democratic Club. Founded on May 19, 
1952, the Monterey Park Democratic Club has made many achievements over 
the years.
  One of their most important efforts has been to increase voter turn 
out. This has given a voice to numerous residents of Monterey Park who 
otherwise would not have had one. Since the 1950s, the Monterey Park 
Democratic Club has also sponsored and promoted several important 
community events, including roundtable discussions on local issues. 
Club members are not afraid to tackle sensitive issues like the Nuclear 
Freeze campaign of the 1980s, lobbying to close the nearby landfill or 
opposing billboard signs and gambling. Their passion and leadership is 
commendable. I applaud President Irv Wilner and other club leaders for 
their leadership.
  It is clear that the Monterey Park Democratic Club has done much to 
enhance people's confidence in government and faith in democracy. I ask 
you to join me in honoring their celebration.

                          ____________________
```

Fixed Interest Rates for Student Loans: The House passed S. 1762, to 
amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to establish fixed interest 
rates for student and parent borrowers and to extend current law with 
respect to special allowances for lenders by a yea-and-nay vote of 372 
yeas to 3 nays, Roll No. 4--clearing the measure for the President.

# THANKING COLLEAGUES FOR THEIR GENEROUS ACKNOWLEDGMENT REGARDING RETIREMENT ANNOUNCEMENT

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

```
    THANKING COLLEAGUES FOR THEIR GENEROUS ACKNOWLEDGMENT REGARDING 
                        RETIREMENT ANNOUNCEMENT

  (Mr. ARMEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I just wanted to take a moment to thank the

[[Page H49]]

gentlewoman from California for her kind words. I have had so many 
people from the other side of the aisle speak so kindly to me on my 
decision to retire from Congress that I could not resist taking a 
moment to say that at last I finally have made a decision that is a 
source of great pleasure to my colleagues on the other side of the 
aisle, and I want to thank them for their generous acknowledgment of 
that.
```

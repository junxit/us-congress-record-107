# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 25, 2002

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 25, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 a.m., 
Friday, January 25; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceeding be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

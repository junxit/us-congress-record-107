# JOSEPH W. WESTMORELAND POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3960

```
              JOSEPH W. WESTMORELAND POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3960) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 3719 Highway 4 in Jay, Florida, as the 
``Joseph W. Westmoreland Post Office Building,'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```

# CLARENCE B. CRAFT POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 4486

```
                 CLARENCE B. CRAFT POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4486) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1590 East Joyce Boulevard in Fayetteville, 
Arkansas, as the ``Clarence B. Craft Post Office Building,'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```

# RECESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess until the hour of 2 p.m. today.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 1:09 p.m., recessed until 
2:01 p.m. and reassembled when called to order by the Acting President 
pro tempore.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Nevada.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, it is my understanding the supplemental 
appropriations bill has been received from the House. Is this the 
appropriate time to report the bill?
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Will the Senator withhold for one 
moment.
```

# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 118

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  Under the authority of the Senate of January 3, 2001, the Secretary 
of the Senate, on May 29, 2002, during the recess of the Senate, 
received a message from the House of Representatives announcing that 
the House has agreed to the following concurrent resolution, without 
amendment:

       S. Con. Res. 118. Concurrent resolution providing for a 
     conditional adjournment or recess of the Senate and a 
     conditional adjournment of the House of Representatives.
```

# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 4, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 4775

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 4, 2002

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. tomorrow, Tuesday, June 4; 
that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
resume consideration of H.R. 4775; further, the Senate recess from 
12:30 to 2:15 tomorrow for the weekly party conferences.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                       special committee on aging

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Special 
Committee on Aging be authorized to meet on Monday, May 20, 2002, from 
1 p.m.-5 p.m. in Dirksen 215 for the purpose of conducting a hearing 
entitled: ``Financial Exploitation of the Elderly.''
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```

# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, the Senate will convene on Tuesday at 9 a.m. 
with a period of morning business until 9:30. At 9:30 the Senate will 
resume consideration of the trade act, with 90 minutes of debate in 
relation to the steel amendment prior to a rollcall vote on cloture on 
the amendment at approximately 11 a.m.
  The Senate will recess from 12:30 to 2:15 p.m. for our regular weekly 
party conferences.
```

**Speaking:** Becerra, Xavier (B000287) [D-CA]

                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. XAVIER BECERRA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 18, 2002

  Mr. BECERRA. Mr. Speaker, on Monday, June 17, 2002, I was unable to 
cast my floor vote on rollcall numbers 230, 231, and 232. The votes I 
missed include rollcall vote 230 on Approving the Journal; rollcall 
vote 231 on Suspending the Rules and Agreeing to H. Con. Res. 415, 
Recognizing National Homeownership Month and the importance of 
homeownership in the United States; and rollcall vote 232 on Suspending 
the Rules and Agreeing to H. Con. Res. 340, Supporting the goals and 
ideals of Meningitis Awareness Month.
  Had I been present for the votes, I would have voted ``aye'' on 
rollcall votes 230, 231, and 232.

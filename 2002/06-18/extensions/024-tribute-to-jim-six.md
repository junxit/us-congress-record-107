# TRIBUTE TO JIM SIX

**Speaking:** Andrews, Robert E. (A000210) [D-NJ]

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

  Mr. ANDREWS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to the 
patriotic efforts of one of my constituents. In 2001, Gloucester County 
Times columnist Jim Six was able to return to a Texas woman a dog tag 
believed to have belonged to her brother, a Marine who was captured by 
the Viet Cong in 1968 and who reportedly died in a prisoner of war camp 
in 1970. His body has never been recovered. Six, through the efforts of 
an acquaintance, bought more than 400 dog tags from vendors in Vietnam 
in 1993 and is attempting to find more matches.

# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1979, SMALL AIRPORT SAFETY, SECURITY, AND AIR SERVICE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 2002

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1979, SMALL 
   AIRPORT SAFETY, SECURITY, AND AIR SERVICE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 2002

  Ms. PRYCE of Ohio, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 107-515) on the resolution (H. Res. 447) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1979) to amend title 49, 
United States Code, to provide assistance for the construction of 
certain air traffic control towers, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```

# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 14, 2002

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 14, 2002

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 a.m., Thursday, 
February 14; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and there be a period of morning business until 10:15 a.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the first 
20 minutes under the control of Senators Dorgan and Hagel; further, 
that at 10:15 a.m. the Senate resume consideration of the election 
reform bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

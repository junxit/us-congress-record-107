# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

**Measures:** S. 565

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there be a 
period for morning business until 2:30 p.m. today, with 60 minutes 
under the control of Senator Byrd and the remaining time controlled 
equally between Senators Brownback and Torricelli or their designees, 
and that at 2:30 p.m. today the Senate begin consideration of Calendar 
No. 239, S. 565, the election reform bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from New Jersey.

                          ____________________
```

# VISIT TO THE SENATE BY THE PRESIDENT OF ROMANIA

```
            VISIT TO THE SENATE BY THE PRESIDENT OF ROMANIA

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, I ask that it be in order for the Senate to 
stand in recess in honor of the distinguished guest we have today. He 
is the President of Romania. He is in his second term. His name is Ion 
Iliescu. Welcome, Mr. President.
```

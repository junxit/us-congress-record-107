# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 2002

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 2002

  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 9:30 
a.m., Thursday, November 14; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate proceed under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

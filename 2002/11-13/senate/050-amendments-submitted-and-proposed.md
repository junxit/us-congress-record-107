       SA 4900. Mr. FEINGOLD proposed an amendment to the bill

     Miller, Mr. Thompson, Mr. Barkley, and Mr. Voinovich))

     online environment for children, and helps to prevent 
     children from being exposed to harmful material on the 
     Internet, and for other purposes.
       SA 4904. Mr. DURBIN (for Mr. McCain (for himself and Mr.

       SA 4905. Mr. DURBIN (for Mr. Thompson) proposed an 
     amendment to the bill S. 3067, to amend title 44, United 
     States Code, to extend certain Government information

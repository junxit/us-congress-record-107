# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Bingaman, Jeff (B000468) [D-NM]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 4 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the time equally divided between the majority leader and the 
Republican leader or their designees.
  The Senator from New Mexico.
  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that I be 
allowed to speak for up to 20 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

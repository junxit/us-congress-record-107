# REQUEST FOR SEQUENTIAL REFERRAL--S. 2506

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

**Measures:** S.Res. 400, S. 2506

```
                REQUEST FOR SEQUENTIAL REFERRAL--S. 2506

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the following 
letter be printed in the Record.
  There being no objection, the letter was ordered to be printed in the 
Record, as follows:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                  Committee on Armed Services,

                                     Washington, DC, May 13, 2002.
     Hon. Tom Daschle,
     Majority Leader, U.S. Senate,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Senator Daschle: Pursuant to section 3(b) of S. Res. 
     400 of the 94th Congress, we request that S. 2506, the 
     Intelligence Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2003, be 
     sequentially referred to the Committee on Armed Services for 
     a period not to exceed thirty days.
       Best wishes,
           Sincerely,
     John Warner,
       Ranking Member.
     Carl Levin,
       Chairman.

                          ____________________
```

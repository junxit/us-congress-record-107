  At the request of Mr. Leahy, the name of the Senator from New York

Michigan (Ms. Stabenow) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1408, a bill to 
amend title 38, United States Code, to standardize the income threshold 
for copayment for outpatient medications with the income threshold for 
inability to defray necessary expense of care, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1662

  At the request of Mr. Hutchinson, the name of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Mr. Smith) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1662, a bill to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow Coverdell educational 
savings accounts to be used for homeschooling expenses.


                                S. 1686

  At the request of Mr. Kennedy, the names of the Senator from New 
Jersey (Mr. Torricelli) and the Senator from New Jersey (Mr. Corzine)

Social Security Act to provide for patient protection by limiting the

  At the request of Mr. Chafee, his name was added as a cosponsor of S. 
1777, supra.

# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11:30 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Niland, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1366. An act to designate the United States Post 
     Office building located at 3101 West Sunflower Avenue in 
     Santa Ana, California, as the ``Hector G. Godinez Post Office 
     Building.''
       H.R. 3925. An act to establish an exchange program between 
     the Federal Government and the private sector in order to 
     promote the development of expertise in information 
     technology management, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to section 703 of the Social 
Security Act (42 U.S.C. 903), as amended by section 103 of Public Law 
103-296, the Speaker appoints the following member on the part of the 
House of Representatives to the Social Security Advisory Board to fill 
the existing vacancy thereon: Mrs. Dorcas R. Hardy of Spotsylvania, 
Virginia.

                          ____________________
```

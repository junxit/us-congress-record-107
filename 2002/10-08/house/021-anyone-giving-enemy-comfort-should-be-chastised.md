# ANYONE GIVING ENEMY COMFORT SHOULD BE CHASTISED

**Speaking:** Cunningham, Randy (Duke) (C000994) [R-CA]

```
            ANYONE GIVING ENEMY COMFORT SHOULD BE CHASTISED

  (Mr. CUNNINGHAM asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CUNNINGHAM. Mr. Speaker, as a combat veteran from Vietnam, I and 
many other men and women still harbor ill feelings towards Jane Fonda 
and Tom Hayden, who gave the enemy comfort and gave them propaganda 
against the United States.
  Anyone, anyone who would travel to an enemy country and do the same 
thing should be chastised by this body and by this country.

                          ____________________
```

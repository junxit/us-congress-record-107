# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 24, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 622

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 24, 2002

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes it business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Thursday, January 24; that following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and there be a period of morning business until 10 
a.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each with 
the time equally divided between the two leaders or their designees; 
further, at 10 a.m., the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 622, the 
Adoption Tax Credit Act, with the Daschle economic recovery amendment 
being the pending amendment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

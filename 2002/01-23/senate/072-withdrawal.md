# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
January 23, 2002, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nomination:
       Scott A. Abdallah, of South Dakota, to be United States 
     Attorney for the District of South Dakota for the term of 
     four years, which was sent to the Senate on November 30, 
     2001.
```

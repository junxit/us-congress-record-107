# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the hour of 
12:30 p.m. having arrived, the Senate will now stand in recess until 
the hour of 2:15 p.m. today.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:49 p.m., recessed until 2:15 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the President 
pro tempore.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Delaware.

                          ____________________
```

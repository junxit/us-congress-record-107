# SENATE--Wednesday, January 23, 2002

```
[[Page 39]]

                   SENATE--Wednesday, January 23, 2002

  The 23d day of January being the day prescribed by H. Con. Res. 295 
for the meeting of the second session of the 107th Congress, the Senate 
assembled in its Chamber at the Capitol at 12 noon and was called to 
order by the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).
```

# DAILY HOUR OF MEETING

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 333

```
                         DAILY HOUR OF MEETING

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 333) 
and ask for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 333

       Resolved, That unless otherwise ordered, before Monday, May 
     13, 2002, the hour of daily meeting of the House shall be 2 
     p.m. on Mondays; 11 a.m. on Tuesdays; and 10 a.m. on all 
     other days of the week; and from Monday, May 13, 2002, until 
     the end of the second session, the hour of daily meeting of 
     the House shall be noon on Mondays; 10 a.m. on Tuesdays, 
     Wednesdays, and Thurdays; and 9 a.m. on all other days of the 
     week.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```

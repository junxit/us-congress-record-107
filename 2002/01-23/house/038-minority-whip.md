# MINORITY WHIP

**Speaking:** Gephardt, Richard A. (G000132) [D-MO]

```
                              {time}  1415
                             MINORITY WHIP

  Mr. GEPHARDT. Mr. Speaker, as leader of the Democratic Caucus, I have 
been directed to report to the House that the Democratic Members have 
selected as their minority whip the gentlewoman from California, the 
Honorable Nancy Pelosi.
  As a matter of information to the Members of the House, it is my 
understanding that this is the highest position to which a woman has 
been elected in the history of the House of Representatives.
```

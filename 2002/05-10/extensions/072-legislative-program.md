# LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 3994

```
                          LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. RICHARD K. ARMEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 9, 2002

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I am pleased to announce that the House has 
completed its legislative business for the week.
  The House will next meet for legislative business on Tuesday, May 14 
at 12:30 p.m. for morning hour and 2 o'clock p.m. for legislative 
business. On Tuesday, I will schedule a number of measures under 
suspension of the rules, a list of which will be distributed to 
Members' offices tomorrow. Recorded votes will be postponed until 6:30 
p.m.
  On Wednesday and the balance of the week, I have scheduled the 
Personal Responsibility, Work, and Family Promotion Act and H.R. 3994, 
the Afghanistan Freedom Support Act of 2002.

                          ____________________
```

```
       COMMUNICATION FROM THE HON. TOM DeLAY, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

communication from the Honorable Tom DeLay, Member of Congress:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                      Washington, DC, May 8, 2002.

     Speaker, U.S. House of Representatives,

     Columbia.

     Member of Congress.
```

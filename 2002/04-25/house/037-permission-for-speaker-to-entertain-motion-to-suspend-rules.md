**Measures:** H.R. 2604

```
PERMISSION FOR SPEAKER TO ENTERTAIN MOTION TO SUSPEND RULES RELATING TO 
         H.R. 2604 ON LEGISLATIVE DAY OF WEDNESDAY, MAY 1, 2002
```

# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 23, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 23, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until tomorrow morning, 
Thursday, May 23, at 9:30 a.m.; that following the prayer and pledge 
the Journal of proceeding be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day; that there be a period for morning business until 
10:30 a.m. with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each, with the first half of the time under the control of the 
Republican leader, or his designee, and the second half under the 
control of the Democrat leader, or his designee; and, that at 10:30 
a.m. the Senate resume consideration of the bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

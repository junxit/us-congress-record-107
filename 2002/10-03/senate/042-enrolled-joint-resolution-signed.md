# ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 112

```
                    ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

  At 5:42 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by one of its clerks, announced that the Speaker has signed the 
following enrolled bill:

       H.J. Res. 112. A joint resolution making further continuing 
     appropriations for the fiscal year 2003, and for other 
     purposes.

  The enrolled joint resolution was signed subsequently by the 
President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).

                          ____________________
```

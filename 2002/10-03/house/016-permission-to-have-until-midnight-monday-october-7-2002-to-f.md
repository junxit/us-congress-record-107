# PERMISSION TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, MONDAY, OCTOBER 7, 2002 TO FILE CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 4628, INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2003

```
  PERMISSION TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, MONDAY, OCTOBER 7, 2002 TO FILE 
  CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 4628, INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR 
                            FISCAL YEAR 2003

  Mr. GOSS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the managers may 
have until midnight on Monday, October 7, 2002, to file the conference 
report on the bill (H.R. 4628) to authorize appropriations for fiscal 
year 2003 for intelligence and intelligence-related activities of the 
United States Government, the Community Management Account, and the 
Central Intelligence Agency Retirement and Disability System, and for 
other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

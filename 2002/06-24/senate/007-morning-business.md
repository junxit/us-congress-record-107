# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 4 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each, and with the time to be equally 
divided between the two leaders or their designees.
  The Senator from Nevada.

                          ____________________
```

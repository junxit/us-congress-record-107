# NEW RIVER GORGE BOUNDARY ACT OF 2002

**Measures:** H.R. 3858

```
                  NEW RIVER GORGE BOUNDARY ACT OF 2002

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair will now resume proceedings on the 
question of suspending the rules and passing the bill, H.R. 3858.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Utah (Mr. Cannon) that the House suspend the rules and 
pass the bill, H.R. 3858.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the bill was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```

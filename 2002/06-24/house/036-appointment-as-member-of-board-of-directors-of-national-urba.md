# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NATIONAL URBAN AIR TOXIC RESEARCH CENTER

```
APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF NATIONAL URBAN AIR TOXIC 
                            RESEARCH CENTER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Isakson). Without objection, and 
pursuant to section 112 of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7412) the Chair 
announces the Speaker's appointment of the following member on the part 
of the House to the board of directors of the National Urban Air Toxic 
Research Center to fill the existing vacancy thereon:
  Dr. Arthur C. Vailas, Houston, Texas.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

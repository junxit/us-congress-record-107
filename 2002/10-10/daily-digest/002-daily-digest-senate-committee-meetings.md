# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

**Measures:** S. 2986

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
SHIPBOARD HAZARD AND DEFENSE TESTS
Committee on Armed Services: Committee concluded hearings to examine 
the Department of Defense's inquiry into Project 112 (a chemical and 
biological warfare testing program management initiative) and Shipboard 
Hazard and Defense (SHAD) tests, after receiving testimony from 
Representative Mike Thompson; William Winkenwerder, Jr., Assistant 
Secretary for Health Affairs, and Michael E. Kilpatrick, Deputy 
Director for Deployment Health Support Directorate, both of the 
Department of Defense; Robert J. Epley, Associate Deputy Under 
Secretary for Policy and Program Management, and Kenneth C. Hyams, 
Chief Consultant, Occupational and Environmental State Healthcare 
Group, both of the Department of Veterans Affairs; LCDR Jack B. 
Alderson, USNR (Ret.), Eureka, California; Robert W. Bates, Vancouver, 
Washington; and George J. Brocklebank, Palm Beach Gardens, Florida.
BAY MILLS INDIAN COMMUNITY LAND CLAIM SETTLEMENT ACT
Committee on Indian Affairs: Committee concluded hearings on S. 2986, 
to provide for and approve the settlement of certain land claims of the 
Bay Mills Indian Community, Michigan, after receiving testimony from 
Senator Stabenow; Representatives Stupak and Bonior; Aurene M. Martin, 
Deputy Assistant Secretary of the Interior for Indian Affairs; R. Lance 
Boldrey, Office of Michigan Governor John Engler, Lansing; L. John 
Lufkins, Bay Mills Indian Community, Brimley, Michigan; Bernard 
Bouschor, Sault Ste. Marie Tribe of Chippewa Indians, Sault Ste. Marie, 
Michigan; and George Bennett, Grand Traverse Band Tribal Council, 
Peshawbestown, Michigan, on behalf of the Grand Traverse Band of Ottawa 
and Chippewa Indians.
```

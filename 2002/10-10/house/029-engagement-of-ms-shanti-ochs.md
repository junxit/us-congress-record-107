# ENGAGEMENT OF MS. SHANTI OCHS

```
                     ENGAGEMENT OF MS. SHANTI OCHS

  (Mr. ARMEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, it has been brought to my attention that one 
Shanti Ochs, a distinguished member of our floor staff, is sporting a 
new diamond ring on her left hand. This causes the gentleman from Texas 
to conclude that she has just become engaged to a young man who most 
certainly is not good enough for her. So I would recommend to Ms. 
Shanti Ochs that she postpone any permanent wedding plans until the 
majority leader receives his FBI report on the young man in question.
```

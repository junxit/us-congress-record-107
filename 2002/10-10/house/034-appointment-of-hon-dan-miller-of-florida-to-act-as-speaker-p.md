# APPOINTMENT OF HON. DAN MILLER OF FLORIDA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH OCTOBER 15, 2002

```
APPOINTMENT OF HON. DAN MILLER OF FLORIDA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE 
 TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH OCTOBER 15, 2002

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker.

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                 October 10, 2002.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Dan Miller to act as Speaker 
     pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions 
     through October 15, 2002.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 5427

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 5427

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 5427

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, H.R. 5427 is at the desk and due for its 
second reading; is that right?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct.
  Mr. REID. I ask that H.R. 5427 be read a second time, but I also 
object to any further proceedings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 5427) to designate the Federal building 
     located at Fifth and Richardson Avenues in Roswell, New 
     Mexico, as the ``Joe Skeen Federal Building''.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill will be 
placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```

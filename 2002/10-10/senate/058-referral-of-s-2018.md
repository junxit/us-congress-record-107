# REFERRAL OF S. 2018

```
                          REFERRAL OF S. 2018

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that Calendar No. 637, S. 2018, a 
bill to establish the T'uf Shur Bien Preservation Trust Area, be 
referred to the Indian Affairs Committee for the sole purpose of the 
committee reporting the bill with amendments that were agreed upon by 
the committee, and the bill be returned to the calendar forthwith.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

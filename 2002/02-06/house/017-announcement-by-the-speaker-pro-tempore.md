# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Fossella). The Chair must remind Members 
that during a session of the House, it shall not be in order for a 
Member, Delegate, or Resident Commissioner to introduce to or bring to 
the attention of the House an occupant of the galleries of the House.
```

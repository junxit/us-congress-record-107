# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 7, 2002

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 7, 2002

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 
a.m. tomorrow, Thursday, February 7; that following the prayer and 
pledge the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour 
be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of S. 
1731.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

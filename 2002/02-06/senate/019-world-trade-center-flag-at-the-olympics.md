# WORLD TRADE CENTER FLAG AT THE OLYMPICS

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

```
                WORLD TRADE CENTER FLAG AT THE OLYMPICS

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, first I wish to compliment Senator 
Clinton from New York for her speech in criticizing the International 
Olympic Committee for refusing to allow us to use the damaged flag that 
flew in the recovery efforts at the World Trade Center. I find that 
decision very offensive. I am going to join her on that letter, and I 
would encourage my colleagues to do so as well.

                          ____________________
```

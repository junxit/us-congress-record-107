# GUEST CHAPLAINS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                            GUEST CHAPLAINS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, the Chaplain, Reverend Ogilvie, has been out 
of the city for all of this week, and he has had military chaplains 
come in. They have been very impressive. Yesterday, we had the Coast 
Guard Chaplain, today the Navy Chaplain, and the day before the Army 
Chaplain. I have been very impressed with their stature and their 
message.
  I am sure this means a great deal to the Presiding Officer, who is a 
graduate of the Military Academy at West Point. It is good that it 
reminds us on occasion of the importance of these men and women in 
uniform, and also the fact that they are constantly aware of the need 
for spiritual guidance.
  I think their being here the last few days has certainly indicated 
that to anyone watching these proceedings.

                          ____________________
```

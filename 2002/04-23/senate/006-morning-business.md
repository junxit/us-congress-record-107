# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 11:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each and with the time equally divided 
between the two leaders or their designees.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 24, 2002

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 24, 2002

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., tomorrow, Wednesday, April 
24; following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, and the Senate 
resume consideration of the energy reform bill; that the next amendment 
to be offered be a Craig amendment regarding hydro; further, that 18 
hours remain under cloture on the Daschle-Bingaman substitute 
amendment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

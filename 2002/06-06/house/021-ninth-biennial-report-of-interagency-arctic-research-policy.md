# NINTH BIENNIAL REPORT OF INTERAGENCY ARCTIC RESEARCH POLICY COMMITTEE TO THE CONGRESS--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
 NINTH BIENNIAL REPORT OF INTERAGENCY ARCTIC RESEARCH POLICY COMMITTEE 
    TO THE CONGRESS--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, referred to the Committee on Science:

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 108(b) of Public Law 98-373 (15 U.S.C. 
4107(b)), I transmit herewith the Ninth Biennial Report of the 
Interagency Arctic Research Policy Committee (February 1, 2000, to 
January 31, 2002).
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, June 6, 2002.

                          ____________________
```

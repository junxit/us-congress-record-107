# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 4800

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 4800

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 4800

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, it is my understanding that H.R. 4800 is at 
the desk, and I, therefore, ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the first 
time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 4800) to repeal the sunset of the Economic 
     Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001 with respect 
     to the expansion of the adoption credit and adoption 
     assistance programs.

  Mr. REID. I now ask for its second reading but object to my own 
request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```

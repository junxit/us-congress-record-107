# NINTH BIENNIAL REPORT OF THE INTERAGENCY ARCTIC RESEARCH POLICY COMMITTEE FROM FEBRUARY 1, 2000 THROUGH JANUARY 31, 2002--PM 89

```
    NINTH BIENNIAL REPORT OF THE INTERAGENCY ARCTIC RESEARCH POLICY 
    COMMITTEE FROM FEBRUARY 1, 2000 THROUGH JANUARY 31, 2002--PM 89

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Government Affairs:

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 108(b) of Public Law 98-373 (15 U.S.C. 
4107(b)), I transmit herewith the Ninth Biennial Report of the 
Interagency Arctic Research Policy Committee (February 1, 2000, to 
January 31, 2002).
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, June 6, 2002.

                          ____________________
```

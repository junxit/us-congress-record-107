# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** McIntyre, Mike (M000485) [D-NC]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE McINTYRE

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 5, 2002

  Mr. McINTYRE. Mr. Speaker, on Wednesday, May 22, 2002, 1 was 
unavoidably absent for rollcall votes 195 and 196. Had I been present I 
would have voted ``no'' on rollcall vote 195 and ``no'' on rollcall 
vote 196. Additionally, on Thursday, May 24 and Friday, May 25, 2002, 1 
was unavoidable absent for rollcall votes 199 through 206, in order to 
attend my son's high school graduation ceremony. Had I been present I 
would have voted ``no'' on rollcall 199, ``yea'' on rollcall 200, 
``yea'' on rollcall 201, ``yea'' on rollcall 202, ``no'' on rollcall 
203, ``no'' on rollcall 204, ``yea'' on rollcall 205, and ``yea'' on 
rollcall 206.
```

# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
October 17, 2002, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nomination:

       Peter Marzio, of Texas, to be a Member of the National 
     Museum Services Board for a term expiring December 6, 2006, 
     vice Ruth Y. Tamura, term expired, which was sent to the 
     Senate on September 4, 2002.
```

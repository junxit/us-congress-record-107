# ROLLAN D. MELTON POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 4102

```
                 ROLLAN D. MELTON POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4102) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 120 North Maine Street in Fallon, Nevada, as 
the ``Rollan D. Melton Post Office Building'' was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```

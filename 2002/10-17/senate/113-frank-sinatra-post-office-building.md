# FRANK SINATRA POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                   FRANK SINATRA POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3034) to redesignate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 89 River Street in Hoboken, New Jersey, as 
the ``Frank Sinatra Post Office Building'' was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```

# NAT KING COLE POST OFFICE

```
                       NAT KING COLE POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 4797) to redesignate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 265 South Western Avenue, Los Angeles, 
California, as the ``Nat King Cole Post Office'' was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```

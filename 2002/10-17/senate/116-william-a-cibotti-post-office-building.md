# WILLIAM A. CIBOTTI POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                WILLIAM A. CIBOTTI POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3740) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 925 Dickinson Street in Philadelphia, 
Pennsylvania, as the ``William A. Cibotti Post Office Building'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```

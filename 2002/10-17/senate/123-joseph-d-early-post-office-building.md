# JOSEPH D. EARLY POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                  JOSEPH D. EARLY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5333) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 4 East Central Street in Worcester, 
Massachusetts, as the ``Joseph D. Early Post Office Building'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```

# BARNEY APODACA POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 5308

```
                       BARNEY APODACA POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 5308) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 301 South Howes Street in Fort Collins, 
Colorado, as the ``Barney Apodaca Post Office'' was considered, ordered 
to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```

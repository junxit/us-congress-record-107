# REV. LEON SULLIVAN POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3739

```
                REV. LEON SULLIVAN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3739) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 6150 North Broad Street in Philadelphia, 
Pennsylvania, as the ``Rev. Leon Sullivan Post Office Building'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```

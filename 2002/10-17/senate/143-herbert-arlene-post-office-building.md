# HERBERT ARLENE POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3738

```
                  HERBERT ARLENE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 3738) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1299 North 7th Street in Philadelphia, 
Pennsylvania, as the ``Herbert Arlene Post Office Building'' was 
considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and 
passed.
```

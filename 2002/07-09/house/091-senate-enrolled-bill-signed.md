# SENATE ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** S. 2594

```
                      SENATE ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  The SPEAKER announced his signature to an enrolled bill of the Senate 
of the following title:

       S. 2594. To authorize the Secretary of the Treasury to 
     purchase silver on the open market when the silver stockpile 
     is depleted, to be used to mint coins.
```

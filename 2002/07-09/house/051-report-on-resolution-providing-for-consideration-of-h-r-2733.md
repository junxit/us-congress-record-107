# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2733, ENTERPRISE INTEGRATION ACT OF 2002

**Measures:** H.Res. 474, H.R. 2733

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2733, 
                   ENTERPRISE INTEGRATION ACT OF 2002

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-559) on the resolution (H. Res. 474) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2733) to authorize the National 
Institute of Standards and Technology to work with major manufacturing 
industries on an initiative of standards development and implementation 
for electronic enterprise integration, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```

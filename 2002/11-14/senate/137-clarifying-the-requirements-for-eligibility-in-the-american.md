**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 2934

```
   CLARIFYING THE REQUIREMENTS FOR ELIGIBILITY IN THE AMERICAN LEGION

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of calendar No. 756, S. 2934.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will state the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2934) to amend title 36, United States Code, to

     Legion.

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```

# AMENDING TITLE 36 U.S. CODE TO CLARIFY THE REQUIREMENT FOR ELIGIBILITY IN THE AMERICAN LEGION

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3988

```
AMENDING TITLE 36 U.S. CODE TO CLARIFY THE REQUIREMENT FOR ELIGIBILITY 
                         IN THE AMERICAN LEGION

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of Calendar No. 758, H.R. 3988.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will state the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3988) to amend title 36, United States Code, 
     to clarify the requirement for eligibility in the American 
     Legion.

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
read the third time and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon 
the table, and that any statements related to the bill be printed in 
the Record.
```

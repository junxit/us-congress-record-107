# GRANTING MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO REVISE AND EXTEND REMARKS IN CONGRESSIONAL RECORD UNTIL LAST EDITION IS PUBLISHED

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

```
GRANTING MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO REVISE AND EXTEND REMARKS IN 
          CONGRESSIONAL RECORD UNTIL LAST EDITION IS PUBLISHED

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that Members may have 
until publication of the last edition of the Congressional Record 
authorized for the second session of the 107th Congress by the Joint 
Committee on Printing to revise and extend their remarks and to include 
brief, related extraneous material on any matter occurring before the 
adjournment of the second session sine die.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

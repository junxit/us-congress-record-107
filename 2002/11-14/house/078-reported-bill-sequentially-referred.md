# REPORTED BILL SEQUENTIALLY REFERRED

**Speaking:** Burton, Dan (B001149) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2458

```
                  REPORTED BILL SEQUENTIALLY REFERRED

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, bills and reports were delivered to the 
Clerk for printing, and bills referred as follows:

       Mr. BURTON: Committee on Government Reform. H.R. 2458. A 
     bill to enhance the management and promotion of electronic 
     Government services and processes by establishing a Federal 
     Chief Information Officer within the Office of Management and 
     Budget, and by reestablishing a broad framework of measurers 
     that require using Internet-based information technology to 
     enhance citizen access to Government information and 
     services, and for other purposes, with an amendment; referred 
     to the Committee on Judiciary for a period ending not later 
     than November 14, 2002, for consideration of such provisions 
     of the bill and amendment as fall within the jurisdiction of 
     that committee pursuant to clause 1(k), rule X (Rept. 107-
     787, Pt. 1).
```

# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 12, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 12, 2002

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until 9:15 a.m., Friday, 
July 12; that following the prayer and pledge, the Senate resume 
consideration of the accounting reform bill with the time until 9:30 
equally divided between the two managers for debate only prior to the 
vote on cloture on the bill; further, that Senators have until 9:25 
a.m. to file second-degree amendments to the accounting reform bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

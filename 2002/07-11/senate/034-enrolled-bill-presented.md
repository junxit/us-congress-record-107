# ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, July 11, 2002, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bill:

       S. 2594. An act to authorize the Secretary of the Treasury 
     to purchase silver on the open market when the silver 
     stockpile is depleted, to be used to mint coins.
```

# PRIVILEGE OF THE FLOOR

**Speaking:** Sarbanes, Paul S. (S000064) [D-MD], Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 2673

```
                         PRIVILEGE OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. SARBANES. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that Glenna 
Humphries, a fellow in the office of Senator Bill Nelson of Florida, be
```

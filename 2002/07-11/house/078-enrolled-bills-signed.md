# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 87, H.R. 2362, H.R. 3971

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Mr. Trandahl, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly enrolled 
bills of the House of the following titles, which were thereupon signed 
by the Speaker:

       H.J. Res. 87. A joint resolution approving the site at 
     Yucca Mountain, Nevada, for the development of a repository 
     for the disposal of high-level radioactive waste and spent 
     nuclear fuel, pursuant to the Nuclear Waste Policy Act of 
     1982.
       H.R. 2362. An act to establish the Benjamin Franklin 
     Tercentenary Commission.
       H.R. 3971. An act to provide for an independent 
     investigation of Forest Service firefighter deaths that are 
     caused by wildfire entrapment or burnover.

                          ____________________
```

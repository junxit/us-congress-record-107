# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 11, 2002

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 11, 2002

  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today it adjourn until the hour of 3 
p.m., Monday, March 11; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the energy reform 
bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

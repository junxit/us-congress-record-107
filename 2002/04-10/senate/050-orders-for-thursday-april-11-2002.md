# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 11, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 565

```
[[Page S2505]]
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, APRIL 11, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 o'clock 
tomorrow morning; that immediately following the prayer and the pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed to have expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the 
energy reform bill; further, that at 11:30 a.m., the Senate resume 
consideration of S. 565, with 30 minutes of debate equally divided 
between Senators Dodd and McConnell, or their designees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection; it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

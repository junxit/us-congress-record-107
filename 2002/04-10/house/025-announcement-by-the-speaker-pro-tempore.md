# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 363, H.R. 3991

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair 
will now put the question on each motion to suspend the rules on which 
further proceedings were postponed on Tuesday, April 9, in the order in 
which that motion was entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  House Resolution 363, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 3991, de novo.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for the electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.
```

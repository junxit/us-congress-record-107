# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING SHAYNA L. SMITH

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
               A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING SHAYNA L. SMITH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 17, 2002

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, whereas, Shayna Smith has devoted herself to 
education during her enrollment at Wheeling Jesuit University; and
  Whereas, Shayna Smith has spent countless hours in the pursuit of 
academic excellence; and
  Whereas, Shayna Smith has demonstrated a commitment to meet 
challenges with enthusiasm, confidence and outstanding service; and
  Whereas, Shayna Smith must be commended for the hard work and 
dedication she put forth in graduating from Wheeling Jesuit University;
  Therefore, I join the entire 18th Congressional District in 
congratulating Shayna Smith as she receives her Bachelor of Science 
from Wheeling Jesuit University Scout Award.

                          ____________________
```

# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
September 17, 2002, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nomination:
       John Roderick Davis, of Alabama, to be a Member of the 
     Board of Trustees of the Henry S Truman Scholarship 
     Foundation for a term expiring December 10, 2005, vice E. 
     Gordon Gee, term expired, which was sent to the Senate on May 
     6, 2002.
```

# VOTE EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Bunning, Jim (B001066) [R-KY]

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

  Mr. BUNNING. Mr. President I was necessarily absent for the vote in 
executive session on September 9, 2002. Therefore, I did not formally 
vote on the nomination of Kenneth A. Marra, of Florida, to be United 
States District Judge for the Southern District of Florida. Had I been 
present for that vote, I would have voted ``yea'' to confirm Mr. Marra 
for this position.
```

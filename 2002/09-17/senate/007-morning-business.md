# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 10:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes. Under the previous order, the first half 
of the time shall be under the control of the majority leader or his 
designee.
  The Senator from North Dakota.

                          ____________________
```

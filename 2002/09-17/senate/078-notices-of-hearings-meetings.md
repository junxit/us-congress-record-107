# NOTICES OF HEARINGS/MEETINGS

**Speaking:** Inouye, Daniel K. (I000025) [D-HI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2880

```
                      NOTICES OF HEARINGS/MEETINGS


                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. INOUYE. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the 
Committee on Indian Affairs will meet on Wednesday, September 18, 2002, 
at 10 a.m. in Room 485 of the Russell Senate Office Building to conduct 
a hearing on H.R. 2880, a bill to amend laws relating to the lands of 
the enrollees and lineal descendants of enrollees whose names appear on 
the final Indian rolls of the Muscogee (Creek), Seminole, Cherokee, 
Chickasaw, and Choctaw Nations, and for other purposes.
  Those wishing additional information may contact the Indian Affairs 
Committee at 224-2251.

                          ____________________
```

# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to 
executive session to consider Calendar Nos. 728 and 729, en bloc; that 
the nominations be confirmed; the motions to reconsider be laid upon 
the table; the President be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action; any statements appear at the appropriate place in the Record; 
and the Senate return to legislative session.

  The nominations considered and confirmed en bloc are as follows:


                         DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

       Don Slazinik, of Illinois, to be United States Marshal for 
     the Southern District of Illinois for the term of four years.
```

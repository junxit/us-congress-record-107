# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 2989, S. 2990, S. 2991

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mrs. BOXER:
       S. 2989. A bill to protect certain lands held in fee by the 
     Pechanga Band of Luiseno Mission Indians from condemnation 
     until a final decision is made by the Secretary of the 
     Interior regarding a pending fee to trust application for 
     that land; to the Committee on Indian Affairs.

[[Page S9033]]

           By Mr. BINGAMAN:
       S. 2990. A bill to provide for programs and activities to 
     improve the health of Hispanic individuals, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. DASCHLE (for Mr. Torricelli):
       S. 2991. A bill for the relief of Sharif Kesbeh, Asmaa 
     Sharif Kesbeh, Batool Kesbeh, Noor Sharif Kesbeh, Alaa 
     Kesbeh, Sandos Kesbeh, Hadeel Kesbeh, and Mohanned Kesbeh; to 
     the Committee on the Judiciary.

                          ____________________
```

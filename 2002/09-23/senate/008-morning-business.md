# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business until 3:30 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each. Under the previous order, the first half of the time will be 
under the control of the Republican leader or his designee.
  The distinguished Senator from Wyoming.

                          ____________________
```

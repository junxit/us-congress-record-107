# PERMISSION TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 23, 2002, TO FILE CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 1646, FOREIGN RELATIONS AUTHORIZATION ACT, FISCAL YEARS 2002 AND 2003

```
PERMISSION TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT, MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 23, 2002, TO FILE 
 CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 1646, FOREIGN RELATIONS AUTHORIZATION ACT, 
                       FISCAL YEARS 2002 AND 2003

  Mr. SMITH of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that

conference report on the bill (H.R. 1646) to authorize appropriations 
for the Department of State for fiscal years 2002 and 2003, and for 
other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Simpson). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from New Jersey?
  There was no objection.
```

# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 11:15 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each. Under the previous order, the first 
half of the time will be under the control of the majority leader or 
his designee. Pursuant to the order, the Senator from New Mexico is 
recognized.
```

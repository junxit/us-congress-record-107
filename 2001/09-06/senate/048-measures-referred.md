# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 1866, H.R. 1886, H.R. 2048, H.R. 2510

                           MEASURES REFERRED

unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 1866. An act to amend title 35, United States Code, to 
     clarify the basis for granting requests for reexamination of 
     patents; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
       H.R. 1886. An act to amend title 35, United States Code, to 
     provide for appeals by third parties in certain patent 
     reexamination proceedings; to the Committee on the Judiciary.

     Defense Production Act of 1950, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.

# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, as has been announced, in 5 minutes the 
Senate will recess for purposes of the joint meeting with President Fox 
of Mexico. Senators have been notified to be here in 5 minutes to 
proceed to the House Chamber for the meeting.
  When the Senate reconvenes at 12 noon, we will continue on the export 
administration bill. It is my understanding, having spoken with the 
managers of the bill, Senators Sarbanes and Enzi, that progress has 
been made over the evening, and I have been told--and we will hear more 
from the managers shortly--that that bill can be wrapped up this 
afternoon. I hope that is the case because we want to alert Senator 
Hollings and Senator Gregg that we should move and will move to the 
Commerce-State-Justice Appropriations Act today as soon as this other 
legislation is finished.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Maryland.

                          ____________________
```

# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Smith, Gordon H. (S001142) [R-OR]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SMITH of Oregon. On Monday, at 2 p.m., the Senate will begin 
debate on the nomination of Gale Norton to be Secretary of the 
Interior. Tuesday morning, the Senate will conduct debate on the 
nomination of Elaine Chao to be Secretary of Labor and Governor Whitman 
to be Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency.
  As a reminder to all Senators, the next rollcall votes will occur on 
Tuesday, January 30, beginning at 2:45 p.m. in a back-to-back sequence. 
Following those back-to-back votes, the Senate will then begin debate 
on the nomination of Senator Ashcroft to be Attorney General of the 
United States.

                          ____________________
```

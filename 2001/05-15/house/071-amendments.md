# AMENDMENTS

**Measures:** H.R. 1

```
                               AMENDMENTS

  Under clause 8 of rule XVIII, proposed amendments were submitted as 
follows:

                                 H.R. 1

                         Offered By Mr. Hoeffel

       Amendment No. 1: In section 5214(b)(1) of the Elementary 
     and Secondary Education Act of 1965, as proposed to be 
     amended by section 501 of the bill, add at the end the 
     following: ``Such a description may include how the applicant 
     will provide release time for teachers (which may include the 
     provision of a substitute teacher).''.
```

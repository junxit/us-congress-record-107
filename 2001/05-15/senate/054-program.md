# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Jeffords, James M. (J000072) [I-VT]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. JEFFORDS. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will be in a short period for morning business beginning at 9:30 
a.m. during tomorrow's session. It is expected that the Senate will 
begin consideration of the reconciliation bill. Senators will be 
notified as to when debate will begin on that legislation. Under the 
rule, there are 20 hours for consideration of that bill. Amendments 
will be offered, and therefore votes are expected throughout the day 
and into the evening.
```

# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 3, 2001

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 3, 2001

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9 
a.m. on Tuesday, April 3. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Tuesday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then resume consideration of House Concurrent Resolution 83, the 
budget resolution.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JANUARY 8, 2001

```
               ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JANUARY 8, 2001

  At the conclusion of the joint session of the two Houses, and in 
accordance with the order previously entered, the Senate adjourned 
until Monday, January 8, 2001, at 12 noon.
```

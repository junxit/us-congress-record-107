# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2500, DEPARTMENTS OF COMMERCE, JUSTICE, AND STATE, THE JUDICIARY, AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2002

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2500, 
DEPARTMENTS OF COMMERCE, JUSTICE, AND STATE, THE JUDICIARY, AND RELATED 
                   AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2002

  Mr. LINDER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-141) on the resolution (H. Res. 192) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2500) making appropriations for the 
Departments of Commerce, Justice, and State, the Judiciary, and related 
agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2002, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```

# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JULY 17, 2001

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JULY 17, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9 
a.m. tomorrow, July 17.
  I further ask consent that on Tuesday, immediately following the 
prayer and the pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate resume 
consideration of the Bankruptcy Reform Act; further, that the Senate 
recess from 12:30 to 2:15 for the weekly party conferences.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# RECOGNIZING VICKI SANTOS, STAFF MEMBER OF COMMITTEE ON RULES

**Speaking:** Reynolds, Thomas M. (R000569) [R-NY]

```
      RECOGNIZING VICKI SANTOS, STAFF MEMBER OF COMMITTEE ON RULES

  (Mr. REYNOLDS asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. REYNOLDS. Mr. Speaker, as we complete our legislative day, before 
I send to the desk a privileged report from the Committee on Rules for 
filing under the rule, I would like to just recognize Vicki Santos on 
the legislative day of November 29 of this year.
  Tomorrow, on November 30, she will be having her last day as she goes 
back home to an accounting practice that her mother has, and we will 
miss her on the Committee on Rules and on the floor of this House.
```

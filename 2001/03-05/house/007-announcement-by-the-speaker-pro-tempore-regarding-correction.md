    ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE REGARDING CORRECTION TO 
     CONGRESSIONAL RECORD OF THURSDAY, MARCH 1, 2001, AT PAGE H598

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair desires to announce that rollcall 
vote 23 of March 1, 2001, as recorded by the electronic voting system, 
was correct and submitted correctly to the Government Printing Office 
for printing in the Congressional Record. The appearance of rollcall 
vote 23 in the Congressional Record of March 1, 2001, was incomplete as 
the result of a Government Printing Office omission.
  A complete listing of rollcall vote 23 and an indication of the 10 
Members whose votes were omitted by the Government Printing Office from 
page 598 of the Record will be inserted at this point, without 
objection.

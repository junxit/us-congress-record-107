# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 858, H.R. 859

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions were 
introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. LaFALCE:
       H.R. 858. A bill to amend the National Housing Act to 
     simplify the downpayment requirements for FHA mortgage 
     insurance for single family homebuyers; to the Committee on 
     Financial Services.
           By Mr. LaFALCE:
       H.R. 859. A bill to amend section 203 of the National 
     Housing Act to reduce the down payment required by a first-
     time homebuyer purchasing a home with a mortgage insured 
     under such section; to the Committee on Financial Services.
```

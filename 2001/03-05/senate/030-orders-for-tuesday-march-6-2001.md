# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 6, 2001

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 6, 2001

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 
a.m. on Tuesday, March 6. I further ask consent that on Tuesday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, and the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. FRIST. Further, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
recess from the hours of 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. for the weekly policy 
conferences to meet.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. For the information of all Senators, the majority leader 
will be recognized at 10 a.m. to begin consideration of Senate Joint 
Resolution 6, the ergonomics disapproval resolution. Under the 
Congressional Review Act, there will be up to 10 hours of debate, with 
a vote on disapproval of the ergonomics rules to occur at the use or 
yielding back of that time. The Senate may also resume consideration of 
the bankruptcy bill. Therefore, Senators can expect votes during 
tomorrow's session.
```

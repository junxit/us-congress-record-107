# PERMISSION TO FILE CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 2883, INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2002

```
    PERMISSION TO FILE CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 2883, INTELLIGENCE 
                 AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2002

  Mr. GOSS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the managers may 
have until midnight, December 6, 2001, to file a conference report on 
the bill (H.R. 2883) to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 2002 
for intelligence and intelligence-related activities of the United 
States Government, the Community Management Account, and the Central 
Intelligence Agency Retirement and Disability System, and for other 
purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaHood). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.
```

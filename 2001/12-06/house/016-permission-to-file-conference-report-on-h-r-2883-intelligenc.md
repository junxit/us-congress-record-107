# PERMISSION TO FILE CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 2883, INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2002

**Speaking:** Goss, Porter J. (G000336) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2883

```
    PERMISSION TO FILE CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 2883, INTELLIGENCE 
                 AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2002

  Mr. GOSS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent on the part of the 
House to have until midnight, December 6, 2001, to file a conference 
report on the bill (H.R. 2883) to authorize appropriations for fiscal 
year 2002 for intelligence and intelligence-related activities of the 
United States Government, the Community Management Account, and the 
Central Intelligence Agency Retirement and Disability System, and for 
other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaHood). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.
```

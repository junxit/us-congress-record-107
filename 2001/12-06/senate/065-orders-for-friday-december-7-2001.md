# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, DECEMBER 7, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, DECEMBER 7, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m., Friday, December 7; that immediately following the prayer and 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate begin consideration of the District of Columbia Appropriations 
conference report.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

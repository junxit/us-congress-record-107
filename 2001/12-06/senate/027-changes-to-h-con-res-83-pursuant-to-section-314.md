           CHANGES TO H. CON. RES. 83 PURSUANT TO SECTION 314

  Mr. CONRAD. Madam President, section 314 of the Congressional Budget 
Act, as amended, requires the chairman of the Senate Budget Committee 
to make adjustments to budget resolution allocations and aggregates for 
amounts designated as emergency requirements pursuant to section 252(e) 
of the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985, as 
amended.
  Pursuant to section 314, I hereby submit the following revisions to 
H. Con. Res. 83 as a result of provisions designated as emergency 
requirements in P.L. 107-42, the Air Transportation Safety and System 
Stabilization Act. This measure was enacted into law on September 22, 
2001.

which reflects the changes made to the allocations provided to the 
Senate Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation and to the 
budget resolution aggregates enforced under section 311(2)(A) of the

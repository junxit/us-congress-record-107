# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. LEVIN. The Senate will convene on Tuesday at 9:30 a.m. with 30 
minutes of closing debate prior to the 10 a.m. rollcall vote on cloture 
on the DOD authorization bill. All second-degree amendments to the DOD 
bill must be filed prior to 9:45 a.m. on Tuesday. The Senate will 
recess from 12:30 to 2:15 p.m. for the weekly party conferences.
```

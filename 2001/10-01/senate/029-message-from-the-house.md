# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 2510

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                          enrolled bill signed

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2001, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on October 1, 2001, during the recess of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 2510. An act to extend the expiration date of the 
     Defense Production Act of 1950, and for other purposes.

  The enrolled bill was signed subsequently by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Byrd).

                          ____________________
```

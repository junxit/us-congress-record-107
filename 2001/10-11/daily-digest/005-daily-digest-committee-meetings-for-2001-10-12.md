```
                     COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR FRIDAY,

                            OCTOBER 12, 2001

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)

  Committee on Armed Services: to hold a closed briefing with the

``Combating Terrorism: Assessing the Threat of Biological 
Terrorism,'' 10 a.m., 2154 Rayburn.
  Committee on Ways and Means, to mark up the Economic Security and 
Recovery Act of 2001, 9 a.m., 1100 Longworth.
```

# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 1583, H.R. 1860

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Jess Trandahl, Clerk of the House reports that on October 3, 2001 he 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills.

       H.R. 1583. To designate the Federal building and United 
     States courthouse located at 121 West Spring Street in New 
     Albany, Indiana, as the ``Lee H. Hamilton Federal Building 
     and United States Courthouse''.
       H.R. 1860. To reauthorize the Small Business Technology 
     Transfer Program, and for other purposes.
```

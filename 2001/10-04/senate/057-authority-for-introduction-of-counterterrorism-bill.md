# AUTHORITY FOR INTRODUCTION OF COUNTERTERRORISM BILL

```
          AUTHORITY FOR INTRODUCTION OF COUNTERTERRORISM BILL

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that notwithstanding 
the adjournment of the Senate today it be in order for a bipartisan 
counterterrorism bill to be introduced today by Senators Daschle and 
Lott and others and that it be considered as having had its first 
reading, with an objection to the second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Watts, J. C., Jr. (W000210) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 168, H.Con.Res. 170, H.Con.Res. 174

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J.C. WATTS, JR.

                              of oklahoma

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 11, 2001

  Mr. WATTS of Oklahoma. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably detained due to 
a delayed flight arriving into the Ronald Reagan National Airport on 
July 10, 2001, and unfortunately missed the following recorded votes: 
No. 211 on H. Con. Res. 170; No. 212 on H. Con. Res. 168; and No. 213 
on H. Con. Res. 174.
  I ask that the Record reflect that, had I not been delayed, I would 
have voted ``yea'' on all of the above bills.
```

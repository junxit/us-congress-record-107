# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12:15 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate.
  H.R. 2131. An act to reauthorize the Tropical Forest Conservation Act 
of 1998 through fiscal year 2004, and for other purposes.
  The message also announced that the House has agreed to the following 
concurrent resolutions, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:
  H. Con. Res. 168. Concurrent resolution expressing the sense of 
Congress in support of victims of torture.
  H. Con. Res. 170. Concurrent resolution encouraging corporations to 
contribute to faith-based organizations.
  H. Con. Res. 174. Concurrent resolution authorizing the Rotunda of 
the Capitol to be used on July 26, 2001, for a ceremony to present 
Congressional Gold Medals to the original 29 Navajo Code Talkers.

                          ____________________
```

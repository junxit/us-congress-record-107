# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, on Thursday, the Senate will convene at 9 
a.m. and resume consideration of the motion to proceed to the House 
Bankruptcy Reform Act, with 3 hours for debate prior to a cloture vote 
on the motion to proceed.
  Following consideration of the bankruptcy act on Thursday, the Senate 
will resume consideration of the Interior appropriations bill with a 
vote in relation to Nelson of Florida amendment No. 893.
  At 11:30 a.m., the Senate will swear in the new Secretary of the 
Senate, Jeri Thomson.

                          ____________________
```

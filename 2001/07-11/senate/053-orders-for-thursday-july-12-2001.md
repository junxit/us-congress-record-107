# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 12, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 333

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 12, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9 o'clock 
a.m., on Thursday, July 12. I further ask consent that on Thursday 
immediately following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of the 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the motion to 
proceed to H.R. 333, the House Bankruptcy Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

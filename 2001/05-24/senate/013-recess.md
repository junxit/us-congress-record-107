# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, Trent 
Lott, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess until the 
hour of 1 o'clock.
  There being no objection, at 12 noon, the Senate recessed until 1:02 
p.m., and reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Mr. Bunning).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from Kentucky, I 
suggest the absence of a quorum. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 25, 2001

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 25, 2001

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 
a.m. on Friday, May 25. I further ask unanimous consent that on Friday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, and the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day and the 
Senate then begin a period for morning business, with Senators speaking 
for up to 10 minutes each, with the following exceptions: Senator 
Durbin, or his designee, 30 minutes; Senator Thomas, or his designee, 
30 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

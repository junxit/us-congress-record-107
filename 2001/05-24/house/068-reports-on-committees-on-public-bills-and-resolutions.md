         REPORTS ON COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as

     from the Committee on Rules (Rept. 107-80). Referred to the 
     House Calendar.
       Mr. REYNOLDS: Committee on Rules. House Resolution 150. 
     Resolution waiving a requirement of clause 6(a) of rule XIII 
     with respect to consideration of certain resolutions reported 
     from the Committee on Rules (Rept. 107-81). Referred to the 
     House Calendar.
       Mr. YOUNG of Alaska: Committee on Transportation and 
     Infrastructure. H.R. 1140. A bill to modernize the financing

# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Larson, John B. (L000557) [D-CT]

**Measures:** H.R. 1, H.R. 1836

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN B. LARSON

                             of connecticut

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 24, 2001

  Mr. LARSON of Connecticut. Mr. Speaker, for Roll Call Vote No. 145, 
on final passage of H.R. 1, I was present in the Chamber and engaged in 
the debate on this bill as indicated by my previous vote on the Motion 
to Recommit (Roll Call Vote No. 144) and subsequent vote on the Motion 
to Instruct Conferees on H.R. 1836. Although I intended to vote ``aye'' 
on final passage of this bill, my vote was not registered. I ask 
unanimous consent that this statement appear immediately after Roll 
Call Vote No. 145 in the permanent Congressional Record.

                          ____________________
```

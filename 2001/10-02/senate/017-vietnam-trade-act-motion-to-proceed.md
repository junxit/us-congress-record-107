# VIETNAM TRADE ACT--MOTION TO PROCEED

```
                  VIETNAM TRADE ACT--MOTION TO PROCEED

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I move to proceed to calendar No. 154, H.J. 
Res. 51, the Vietnam trade bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A joint resolution (H.J. Res. 51), approving the extension 
     of nondiscriminatory treatment with respect to the products 
     of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is on agreeing to the motion.
  The motion was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```

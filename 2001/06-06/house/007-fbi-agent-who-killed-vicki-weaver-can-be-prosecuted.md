# FBI AGENT WHO KILLED VICKI WEAVER CAN BE PROSECUTED

```
[[Page 9947]]

          FBI AGENT WHO KILLED VICKI WEAVER CAN BE PROSECUTED

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, a Federal court ruled that the FBI agent 
that killed Vicki Weaver can be prosecuted. Finally, a Federal court 
with some anatomy. Check the facts. The Department of Justice once 
again investigating the Justice Department once again concluded that 
Agent Horiuchi accidentally shot Mrs. Weaver. Accident, my BVDs. Vicki 
Weaver was shot stone cold right between the eyes while holding her 
infant child.
  Mr. Speaker, the FBI is beginning to look more and more like the KGB. 
I yield back the fact that if the FBI and Justice Department were not 
guilty at Ruby Ridge, why did they pay Randy Weaver $3 million and his 
wounded friend, Kevin Harris, $400,000 to shut them up? Think about it.
```

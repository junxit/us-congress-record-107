# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Sununu). Pursuant to clause 8 of rule 
XX, the Chair announces that he will postpone further proceedings on 
each motion to suspend the rules on which a recorded vote or the yeas 
and nays are ordered, or on which the vote is objected to under clause 
6 of rule XX.
  Any recorded votes on postponed questions will be taken after debate 
has concluded on all motions to suspend the rules.
```

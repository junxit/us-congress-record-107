# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Ros-Lehtinen, Ileana (R000435) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.Res. 157

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Ms. ROS-LEHTINEN. Madam Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 157, I 
move that the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Hon. John 
Joseph Moakley.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 6 o'clock and 43 minutes 
p.m.), pursuant to House Resolution 157, the House adjourned until 
tomorrow, Thursday, June 7, 2001, at 10 a.m. in memory of the late Hon. 
John Joseph Moakley of Massachusetts.
```

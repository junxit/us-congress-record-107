# CONGRATULATING DETROIT ON THE TRICENTENNIAL

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 80

```
              CONGRATULATING DETROIT ON THE TRICENTENNIAL

  Mr. DASCHLE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Judiciary Committee be discharged from consideration of H. Con. Res. 80 
and the Senate then proceed to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection it is so ordered. The clerk 
will report the concurrent resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 80) congratulating 
     the city of Detroit and its residents on the occasion of the 
     tricentennial of the city's founding.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. DASCHLE. I ask unanimous consent that the concurrent resolution 
and preamble be agreed to en bloc and the motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table with no intervening action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 80) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```

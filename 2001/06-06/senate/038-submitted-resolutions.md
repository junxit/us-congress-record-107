# SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

  SENATE RESOLUTION 100--TO ELECT ROBERT C. BYRD, A SENATOR FROM THE 
 STATE OF WEST VIRGINIA, TO BE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE OF 
                           THE UNITED STATES.

  Mr. DASCHLE submitted the following resolution; which was considered 
and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 100

       Resolved, That Robert C. Byrd, a Senator from the State of 
     West Virginia, be, and he is hereby, elected President of the 
     Senate pro tempore, in accordance with rule I, paragraph 1, 
     of the Standing Rules of the Senate.
```

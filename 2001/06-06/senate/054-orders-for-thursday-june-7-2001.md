# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 7, 2001

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

**Measures:** S. 1

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 7, 2001

  Mr. DASCHLE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m., Thursday, June 7. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Thursday, immediately following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 1, the elementary and 
secondary education bill under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

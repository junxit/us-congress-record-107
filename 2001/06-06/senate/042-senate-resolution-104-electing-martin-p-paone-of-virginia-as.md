# SENATE RESOLUTION 104--ELECTING MARTIN P. PAONE OF VIRGINIA AS SECRETARY FOR THE MAJORITY OF THE SENATE

```
    SENATE RESOLUTION 104--ELECTING MARTIN P. PAONE OF VIRGINIA AS 
                SECRETARY FOR THE MAJORITY OF THE SENATE

  Mr. DASCHLE submitted the following resolution; which was considered 
and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 104

       Resolved, That Martin P. Paone of Virginia, be, and he is 
     hereby, elected Secretary for the Majority of the Senate, 
     effective June 6, 2001.
```

# SENATE RESOLUTION 101--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 101

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 101--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE 
           ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE

  Mr. DASCHLE submitted the following resolution; which was considered 
and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 101

       Resolved, That the House of Representatives be notified of 
     the election of Robert C. Byrd, a Senator from the State of 
     West Virginia, as President pro tempore.
```

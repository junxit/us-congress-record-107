# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 801, H.R. 1727

```
                             NEW PUBLIC LAWS

(For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest of June 5, 2001, p. 
                                 D530)
  H.R. 801, to amend title 38, United States Code, to expand 
eligibility for CHAMPVA, to provide for family coverage and retroactive 
expansion of the increase in maximum benefits under Servicemembers' 
Group Life Insurance, to make technical amendments. Signed on June 5, 
2001. (Public Law 107-14)
  H.R. 1727, to amend the Taxpayer Relief Act of 1997 to provide for 
consistent treatment of survivor benefits for public safety officers 
killed in the line of duty. Signed on June 5, 2001. (Public Law 107-15)
```

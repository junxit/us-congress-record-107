# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 3 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes.
  Under the previous order, the time until 2:30 p.m. shall be under the 
control of the Senator from Illinois or his designee.
  The Senator from Nevada.

                          ____________________
```

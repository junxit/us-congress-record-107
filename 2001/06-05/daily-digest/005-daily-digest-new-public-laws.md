# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 428, H.R. 581, S. 700, H.R. 802, H.R. 1696

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

(For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest of May 14, 2001, p. 
                                 D440)
  S. 700, to establish a Federal interagency task force for the purpose 
of coordinating actions to prevent the outbreak of bovine spongiform 
encephalopathy (commonly known as ``mad cow disease'') and foot-and-
mouth disease in the United States. Signed on May 24, 2001. (Public 
Law107-9)
  H.R. 428, concerning the participation of Taiwan in the World Health 
Organization. Signed on May 28, 2001. (Public Law 107-10)
  H.R. 1696, to expedite the construction of the World War II memorial 
in the District of Columbia. Signed on May 28, 2001. (Public Law 107-
11)
  H.R. 802, to authorize the Public Safety Officer Medal of Valor. 
Signed on May 30, 2001. (Public Law 107-12)
  H.R. 581, to authorize the Secretary of the Interior and the 
Secretary of Agriculture to use funds appropriated for wildland fire 
management in the Department of the Interior and Related Agencies 
Appropriations Act, 2001, to reimburse the United States Fish and 
Wildlife Service and the National Marine Fisheries Service to 
facilitate the interagency cooperation required under the Endangered 
Species Act of 1973 in connection with wildland fire management. Signed 
on June 3, 2001. (Public Law 107-13)
```

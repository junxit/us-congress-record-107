# MAKING IN ORDER MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES ON WEDNESDAY, JUNE 6, 2001

```
MAKING IN ORDER MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES ON WEDNESDAY, JUNE 6, 2001

  Mr. EHLERS. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it be in 
order at any time on the legislative day of Wednesday, June 6, 2001, 
for the Speaker to entertain motions that the House suspend the rules 
relating to the following measures: H.R. 1000, H.R. 37, H.R. 640, H.R. 
1661, H.R. 1209, H.R. 1914, and H. Con. Res. 150.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Biggert). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Michigan?
  There was no objection.
```

# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 12:30 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for not to exceed 10 minutes each.
```

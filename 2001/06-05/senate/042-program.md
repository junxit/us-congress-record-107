# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will convene at 11 a.m. and begin consideration of a few 
housekeeping resolutions which will allow for the transition of power. 
Following the transition, the Senate will resume consideration of the 
Wellstone amendment No. 465. Under the previous order, there will be up 
to 20 minutes of debate with the vote to occur at the expiration of 
that time. Therefore, Senators should expect a vote to occur at 
approximately 11:30 a.m. Following the vote, Senator Collins will be 
recognized to offer an amendment.
```

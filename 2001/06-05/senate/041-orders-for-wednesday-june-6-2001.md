# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 6, 2001

```
                   ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 6, 2001

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 11 
a.m. on Wednesday, June 6. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Wednesday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, and the time 
for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

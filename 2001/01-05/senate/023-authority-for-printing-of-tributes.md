# AUTHORITY FOR PRINTING OF TRIBUTES

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                   AUTHORITY FOR PRINTING OF TRIBUTES

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the tributes to 
Alan Cranston, late Senator of the State of California, be printed as a 
Senate document and that Senators have until Friday, February 9, 2001, 
to submit said tributes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

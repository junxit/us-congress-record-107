# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mrs. MURRAY. I ask unanimous consent the Senate move to a period of 
morning business with Senators permitted to speak for 5 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mrs. MURRAY. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mrs. HUTCHISON. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
order for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mrs. HUTCHISON. Madam President, is the order that we are in morning 
business with Senators allowed to speak for up to 5 minutes?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct.
  Mrs. HUTCHISON. Thank you, Madam President.

                          ____________________
```

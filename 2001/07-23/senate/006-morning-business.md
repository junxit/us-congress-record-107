# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
following exceptions: The Senator from Arizona, Mr. Kyl, from 2 to 3 
p.m., and the Senator from West Virginia, Mr. Byrd, from 3 to 4 p.m.
  The Senator from Arizona, Mr. Kyl.
  Mr. KYL. Thank you, Mr. President.
  When my colleague, the Senator from Idaho, arrives, I will stop my 
presentation and give him an opportunity to join me in our comments 
today. We intend to take this hour to both talk about the same general 
subject.

                          ____________________
```

# MORNING HOUR DEBATES

```
                          MORNING HOUR DEBATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of 
January 3, 2001, the Chair will now recognize Members from lists 
submitted by the majority and minority leaders for morning hour 
debates. The Chair will alternate recognition between the parties, with 
each party limited to 30 minutes, and each Member, other than majority 
and minority leaders and the minority whip, limited to 5 minutes.
  The Chair recognizes the gentleman from Florida (Mr. Stearns) for 5 
minutes.

                          ____________________
```

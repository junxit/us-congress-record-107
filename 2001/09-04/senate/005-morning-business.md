# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 11 a.m. with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes. 
Under the previous order, the time until 10:30 a.m. shall be under the 
control of the Senator from Wyoming, Mr. Thomas, or his designee.
```

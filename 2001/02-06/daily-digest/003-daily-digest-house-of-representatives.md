  90th Birthday of Ronald Reagan: H.J. Res. 7, recognizing the 90th

  Pages H171-78, H185-86

[[Page D99]]

  Pages H178-79
  Contributions of Catholic Schools: H. Res. 28, honoring the

10).
  Pages H180-85, H186-87

# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, today the Senate will be in a period for 
morning business until 12:30 p.m., with Senators Durbin, Daschle, and 
Hutchison in control of the time. By previous consent, at 12:30 p.m. 
the Senate will recess for the weekly party conference meetings. Upon 
reconvening at 2:15 p.m., the Senate will begin consideration of the 
nomination of Robert Zoellick to be U.S. Trade Representative. There 
will be up to 2 hours debate on the nomination, with a vote scheduled 
to occur at 4:15 p.m.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```

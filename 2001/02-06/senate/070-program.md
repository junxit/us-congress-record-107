# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 248

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, on Wednesday, the Senate will be in a period 
of morning business until 1 p.m. under the order. At 1 p.m., the Senate 
will begin consideration of S. 248, the U.N. dues legislation. A 
minimal amount of debate is expected on the legislation. Therefore, it 
is expected that a vote will occur during tomorrow afternoon's session.
  On Thursday, the Senate will begin consideration of the pipeline 
safety bill. I hope the Senate can complete consideration of that bill 
prior to the week's adjournment.

                          ____________________
```

# REPORT ON THE OPERATION OF THE ANDEAN TRADE PREFERENCE ACT--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 3

```
  REPORT ON THE OPERATION OF THE ANDEAN TRADE PREFERENCE ACT--MESSAGE 
                        FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 3

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Finance.

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 203(f) of the Andean Trade Preference Act 
(ATPA) of 1991, as amended (19 U.S.C. 3201 et seq.), I transmit 
herewith the third report to the Congress on the Operation of the 
Andean Trade Preference Act.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, February 5, 2001.

                          ____________________
```

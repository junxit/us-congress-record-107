# ORDER OF BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                           ORDER OF BUSINESS

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, we have had a number of conversations over 
the past several days with Senator McCain and Senator Feingold, with 
the Democratic leadership, Senator McConnell, Senator Hollings, Senator 
Nickles, a whole number of Senators have been involved in this, 
Senators Dodd and Levin, in coming to an agreement on how to proceed on 
the election campaign reform issue. We have come to agreement here that 
everybody seems to be satisfied with at this time. I would like to 
enter this unanimous consent request.
  I actually have three. One is dealing with how to handle the campaign 
finance reform issue. The next one is the Hollings constitutional 
amendment, and then also a consent regarding the U.N. dues and its 
consideration on the floor of the Senate beginning tomorrow.
```

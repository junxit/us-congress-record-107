# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 7

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 7

```
                UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.J. RES. 7

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, when the Senate receives from the House H.J. 
Res. 7 relating to the birthday of former President Ronald Reagan, I 
ask unanimous consent that the resolution be considered read the third 
time and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, and 
that any statements thereon be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

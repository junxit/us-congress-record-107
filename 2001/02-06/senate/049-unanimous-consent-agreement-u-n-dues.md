# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--U.N. DUES

```
                 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--U.N. DUES

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 1 p.m. 
Wednesday, February 7, the Senate proceed to the U.N. dues bill, if 
reported by the Foreign Relations Committee, and all amendments offered 
be relevant to the subject matter and cleared by both managers.
  I further ask consent that if the committee has not reported the bill 
by 1 p.m., it be immediately discharged and the Senate proceed 
immediately to its consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

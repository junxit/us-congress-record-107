# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Biggert). Pursuant to the provisions of 
clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair will reduce to a minimum of 5 minutes 
the period of time within which a vote by electronic device may be 
taken on each additional motion to suspend the rules on which the Chair 
has postponed further proceedings.
```

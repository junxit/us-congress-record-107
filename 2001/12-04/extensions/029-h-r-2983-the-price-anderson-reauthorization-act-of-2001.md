# H.R. 2983, THE PRICE-ANDERSON REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2001

**Speaking:** Barton, Joe (B000213) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2983

```
       H.R. 2983, THE PRICE-ANDERSON REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2001

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE BARTON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 4, 2001

  Mr. BARTON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, in my previous remarks on H.R. 
2983, the Price-Anderson Reauthorization Act of 2001, I stated that 
$187 million had been paid out in response to the accident at Three 
Mile Island. In fact, approximately $70 million has been paid out to 
date, and this amount is well within the plant's primary insurance 
policy required by the Price-Anderson Act.
```

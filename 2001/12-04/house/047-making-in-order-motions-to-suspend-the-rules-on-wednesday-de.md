# MAKING IN ORDER MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES ON WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 5, 2001

MAKING IN ORDER MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES ON WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 5, 
                                  2001

  Mr. NUSSLE. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it be in 
order at any time on the legislative day of Wednesday, December 5, 
2001, for the Speaker to entertain motions that the House suspend the

H. Con. Res. 280, H.R. 3322, H.R. 2238, H.R. 2115 and H.R. 2538.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Iowa?
  There was no objection.

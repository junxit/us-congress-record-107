# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will resume consideration of the DeWine amendment regarding 
advocacy ads tomorrow morning. There will be up to 15 minutes of debate 
prior to a vote at 9:45 a.m. Following that vote, there will be up to 2 
hours on a Harkin amendment on volunteer spending limits. Therefore, a 
second vote will occur before 12 noon on Thursday. Further amendments 
will be offered. Votes will occur throughout the day, and it is the 
intention of the managers and leaders to conclude this bill by tomorrow 
night. Therefore, votes could occur late into the evening tomorrow.

                          ____________________
```

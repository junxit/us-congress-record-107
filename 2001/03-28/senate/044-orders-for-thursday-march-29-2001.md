# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 29, 2001

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

**Measures:** S. 27

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 29, 2001

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Thursday, March 29. I further ask consent that on Thursday, 
immediately

[[Page S3067]]

following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then resume 
consideration of the DeWine amendment to S. 27, the campaign finance 
reform bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

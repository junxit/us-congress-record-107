# EXECUTIVE SESSION

```
                           EXECUTIVE SESSION

                                 ______
                                 

                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
immediately proceed to executive session to consider the following 
nomination reported by the Foreign Relations Committee: Calendar No. 
23, Grant Green. I further ask unanimous consent that the nomination be 
confirmed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, the 
President be immediately notified of the Senate's action, and the 
Senate then return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The nomination considered and confirmed is as follows:


                          department of state

       Grant S. Green, Jr., of Virginia, to be an Under Secretary 
     of State (Management).
```

# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
exceed the hour of 2 p.m. with Senators permitted to speak therein for 
up to 10 minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the time until 10:40 a.m. shall be under 
the control of the Senator from Wyoming or his designee.
```

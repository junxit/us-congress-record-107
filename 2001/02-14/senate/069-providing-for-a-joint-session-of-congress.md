# PROVIDING FOR A JOINT SESSION OF CONGRESS

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 28

```
               PROVIDING FOR A JOINT SESSION OF CONGRESS

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 28, regarding an 
address to Congress by the President of the United States. Further, I 
ask unanimous consent that the resolution be agreed to and the motion 
to reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 28) was agreed to.
```

# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2217, DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2202

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2217, 
  DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 
                                  2202
```

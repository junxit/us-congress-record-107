# CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1594, FOREIGN MILITARY TRAINING RESPONSIBILITY ACT

**Speaking:** McGovern, James P. (M000312) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1594

```
  CONSIDERING MEMBER AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1594, FOREIGN MILITARY 
                      TRAINING RESPONSIBILITY ACT

  Mr. McGOVERN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I might 
hereafter be considered as first sponsor of H.R. 1594, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Moakley of Massachusetts, for 
the purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprints pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Foley). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Massachusetts?
  There was no objection.
```

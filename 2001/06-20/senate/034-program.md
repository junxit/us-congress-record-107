# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, as announced, we are going to convene at 
9:15 a.m. tomorrow. We will have about 10 minutes of debate equally 
divided between the proponents and opponents of this legislation. 
Following the vote on the motion to proceed, there will be 
approximately 2 hours for debate equally divided between the leaders or 
their designees.
  At 12 noon, Senator Lott, or his designee, will be recognized to 
offer an amendment in regard to this legislation, S. 1052.
  As has been indicated several times, we are going to conclude this 
legislation prior to the Fourth of July recess. As indicated, Senators 
are advised and their staffs should be making alternative arrangements 
in case we have to work through the weekend.

                          ____________________
```

# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

**Speaking:** Inouye, Daniel K. (I000025) [D-HI]

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. INOUYE. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the 
Committee on Indian Affairs will meet on June 26, 2001, at 10:30 a.m. 
in room 485 Russell Senate Building to conduct a hearing to receive 
testimony on the goals and priorities of the Great Plains Tribes for 
the 107th session of the Congress.
  Those wishing additional information may contact committee staff at 
202/224-2251.


                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. INOUYE. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the 
Committee on Indian Affairs will meet on June 28, 2001, at 10:00 a.m. 
in room 485 Russell Senate Building to conduct a hearing to receive 
testimony on the goals and priorities of the Montana Wyoming Tribal 
Leaders Council for the 107th session of the Congress.
  Those wishing additional information may contact committee staff at 
202/224-2251.
```

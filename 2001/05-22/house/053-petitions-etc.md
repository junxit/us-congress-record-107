# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       15. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Council of the 
     City of Mansfield, Ohio, relative to Resolution 01-091 
     petitioning the United States Congress to take all actions 
     that are necessary to stop the dumping of foreign steel in 
     the United States, including the amendment of existing 
     foreign trade laws or the enactment of new foreign trade law 
     to address the crisis in the steel industry; to the Committee 
     on Ways and Means.
```

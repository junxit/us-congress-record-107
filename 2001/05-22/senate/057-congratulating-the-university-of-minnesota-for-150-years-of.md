# CONGRATULATING THE UNIVERSITY OF MINNESOTA FOR 150 YEARS OF OUTSTANDING SERVICE TO MINNESOTA

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

**Measures:** S.Res. 93

```
CONGRATULATING THE UNIVERSITY OF MINNESOTA FOR 150 YEARS OF OUTSTANDING 
                          SERVICE TO MINNESOTA

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of S. Res. 93, submitted earlier today by 
Senators Wellstone and Dayton.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 93) congratulating the University of 
     Minnesota, its faculty, staff, students, alumni, and friends 
     for 150 years of outstanding service to the State of 
     Minnesota, the Nation, and the world.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
resolution and preamble be agreed to en bloc, the motion to reconsider 
be laid upon the table, that any statements relating thereto be printed 
in the Record, with no intervening action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 93) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
  (The text of the resolution is located in today's Record under 
``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```

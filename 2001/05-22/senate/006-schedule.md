# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, I announce on behalf of the majority 
leader, today the Senate will resume voting on final amendments to the 
reconciliation bill. Consecutive votes will occur throughout the 
morning and will include final passage of the bill. It is hoped the 
Senate will complete action as soon as possible in order to resume 
consideration of the education bill. There are amendments pending to 
the education bill, and others will be offered during today's session. 
There will be many votes throughout the day, and Senators are 
encouraged to stay in the Senate Chamber during final votes on this tax 
bill.
  On behalf of the majority leader, I thank my colleagues for their 
cooperation.

                          ____________________
```

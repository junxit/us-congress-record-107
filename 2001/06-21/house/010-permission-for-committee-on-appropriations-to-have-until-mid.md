# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT FRIDAY, JUNE 22, 2001, TO FILE REPORT ON DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2002

**Speaking:** Rogers, Harold (R000395) [R-KY]

```
   PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL MIDNIGHT 
 FRIDAY, JUNE 22, 2001, TO FILE REPORT ON DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION 
             AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2002

  Mr. ROGERS of Kentucky. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Appropriations have until midnight tomorrow, June 22, to 
file a privileged report making appropriations for the Department of 
Transportation and Related Agencies for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2002, and for other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Simpson). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Kentucky?
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 1 of rule XXI, all points 
of order are reserved on the bill.
```

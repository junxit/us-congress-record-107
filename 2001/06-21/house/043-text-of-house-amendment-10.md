# Text of House Amendment 10

**Speaking:** Inslee, Jay (I000026) [D-WA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2217

```
H.R. 2217

                         Offered By Mr. Inslee

       Amendment No. 10: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to suspend or revise the final regulations published 
     in the Federal Register on November 21, 2000, that amended 
     part 3809 of title 43, Code of Federal Regulations.
```

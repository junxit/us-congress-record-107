# AMENDMENTS

**Measures:** H.R. 2217

```
                               AMENDMENTS

  Under clause 8 of rule XVIII, proposed amendments were submitted as 
follows:

[[Page H3467]]

                               H.R. 2217

                         Offered By Mr. Inslee

       Amendment No. 10: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to suspend or revise the final regulations published 
     in the Federal Register on November 21, 2000, that amended 
     part 3809 of title 43, Code of Federal Regulations.

                               H.R. 2217

                        Offered By: Mr. Tancredo

       Amendment No. 11: At the end of the bill, add the following 
     section:
       Sec. 332. None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act may be used to fund the National 
     Endowment of the Arts.
```

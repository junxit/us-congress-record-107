# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will be in a period for morning business until 1 p.m. Following 
morning business, the Senate hopes to begin consideration of the 
bankruptcy bill which was reported out today by the Judiciary 
Committee. We will consult with Senators and see if we can find a way 
to proceed to that. We also may consider other nominations that will be 
available for floor action. We believe there will be some who will be 
available, so there is a strong possibility there will be a vote or 
votes tomorrow. We will let the Senators know, after I consult with 
Senator Daschle, exactly when those votes might occur and when the 
business for the week will be completed.
```

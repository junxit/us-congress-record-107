# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 10:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 5 minutes each. Under the previous order, the time 
until 10 a.m. shall be under the control of the Senator from Wyoming, 
Mr. Thomas, or his designee.

                          ____________________
```

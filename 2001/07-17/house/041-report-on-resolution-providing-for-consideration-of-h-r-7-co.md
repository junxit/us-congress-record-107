# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 7, COMMUNITY SOLUTIONS ACT OF 2001

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 7, COMMUNITY 
                         SOLUTIONS ACT OF 2001

  Mr. DREIER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-144) on the resolution (H. Res. 196) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 7) to provide incentives for 
charitable contributions by individuals and businesses, to improve the 
effectiveness and efficiency of government program delivery to 
individuals and families in need, and to enhance the ability of low-
income Americans to gain financial security by building assets, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 7, COMMUNITY 
                         SOLUTIONS ACT OF 2001

  Mr. DREIER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report

[[Page H4071]]

(Rept. No. 107-144) on the resolution (H. Res. 196) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 7) to provide incentives for charitable
```

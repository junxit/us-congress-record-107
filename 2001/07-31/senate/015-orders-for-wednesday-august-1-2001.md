# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 1, 2001

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 1, 2001

  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Wednesday, August 1. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Wednesday, immediately following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of the Agriculture 
supplemental authorization bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

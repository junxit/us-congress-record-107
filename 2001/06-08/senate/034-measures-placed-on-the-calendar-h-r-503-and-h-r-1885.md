# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 503 AND H.R. 1885

```
        MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 503 AND H.R. 1885

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand that the following bills are at 
the desk, having been read for the first time: H.R. 503 and H.R. 1885.
  So I ask unanimous consent that it be in order, en bloc, for these 
two bills to receive a second reading, and then I would object to any 
further consideration of this legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bills will be placed on the calendar.
  Mr. REID. Thank you, Mr. President.
```

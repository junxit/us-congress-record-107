# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3167, GERALD B.H. SOLOMON FREEDOM CONSOLIDATION ACT OF 2001

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3167, GERALD 
             B.H. SOLOMON FREEDOM CONSOLIDATION ACT OF 2001

  Mrs. MYRICK, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-271) on the resolution (H. Res. 277) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3167) to endorse the vision of 
further enlargement of the NATO Alliance articulated by President 
George W. Bush on June 15, 2001, and by former President William J. 
Clinton on October 22, 1996, and for other purposes, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```

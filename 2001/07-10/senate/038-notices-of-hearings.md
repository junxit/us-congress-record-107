# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

**Speaking:** Inouye, Daniel K. (I000025) [D-HI]

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. INOUYE. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the 
Committee on Indian Affairs will meet on July 12, 2001, at 10 a.m. in 
room 485 Russell Senate Building to conduct a hearing to receive 
testimony on the goals and priorities of the member tribes of the 
Montana Wyoming Tribal Leaders Council for the 107th session of the 
Congress.
  Those wishing additional information may contact Committee staff at 
202/224-2251.
```

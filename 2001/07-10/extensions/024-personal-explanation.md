                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DENNIS MOORE

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 10, 2001

record my vote on vote No. 4187, H. Res. 99. This motion to suspend the 
rules adopted a resolution that would urge Lebanon, Syria and Iran to 
push Hezbollah to allow Red Cross staff to visit four Israelis abducted

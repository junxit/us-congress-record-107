# A TIME FOR WAR

**Speaking:** Pence, Mike (P000587) [R-IN]

```
                             A TIME FOR WAR

  (Mr. PENCE asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. PENCE. Mr. Speaker, as was written long ago, there is a time for 
everything, and a season for every activity under heaven; a time to be 
born, and a time to die; a time to tear down, and a time to build; a 
time for war, and a time for peace.
  Tonight, as our American President will again walk the blue carpet of 
this Chamber to lead our Nation in a time of war, my hope and my 
prayer, Mr. Speaker, is that this President will reflect the heart of 
the American people from this Chamber. I know there is a need to plan, 
to prepare, to deploy. But justice is inherently impatient, and so am 
I.
  Mr. Speaker, I pray that the President knows in the defense of our 
Nation, now is the time to tear down. Now is the time for war.

                          ____________________
```

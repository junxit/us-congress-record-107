# PERMISSION TO EXPAND TIME FOR GENERAL DEBATE DURING CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2586, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2002

```
 PERMISSION TO EXPAND TIME FOR GENERAL DEBATE DURING CONSIDERATION OF 
   H.R. 2586, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2002

  Mr. STUMP. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during 
consideration of H.R. 2586 in the Committee of the Whole pursuant to 
the order of September 19, 2001, general debate be enlarged to 2 hours 
equally divided and controlled by the chairman and ranking minority 
member of the Committee on Armed Services.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Whitfield). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Arizona?
  There was no objection.
```

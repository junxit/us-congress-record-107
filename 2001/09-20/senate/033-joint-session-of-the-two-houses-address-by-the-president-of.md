# JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
     JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF 
                           THE UNITED STATES

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will now proceed in body to the House of Representatives.
  At 8:40 p.m., the Senate took a recess subject to the call of the 
Chair for the purpose of attending a joint session with the House of 
Representatives to hear the address by the President of the United 
States.
  Thereupon, the Senate, preceded by the Secretary of the Senate, Jeri 
Thomson; the Assistant Sergeant at Arms, Elizabeth McAlhany; and the 
President pro tempore, proceeded to the Hall of the House of 
Representatives to hear the address by the President of the United 
States.
  (The address delivered by the President of the United States to the 
joint session of the two Houses of Congress appears in today's Record 
under ``Messages from the President.'')

                          ____________________
```

# JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
[[Page 17328]]

     JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF 
                           THE UNITED STATES

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the


Senate will now proceed in body to the House of Representatives.
  Thereupon, at 8:40 p.m., the Senate, preceded by the Secretary of the 
Senate, Jeri Thomson; the Assistant Sergeant at Arms, Elizabeth 
McAlhany; and the President pro tempore, proceeded to the Hall of the 
House of Representatives to hear the address by the President of the 
United States.
  (The address delivered by the President of the United States to the 
joint session of the two Houses of Congress appears in today's Record 
under ``Messages from the President.'')

                          ____________________
```

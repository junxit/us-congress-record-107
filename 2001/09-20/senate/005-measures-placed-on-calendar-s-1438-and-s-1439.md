# MEASURES PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 1438 AND S. 1439

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1438, S. 1439

```
            MEASURES PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 1438 AND S. 1439

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I understand that the following bills are 
at the desk having been read for the first time: S. 1438 and S. 1439. I
```

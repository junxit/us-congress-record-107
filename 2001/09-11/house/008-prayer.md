# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Reverend Gerard Creedon, St. Charles Borromeo Catholic Church, 
offered the following prayer:
  God of peace and life, send Your spirit to heal our country; bring 
consolation to all injured in today's tragedy in New York and 
Washington. Protect us and help our leaders to lead us out of this 
moment of crisis to a new day of peace. Amen.

                          ____________________
```

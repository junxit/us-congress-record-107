# AVENGING TERRORIST ATTACK

```
                       AVENGING TERRORIST ATTACK

  (Mr. GANSKE asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GANSKE. Mr. Speaker, the events of yesterday, September 11, are 
forever seared into our minds as with branding irons. We grieve for the 
victims and their families. We pray for them.
  Let the word go forth to our enemies. We are united in our resolve to 
avenge the deaths of our brothers and sisters, our mothers and fathers, 
our husbands and wives.
  In righteousness, the United States Government will hunt down to the 
ends of the earth the assassins of our innocent kin and strike them 
down.

                          ____________________
```

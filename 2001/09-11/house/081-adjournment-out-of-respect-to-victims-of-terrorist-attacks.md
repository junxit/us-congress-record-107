# ADJOURNMENT OUT OF RESPECT TO VICTIMS OF TERRORIST ATTACKS

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

```
       ADJOURNMENT OUT OF RESPECT TO VICTIMS OF TERRORIST ATTACKS

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that, consistent with 
the language of the joint resolution just passed, when the House 
adjourns on this legislative day, it stand adjourned out of respect to 
the victims of the terrorist attacks.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. All time has expired. Under the previous 
order, the hour of 12:30 having arrived, the Senate stands in recess 
until the hour of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:38 p.m., recessed until 2:15 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Inhofe).
```

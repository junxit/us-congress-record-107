# REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO NATIONAL COMMITTEE ON VITAL AND HEALTH STATISTICS

```
   REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO NATIONAL COMMITTEE ON VITAL AND HEALTH 
                               STATISTICS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to section 
306(k) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 242k), the Chair 
announces the Speaker's reappointment of the following member on the 
part of the House to the National Committee on Vital and Health 
Statistics for a term of 4 years:
  Mr. Jeffrey S. Blair, Albuquerque, New Mexico.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

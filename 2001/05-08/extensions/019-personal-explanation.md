# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Rush, Bobby L. (R000515) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 10

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BOBBY L. RUSH

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

  Mr. RUSH. Mr. Speaker, on rollcall vote No. 96, I inadvertently voted 
no. I am a cosponsor of H.R. 10, the Comprehensive Retirement Security
```

# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2505, HUMAN CLONING PROHIBITION ACT OF 2001

**Measures:** H.Res. 214, H.R. 2505

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2505, HUMAN 
                    CLONING PROHIBITION ACT OF 2001
```

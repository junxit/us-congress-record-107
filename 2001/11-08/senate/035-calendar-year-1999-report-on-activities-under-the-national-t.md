# CALENDAR YEAR 1999 REPORT ON ACTIVITIES UNDER THE NATIONAL TRAFFIC AND MOTOR VEHICLE SAFETY ACT OF 1966, THE HIGHWAY SAFETY ACT OF 1966, AND THE MOTOR VEHICLE INFORMATION AND COST SAVINGS ACT OF 1972--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 55

```
CALENDAR YEAR 1999 REPORT ON ACTIVITIES UNDER THE NATIONAL TRAFFIC AND 
 MOTOR VEHICLE SAFETY ACT OF 1966, THE HIGHWAY SAFETY ACT OF 1966, AND 
  THE MOTOR VEHICLE INFORMATION AND COST SAVINGS ACT OF 1972--MESSAGE 
                       FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 55

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
Transportation.

       To the Congress of the United States:
  I transmit herewith the Department of Transportation's Calendar Year 
1999 reports on Activities Under the National Traffic and Motor Vehicle 
Safety Act of 1966, the Highway Safety Act of 1966, and the Motor 
Vehicle Information and Cost Savings Act of 1972.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, November 8, 2001.
```

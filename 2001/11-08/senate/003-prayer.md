# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The guest Chaplain, Elder Francis Cree, offered the following prayer:
  [Speaking Chippewa]
  Great Spirit of God, we want to thank You for this wonderful day You 
have given us, for all the many good things You have blessed us with. 
You have also given us this love and respect and unity and faith in 
God. And we ask You, at this time, that You bless the President, and 
all his employees, and all of us here and all over the world. We thank 
You. We thank You, again.
  That is the prayer I said in the Chippewa language.

                          ____________________
```

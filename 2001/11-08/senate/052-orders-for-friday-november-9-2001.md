# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 9, 2001

```
                          ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, 
                            NOVEMBER 9, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 a.m. 
Friday, November 9, that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with Senators 
permitted to speak for 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

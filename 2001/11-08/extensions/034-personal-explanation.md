                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CHRISTOPHER SHAYS

                             of connecticut

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, November 8, 2001

  Mr. SHAYS. Mr. Speaker, on November 6, I was in Connecticut 
participating in our local elections and, therefore, missed three 
recorded votes.

Congressional Record to reflect that, had I been present, I would have

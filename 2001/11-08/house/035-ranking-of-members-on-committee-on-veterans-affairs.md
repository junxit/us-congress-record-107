# RANKING OF MEMBERS ON COMMITTEE ON VETERANS' AFFAIRS

```
          RANKING OF MEMBERS ON COMMITTEE ON VETERANS' AFFAIRS

  Mr. FROST. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 282) and I ask 
unanimous consent for its immediate consideration in the House.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 282

       Resolved, That Mr. Lynch of Massachusetts shall rank after 
     Mr. Shows of Mississippi on the Committee on Veterans' 
     Affairs.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```

# SENATE RESOLUTION 155--ELECTING DAVID J. SCHIAPPA OF MARYLAND AS SECRETARY OF THE MINORITY OF THE SENATE

```
   SENATE RESOLUTION 155--ELECTING DAVID J. SCHIAPPA OF MARYLAND AS 
                SECRETARY OF THE MINORITY OF THE SENATE

agreed to:

                              S. Res. 155
```

# SENATE RESOLUTION 155--ELECTING DAVID J. SCHIAPPA OF MARYLAND AS SECRETARY OF THE MINORITY OF THE SENATE

```
   SENATE RESOLUTION 155--ELECTING DAVID J. SCHIAPPA OF MARYLAND AS 
                SECRETARY OF THE MINORITY OF THE SENATE

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to:

                              S. Res. 155

       Resolved, That David J. Schiappa of Maryland be, and he is 
     hereby, elected Secretary for the Minority of the Senate 
     effective August 29, 2001.
```

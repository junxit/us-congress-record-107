# Daily Digest/House of Representatives

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 208

```
                        House of Representatives

Chamber Action
  The House was not in session. Pursuant to the order of the House of 
Thursday, August 2, the House stands adjourned until noon on Monday, August 
6, 2001, unless it sooner has received a message from the Senate 
transmitting its concurrence in H. Con. Res. 208. In which case, the House 
shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution for the August 
District Work Period and will reconvene on Wednesday, September 5 at 2 p.m.
```

# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 15, 2001

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 15, 2001

  Mr. JEFFORDS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10:30 
a.m. on Tuesday, May 15. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Tuesday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then resume consideration of the Murray amendment as under the 
previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. JEFFORDS. Mr. President, further, I ask unanimous consent that 
the Senate stand in recess from the hours of 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. 
for the weekly policy conferences to meet.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

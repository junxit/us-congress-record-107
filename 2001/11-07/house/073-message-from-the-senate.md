# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 2944

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Monahan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed with an

[[Page H7908]]

amendment in which the concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of 
the House of the following title:

       H.R. 2944. An act making appropriations for the government 
     of the District of Columbia and other activities chargeable 
     in whole or in part against the revenues of said District for 
     the fiscal year ending September 30, 2002, and for other 
     purposes.

  The message was announced that the Senate insist, upon its amendment 
to the bill (H.R. 2944) entitled ``An Act making appropriations for the 
government of the District of Columbia and other activities chargeable 
in whole or in part against the revenues of said District for the 
fiscal year ending September 30, 2002, and for other purposes,'' 
requests a conference with the House on the disagreeing votes of the 
two Houses thereon, and appoints Ms. Landrieu, Mr. Durbin, Mr. Reed, 
Mr. Inouye, Mr. DeWine, Mrs. Hutchison, and Mr. Stevens, to be the 
conferees on the part of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```

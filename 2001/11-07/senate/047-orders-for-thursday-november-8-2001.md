# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 8, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 8, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 
a.m. on Thursday, November 8; that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and that the Senate begin consideration of the 
intelligence authorization bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

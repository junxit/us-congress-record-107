# THANKING SENATOR ALLEN

```
                         THANKING SENATOR ALLEN

  Ms. MIKULSKI. Madam President, I would like to take this opportunity 
to thank Senator Allen for his generosity and his collegiality.
  As one of the displaced Hart people, he very graciously offered 
facilities in his own office to welcome my staff. It was a bridge 
across the Potomac, hopefully a little bit less expensive than the 
Woodrow Wilson Bridge. Now we are working together on the capital 
region security plan. I express in this time this is what bipartisan 
collegiality is all about.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 4, 2001

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 4, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 a.m., 
Tuesday, December 4; that immediately following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate begin consideration of the 
Transportation appropriations conference report; further, that the 
Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. for the weekly party 
conferences, and that the time be charged against cloture on the 
Daschle substitute amendment, and that the time during the adjournment 
of the Senate also be charged against cloture.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

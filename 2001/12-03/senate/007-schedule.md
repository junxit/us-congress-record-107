# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, the Senate will be in a period for 
morning business until 4:45 p.m., with the time equally divided between 
the two leaders or their designees. At 4:45 p.m., the Senate will 
resume consideration of the Railroad Retirement Act. There will be 30 
minutes of debate prior to a 5:15 p.m. cloture vote on the Lott 
amendment. If cloture is not invoked on the Lott amendment, a second 
cloture vote will occur on the Daschle substitute amendment. As a 
reminder, all second-degree amendments must be filed prior to 4:15 p.m.

                          ____________________
```

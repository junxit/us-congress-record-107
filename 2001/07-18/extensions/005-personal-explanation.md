                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                           HON. DAVID VITTER

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 17, 2001

  Mr. VITTER. Mr. Speaker, due to a flight cancellation on Tuesday, 
July 17, 2001, I was unable to be present for the following Roll Call 
Votes: # 229, the vote on S. 360, a Bill to Honor Paul Coverdell; and # 
230, the vote on H. Res. 195, Commending the United States military and 
defense contractor personnel responsible for a successful in-flight 
ballistic missile defense interceptor test on July 14, 2001.

``yea'' on Roll Call # 229 and ``yea'' on Roll Call # 230.

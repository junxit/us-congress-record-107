# Text of House Amendment 11

**Speaking:** Conyers, John, Jr. (C000714) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2506

```
H.R. 2506

                        Offered By: Mr. Conyers

       Amendment No. 11: Page 112, after line 22, insert the 
     following:


 prohibition on aerial spraying efforts to eradicate illicit crops in 
                                colombia

       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act under 
     the heading ``Department of State--international narcotics 
     control and law enforcement'' or ``Department of State--
     andean counterdrug initiative'' may be used for aerial 
     spraying efforts to eradicate illicit crops in Colombia.
```

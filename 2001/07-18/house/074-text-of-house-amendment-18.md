# Text of House Amendment 18

**Speaking:** English, Phil (E000187) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2506

```
H.R. 2506

                        Offered By: Mr. English

       Amendment No. 18: Page 112, after line 22, insert the 
     following:


  prohibition against export-import bank assistance for foreign steel 
                               production

       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the Export-Import Bank of the United States to 
     provide assistance for the production of steel by any foreign 
     entity.
```

# Text of House Amendment 29

**Speaking:** Miller, Jeff (M001144) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2506

```
H.R. 2506

                   Offered By: Mr. Miller of Florida

       Amendment No. 29: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following:


 prohibition on funds for countries without extradition treaties with 
                           the united states

       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be made available for a country with respect to which a 
     treaty of extradition is not in effect between that country 
     and the United States.
```

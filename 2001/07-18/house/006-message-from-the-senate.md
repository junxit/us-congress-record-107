# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed with amendment in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 333. An act to amend title 11, United States Code, and 
     for other purposes.

  The message also announced that the Senate insists upon its amendment 
to the bill (H.R. 333) ``An Act to amend title 11, United States Code, 
and for other purposes,'' requests a conference with the House on the 
disagreeing votes of the two Houses thereon, and appoints: Mr. Leahy, 
Mr. Kennedy, Mr. Biden, Mr. Kohl, Mr. Feingold, Mr. Schumer, Mr. 
Durbin, Mr. Hatch, Mr. Grassley, Mr. Kyl, Mr. DeWine, Mr. Sessions, and 
Mr. McConnell, to be the conferees on the part of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```

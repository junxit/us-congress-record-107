H.R. 2506

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson-Lee of Texas

       Amendment No. 20: In title II of the bill in the item

     ``(increased by $40,000,000)''.

     expenses of the united states agency for international 
     development'', after the first dollar amount, insert the 
     following: ``(decreased by $100,000,000)''.

# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on July 
18, 2001, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       Harvey Pitt, of North Carolina, to be a Member of the 
     Securities and Exchange Commission for a term expiring June 
     5, 2005, which was sent to the Senate on July 10, 2001.
```

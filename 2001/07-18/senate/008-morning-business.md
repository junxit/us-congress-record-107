# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there is 
now a period for the transaction of morning business not to extend 
beyond the hour of 10:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein 
for up to 10 minutes each.
  Also, under the previous order, the time until 10:30 a.m. shall be 
under the control of the Republican leader or his designee.

                          ____________________
```

# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2603

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2603

```
                 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 2603

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent on Monday, September 24, at 12 
noon, the Finance Committee be discharged from further consideration of 
H.R. 2603 and that the Senate then proceed to its immediate 
consideration under the following limitations: That no amendments or 
motions be in order; the debate be limited to 2 hours, with 1 hour 
under the control of Senator Gramm of Texas and 1 hour under the 
control of Senator Baucus or his designee; following the use or 
yielding back of the time, the bill be read a third time and passed, 
and the motion to reconsider be laid on the table, all with no 
intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

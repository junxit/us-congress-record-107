# NOTICE OF HEARINGS/MEETINGS

**Speaking:** Bingaman, Jeff (B000468) [D-NM]

```
                      NOTICE OF HEARINGS/MEETINGS


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a closed hearing has been 
scheduled before the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
  The hearing will take place on Wednesday, September 26, at 9:30 a.m., 
in location to be announced.
  The purpose of the hearing is to receive testimony on critical energy 
infrastructure security and the energy industry's response to the 
events of September 11, 2001.
  Those wishing to submit written statements on this subject should 
address them to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, Attn: 
Deborah Estes, U.S. Senate, Washington, DC 20510.
  For further information, please call Deborah Estes at 202/224-5360.

                          ____________________
```

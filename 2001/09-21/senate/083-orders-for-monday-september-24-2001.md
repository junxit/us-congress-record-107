# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 24, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                 ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 24, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 12 noon, 
Monday, September 24. I further ask unanimous consent that on Monday, 
immediately following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

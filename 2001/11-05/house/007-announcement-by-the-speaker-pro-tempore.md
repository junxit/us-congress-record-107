# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 2311, H.R. 2590, H.R. 2647

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 4 of rule I, the Speaker 
signed the following enrolled bills on Friday, November 2, 2001:
  H.R. 2311, making appropriations for energy and water development for 
the fiscal year ending September 30, 2002, and for other purposes.
  H.R. 2590, making appropriations for the Treasury Department, the 
United States Postal Service, the Executive Office of the President, 
and certain Independent Agencies, for the fiscal year ending September 
30, 2002, and for other purposes.
  H.R. 2647, making appropriations for the Legislative Branch for the 
fiscal year ending September 30, 2002, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```

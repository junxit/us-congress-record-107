# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for not to 
extend beyond the hour of 5:45 p.m., and the time is to be equally 
divided between the two leaders or their designees.
```

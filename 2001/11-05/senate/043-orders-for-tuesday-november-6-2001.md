# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 2001

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the previous 
order regarding the convening hour of the Senate, on Tuesday, November 
6, be changed to 2:15 p.m.; that there be 15 minutes of debate equally 
divided between Senators Daschle and Lott or their designees in 
relation to the Daschle-Kennedy collective bargaining amendment to the 
Labor-HHS Appropriations Act prior to a 2:30 p.m. cloture vote on the 
amendment; further, that the remaining provisions of the previous order 
remain in effect.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mrs. Clinton). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.


                          ____________________
```

                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 77

  At the request of Mr. Daschle, the name of the Senator from New 
Jersey (Mr. Corzine) was added as a cosponsor of S. 77, a bill to amend

sex, and for other purposes.

unaccompanied alien children, and for other purposes.

purposes.

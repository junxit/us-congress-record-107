# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I say that I do not see anyone in the 
Chamber wishing to speak on the Democrat side; I am sure there will be 
somebody shortly. Why not have until 5 o'clock set aside equally 
between the majority and minority for morning business, and at 5 
o'clock Senator Leahy and Senator McConnell will use their time as 
appropriate. I ask unanimous consent that be the order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Nelson of Nebraska). Without objection, it 
is so ordered.
  Mr. KYL. I thank the Senator from Nevada.

                          ____________________
```

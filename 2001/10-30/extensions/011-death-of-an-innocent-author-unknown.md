# DEATH OF AN INNOCENT AUTHOR UNKNOWN

**Speaking:** Stearns, Cliff (S000822) [R-FL]

```
                  DEATH OF AN INNOCENT AUTHOR UNKNOWN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CLIFF STEARNS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 30, 2001

  Mr. STEARNS. Mr. Speaker, I recently submitted to you a poem entitled 
Death of an Innocent. One of my constituents, Elisabeth Cercek from 
Ocala, FL, was nice enough to get this across my desk in hopes that it 
would bring awareness to the problem of drinking and driving. I wanted 
to correct my previous statement which named Elisabeth as the author. 
The writer of Death of an Innocent is unknown.

                          ____________________
```

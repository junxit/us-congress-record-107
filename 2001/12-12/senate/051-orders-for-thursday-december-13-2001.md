# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 13, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 13, 2001

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 a.m., Thursday, 
December 13; that immediately following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the farm bill; 
further, that the live quorum with respect to the cloture motion be 
waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

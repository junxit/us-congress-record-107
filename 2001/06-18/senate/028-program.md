# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, on Tuesday the Senate--as I have talked with 
the minority leader today--will convene at 10 a.m. with a period for 
morning business until 11:30 a.m. If agreement is reached, the Senate 
will begin consideration of the Patients' Bill of Rights on Tuesday at 
11:30 a.m. The Senate, as I said, will recess from 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 
p.m.
```

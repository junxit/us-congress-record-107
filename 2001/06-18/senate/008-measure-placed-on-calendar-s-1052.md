# MEASURE PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 1052

```
                  MEASURE PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 1052

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, there is a bill at the desk due its second 
reading. I now ask unanimous consent that the bill be read a second 
time, but I would object to any further proceedings with respect to the 
bill at this time.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the bill for the 
second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1052) to amend the Public Health Service Act and 
     the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 to 
     protect consumers in managed care plans and other health 
     coverage.

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. There being an objection to any further 
proceedings on the bill, the bill will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```

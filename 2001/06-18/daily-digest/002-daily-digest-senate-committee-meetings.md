CROSS-BORDER TELEMARKETING FRAUD
Committee on Governmental Affairs: On Friday, June 15, Permanent 
Subcommittee on Investigations concluded hearings to examine the nature 
and scope of cross border fraud, focusing on the state of binational 
U.S.-Canadian law enforcement coordination and cooperation and what 
steps can be taken to fight such crime in the future, after receiving 
testimony from Mary Ellen Warlow, Acting Deputy Assistant Attorney 
General, Criminal Division, Department of Justice; Hugh G. Stevenson, 
Associate Director, Planning and Information, Consumer Protection 
Bureau, Federal Trade Commission; and Vermont Attorney General William 
H. Sorrell, Montpelier.

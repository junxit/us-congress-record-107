# REPORT ON U.S. TRADE AND INVESTMENT POLICY TOWARD SUB-SAHARAN AFRICA AND IMPLEMENTATION OF THE AFRICAN GROWTH AND OPPORTUNITY ACT--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 21

```
 REPORT ON U.S. TRADE AND INVESTMENT POLICY TOWARD SUB-SAHARAN AFRICA 
 AND IMPLEMENTATION OF THE AFRICAN GROWTH AND OPPORTUNITY ACT--MESSAGE 
                       FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 21

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Finance.

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 106 of title I of the Trade and Development 
Act of 2000 (Public Law 106-200), I transmit herewith the 2001 
Comprehensive Report of the President on U.S. Trade and Investment 
Policy toward Sub-Saharan Africa and Implementation of the African 
Growth and Opportunity Act.
                                                         George Bush.  
The White House, May 18, 2001.

                          ____________________
```

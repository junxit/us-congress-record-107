                       THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business yesterday, 
Thursday, April 5, 2001, the Federal debt stood at 
$5,772,523,327,634.26, Five trillion, seven hundred seventy-two

thousand, six hundred thirty-four dollars and twenty-six cents.

nine hundred forty-one million.

one hundred fifty million.
  Ten years ago, April 5, 1991, the Federal debt stood at 
$3,468,754,000,000, Three trillion, four hundred sixty-eight billion, 
seven hundred fifty-four million.

$595,781,000,000, Five hundred ninety-five billion, seven hundred 
eighty-one million, which reflects a debt increase of more than $5

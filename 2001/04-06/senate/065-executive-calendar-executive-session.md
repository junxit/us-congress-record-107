# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** DeWine, Mike (D000294) [R-OH]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. DeWINE. In executive session, I ask unanimous consent the Senate 
proceed to consideration of Calendar No. 31: Maj. Gen. Joseph M. 
Cosumano, Jr., to be Lieutenant General, and Tim McClain to be general 
counsel for the Department of Veterans' Affairs.
  I further ask unanimous consent the nominations be confirmed en bloc, 
the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table en bloc, that any 
statements relating to the nominations be printed in the Record, that 
the President be immediately notified, and the Senate then return to 
legislative session.

  The nominations considered and confirmed en bloc are as follows:
```

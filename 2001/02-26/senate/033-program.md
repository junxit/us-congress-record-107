# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. HELMS. Madam President, for the information of all Senators, the

[[Page S1591]]

Senate will be in a period for morning business prior to the 12:30 p.m. 
recess tomorrow. Upon reconvening at 2:15 p.m., the Senate is expected 
to resume morning business for the remainder of the afternoon. Senators 
are reminded to be in the Senate Chamber by 8:30 p.m. to proceed at 
8:40 p.m. to the Hall of the House of Representatives for the 
President's address.
  Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business on Tuesday afternoon, it recess until the hour 
of 8:30 p.m. for the joint session of Congress to hear the President's 
address.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```

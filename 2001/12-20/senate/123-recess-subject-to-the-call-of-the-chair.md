# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess subject to the call of the Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 8:11 p.m., recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair and reassembled at 9:37 p.m. when called to 
order by the President pro tempore.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Nevada is recognized.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 23, 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 23, 2002

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 12 noon, 
Wednesday, January 23, 2002; that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business 
until 12:30 p.m. with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes 
each, with time equally divided between Senators Daschle and Lott or 
their designees; further that the Senate recess from 12:30 to 2:15 for 
the weekly party conferences.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

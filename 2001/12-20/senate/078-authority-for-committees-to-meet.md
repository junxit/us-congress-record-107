# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      Committee on Armed Services

  Mr. HARKIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Armed Services be authorized to meet during the session of the 
Senate on Thursday, December 20, 2001, at 11:30 a.m., in executive 
session to consider a civilian nomination and pending military 
nominations.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


           Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation

  Mr. HARKIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Commerce, Science, and Transportation be authorized to meet on 
Thursday, December 20, 2001, at 9:30 a.m., on the nomination of John 
Magaw to be Undersecretary of Transportation Security, (DOT).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

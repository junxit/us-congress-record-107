# MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS

```
                MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to H.J. Res. 79, the continuing resolution, which is at the 
desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A joint resolution (H.J. Res. 79) making further continuing 
     appropriations for the fiscal year 2002, and for other 
     purposes.


[[Page 27898]]


  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the joint 
resolution.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the joint 
resolution be read a third time, passed, and the motion to reconsider 
be laid upon the table, without intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The joint resolution (H.J. Res. 79) was read the third time and 
passed.

                          ____________________
```

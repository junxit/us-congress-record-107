                        ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, December 20, 
2001, she had presented to the President of the United States the 
following enrolled bill:

       S. 1438. An act to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 
     2002 for military activities of the Department of Defense, 
     for military construction, and for defense activities of the 
     Department of Energy, to perscribe personnel strengths for 
     such fiscal year for the Armed Forces, and for other 
     purposes.

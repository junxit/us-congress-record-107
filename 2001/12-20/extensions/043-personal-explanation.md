                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. XAVIER BECERRA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 19, 2001

Suspend the Rules and Pass H.R. 3054, the True American Heroes Act.

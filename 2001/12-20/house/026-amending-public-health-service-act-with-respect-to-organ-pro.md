# AMENDING PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICE ACT WITH RESPECT TO ORGAN PROCUREMENT ORGANIZATIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 3504

```
 AMENDING PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICE ACT WITH RESPECT TO ORGAN PROCUREMENT 
                             ORGANIZATIONS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question of 
suspending the rules and passing the bill, H.R. 3504.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Florida (Mr. Bilirakis) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill, H.R. 3504.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the bill was passed.
```

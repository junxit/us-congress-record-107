# AUTHORIZING SPEAKER TO APPOINT MEMBER TO PERMANENT SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE NOTWITHSTANDING SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

```
AUTHORIZING SPEAKER TO APPOINT MEMBER TO PERMANENT SELECT COMMITTEE ON 
           INTELLIGENCE NOTWITHSTANDING SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that until the day 
the House convenes for the second session of the 107th Congress, the 
Speaker, pursuant to clause 11 of rule X and clause 11 of rule I, and 
notwithstanding the requirement of clause 11(a)(1) of rule X, may 
appoint a Member to the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence to 
fill the existing vacancy thereon.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

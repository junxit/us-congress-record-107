# APPOINTMENT OF HON. TOM DAVIS OR HON. WAYNE T. GILCHREST TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS UNTIL HOUSE CONVENES FOR SECOND SESSION OF 107TH CONGRESS

```
  APPOINTMENT OF HON. TOM DAVIS OR HON. WAYNE T. GILCHREST TO ACT AS 
SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS UNTIL 
          HOUSE CONVENES FOR SECOND SESSION OF 107TH CONGRESS

  The Speaker pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                December 20, 2001.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Tom Davis or, if not 
     available to perform this duty, the Honorable Wayne T. 
     Gilchrest to act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled 
     bills and joint resolutions until the day the House convenes 
     for the second session of the 107th Congress.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. DASCHLE. Madam President, with this request having now been 
agreed to, the Senate will not be in session on Friday, as I have 
announced. On Monday, the Senate will convene at 1 p.m. with a period 
for morning business. There will be no rollcall votes on Monday. 
Rollcall votes will occur on Tuesday afternoon and throughout the 
remainder of the week as the Senate begins consideration of the 
Patients' Bill of Rights.
```

# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1052

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

**Measures:** S. 1052

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1052

  Mr. DASCHLE. Madam President, I understand that S. 1052, introduced 
earlier today by Senators McCain, Edwards, and Kennedy, is at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1052) to amend the Public Health Service Act and 
     the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 to 
     protect consumers in managed care plans and other health 
     coverage.

  Mr. DASCHLE. Madam President, I now ask for its second reading and 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be read a second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```

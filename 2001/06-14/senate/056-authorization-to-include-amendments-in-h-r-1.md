# AUTHORIZATION TO INCLUDE AMENDMENTS IN H.R. 1

```
             AUTHORIZATION TO INCLUDE AMENDMENTS IN H.R. 1

  Mr. DASCHLE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent, 
notwithstanding passage of H.R. 1, on previously agreed-upon amendments 
where language was affected by amendments agreed upon later, that it be 
in order for these amendments to be included in the bill as previously 
was the intent of the two managers.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, DECEMBER 14, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, DECEMBER 14, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., Friday, 
December 14; that immediately following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the farm bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

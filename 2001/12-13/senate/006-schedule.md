# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, pursuant to the order entered last 
evening, there will be 90 minutes of debate equally divided and 
controlled in the usual form on the Bond amendment prior to a vote in 
relation to that amendment. There will be no intervening amendment in 
order prior to that vote.
  The majority leader also announced last night that, after having 
filed a cloture motion on this legislation, there would be a cloture 
vote on that matter either today or tomorrow, whatever the two leaders 
work out. There will be votes throughout the day, and we will await 
further word from the leader as to what is going to transpire this 
evening.

                          ____________________
```

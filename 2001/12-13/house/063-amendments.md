# AMENDMENTS

```
                               AMENDMENTS

  Under clause 8 of rule XVIII, proposed amendments were submitted as 
follows:

                               H.R. 3129

                       Offered By: Mr. Traficant

       Amentment No. 1: Sec. __. No funds appropriated in this Act 
     may be made available to any person or entity that violates 
     the Buy American Act (41 U.S.C. 10a-10c).

                               H.R. 3129

                        Offered By Mr. Traficant

       Amendment No. 2: Sec.   . None of the funds made available 
     by this Act may be used to award a contract to a person or 
     entity whose bid or proposal reflects that the person or 
     entity has violated the Act of March 3, 1933 (41 U.S.C. 10a-
     10c, popularly known as the ``Buy American Act'').

             CONGRESSIONAL RECORD 

                United States
                 of America
```

# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 11:30 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

```
                 ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 11:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, is there further business to come before the 
Senate at this time?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, if there is no 
further business to come before the Senate, the Senate stands adjourned 
until 11:30 a.m. tomorrow.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 3:03 p.m., adjourned until Tuesday, January 
23, 2001, at 11:30 a.m.
```

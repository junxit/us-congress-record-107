# ORDER FOR RECORD TO REMAIN OPEN

**Speaking:** Hutchison, Kay Bailey (H001016) [R-TX]

```
                    ORDER FOR RECORD TO REMAIN OPEN

  Mrs. HUTCHISON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Record remain open until 5 o'clock today for the purpose of adding 
original cosponsors to those bills introduced during today's session 
and, further, that Senators have until 5 o'clock to submit legislation 
and for committees to report executive matters.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

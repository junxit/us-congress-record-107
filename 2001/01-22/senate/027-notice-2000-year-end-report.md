# NOTICE--2000 YEAR END REPORT

```
                      NOTICE--2000 YEAR END REPORT

  The mailing and filing date of the 2000 Year End Report required by 
the Federal Election Campaign Act, as amended, is Wednesday, January 
31, 2001. Principal campaign committees supporting Senate candidates 
file their reports with the Senate Office of Public Records, 232 Hart 
Building, Washington, D.C. 20510-7116.
  The Public Records office will be open from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on 
the filing date to accept these filings. For further information, 
please contact the Public Records office at (202) 224-0322.

                          ____________________
```

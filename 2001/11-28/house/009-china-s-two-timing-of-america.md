# CHINA'S TWO-TIMING OF AMERICA

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
                     CHINA'S TWO-TIMING OF AMERICA

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, reports say that China is two-timing 
Uncle Sam big time. With one hand China slaps Uncle Sam on the back and 
wishes us well in Afghanistan. With the other hand China sells missiles 
and weapons to Iran and Iraq and continues to funnel support under the 
table to the Taliban. Bottom line, China continues to aid and abet our 
enemies.
  Beam me up.
  I yield back the fact that the Taliban are a fly on our face, but 
China is a dragon eating our assets. Think about that.

                          ____________________
```

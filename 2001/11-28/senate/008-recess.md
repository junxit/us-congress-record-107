# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. CLELAND. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess until 2:15 today.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:19 p.m., recessed until 
2:15 p.m. and reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Ms. Stabenow).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as the Senator from Michigan, I 
suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BAUCUS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```

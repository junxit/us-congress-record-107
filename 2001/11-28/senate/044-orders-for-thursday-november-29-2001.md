# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 29, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 10

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 29, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9 
a.m. tomorrow, Thursday, November 29; that following the prayer and 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the 
motion to proceed to H.R. 10, with 60 minutes of debate, beginning at 9 
a.m., prior to the cloture vote, equally divided between the two 
leaders or their designees, with the mandatory quorum being waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

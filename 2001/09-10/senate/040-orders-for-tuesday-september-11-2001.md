# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 11, 2001

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 11, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent when the Senate 
completes its business today it adjourn until the hour of 10 a.m. on 
Tuesday, September 11. I further ask unanimous consent that on Tuesday, 
immediately following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate resume consideration of the Commerce, State, Justice 
Appropriations Act; further, that the Senate recess from 12:30 until 
2:15 p.m. for our weekly party conferences.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

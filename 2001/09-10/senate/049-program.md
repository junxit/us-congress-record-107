# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, on Tuesday, the Senate will convene at 10 
a.m. and resume consideration of the Commerce, State, Justice act. We 
hope we can have a time certain for filing of amendments. We hope to 
complete the bill tomorrow. There will be rollcall votes throughout the 
day. The Senate will recess from 12:30 a.m. until 2:15 p.m. for our 
party conferences.

                          ____________________
```

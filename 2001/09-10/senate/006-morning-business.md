# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of routine morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 12 noon with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  Also under the previous order, the time until 11:30 a.m. will be 
under the control of the Senator from Wyoming, Mr. Thomas, or his 
designee. Under the previous order, the time until 12 noon will be 
under the control of the Senator from Illinois, Mr. Durbin, or his 
designee.

                          ____________________
```

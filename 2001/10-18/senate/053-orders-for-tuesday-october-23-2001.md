# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, OCTOBER 23, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, OCTOBER 23, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 a.m., 
Tuesday, October 23; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the motion to 
proceed to the Foreign Operations Appropriations Act, with 30 minutes 
of debate equally divided between the chairman and ranking member, or 
their designees, prior to a 10 a.m. cloture vote on the motion to 
proceed.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

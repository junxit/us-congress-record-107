# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1564

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1564

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand that S. 1564, introduced 
earlier today by Senator Reid of Nevada, is at the desk, and I ask for 
its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the first 
time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1564) to convey land to the University of Nevada 
     at Las Vegas Research Foundation for a research park and 
     technology center.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading and object 
to my own request on behalf of the minority.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will remain at the desk.

                          ____________________
```

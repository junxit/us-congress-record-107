# A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING WILLIAM E. CHANEY

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
              A PROCLAMATION RECOGNIZING WILLIAM E. CHANEY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 13, 2001

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, I commend the following article to my 
colleagues:


       Whereas, William E. Chaney currently serves as president of 
     the Ohio Hills Health Services' Board of Trustees; and,
       Whereas, through Mr. Chaney's twenty-five years of 
     leadership and unselfish commitment the families of eastern 
     Ohio have received prompt, courteous, and affordable health 
     care; and,
       Whereas, due to his tremendous contributions to the Ohio 
     Hills Health Services organization and the community he will 
     be honored by the Ohio Hills Health Services' Board of 
     Trustees; and,
       Whereas, I ask that my colleagues join me in recognizing 
     William E. Chaney for his commitment and dedication to making 
     lives better in our area. I am honored to call him a 
     constituent.

     

                          ____________________
```

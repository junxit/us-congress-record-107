# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 320

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

**Measures:** S. 320

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 320

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 2 p.m. on 
Wednesday, the Senate proceed to the consideration of S. 320, regarding 
technical changes to patent and copyright laws. Further, I ask 
unanimous consent that no amendments or motions be in order and that 
there be up to 1 hour of debate equally divided in the usual form; and 
following the use or yielding back of time, the bill be read a third 
time and the Senate proceed to vote on passage, with no intervening 
action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

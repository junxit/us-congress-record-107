# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 12:30 p.m.
  Under the previous order, the time until 11 a.m. shall be under the 
control of the Democratic leader or his designee.
  Mr. NICKLES. I thank the Chair.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from New York.
  Mrs. CLINTON. I yield myself 15 minutes of the time controlled by the 
Democrats.
```

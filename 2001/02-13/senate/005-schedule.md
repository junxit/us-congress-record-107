# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, today the Senate will be in a period of 
morning business until 12:30 p.m. At 12:30, the Senate will recess for 
the weekly party conferences. When the Senate reconvenes at 2:15 p.m., 
there will be an additional period for morning business. This afternoon 
the Senate may begin consideration of any executive or legislative 
items available for action. Senators will be notified as votes are 
scheduled for the week.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.
```

# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, NOVEMBER 19, 2001, OR ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 27, 2001, AT 10:30 A.M.

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 85

```
 RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, NOVEMBER 19, 2001, OR ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TUESDAY, 
                    NOVEMBER 27, 2001, AT 10:30 A.M.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent the Senate stand in 
adjournment under the provisions of S. Con. Res. 85.
  There being no objection (and the House having subsequently agreed to 
S. Con. Res. 85), the Senate, at 2:51 p.m., adjourned until, Tuesday, 
November 27, 2001, at 10:30 a.m.
```

# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 2873

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 2873

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 2873 is at the desk 
and is due for a second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The leader is correct.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent H.R. 2873 be read 
for a second time, and I then object to do any further proceedings at 
this time.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the bill by 
title for the second time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2873) to extend and amend the program entitled 
     Promoting Safe and Stable Families under title IV-B, subpart 
     2, of the Social Security Act, and to provide new authority 
     to support programs for mentoring children of incarcerated 
     parents; to amend the Foster Care Independent Living program 
     under title IV-E of that Act to provide for educational and 
     training vouchers for youths aging out of foster care, and 
     for other purposes.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection having been heard, the 
bill will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```

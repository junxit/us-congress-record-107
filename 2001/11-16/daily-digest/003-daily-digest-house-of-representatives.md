Supporting Armed Forces and Civilian Personnel Engaged in the War on 
Terrorism: The House agreed to H. Res. 284, encouraging the people of 
the United States to support the Armed Forces and civilian personnel 
who are engaged in the war on terrorism as part of a united effort to 
be known as Operation Enduring Support. Earlier, agreed to an amendment 
to the resolving clause.

William L. Beatty Federal Building and United States Courthouse, Alton,

Courthouse.''
  Pages H8323-24

the sense of Congress regarding the crash of American Airlines Flight 
587.

enrolled bills and joint resolution through November 27, 2001.

House.

# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON GOVERNMENT REFORM AND OVERSIGHT

```
   ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON GOVERNMENT REFORM AND OVERSIGHT

  Mr. FROST. Mr. Speaker, I offer a resolution (H. Res. 292) and I ask 
unanimous consent for its immediate consideration in the House.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Fossella). The Clerk will report the 
resolution.
  The Clerk read as follows:

                              H. Res. 292

       Resolved, That the following named Members, be, and is 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committee of the 
     House of Representatives:
       Committee on Government Reform: Mr. Lynch of Massachusetts, 
     to rank after Ms. Watson of California.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```

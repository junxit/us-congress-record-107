# APPOINTMENT OF HON. THOMAS E. PETRI TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH NOVEMBER 27, 2001

```
[[Page H8328]]
 APPOINTMENT OF HON. THOMAS E. PETRI TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO 
  SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH NOVEMBER 27, 2001

  The Speaker pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:


                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, November 16, 2001.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Thomas E. Petri to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through November 27, 2001.
                                                J. Dennis Hastert,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is agreed 
to.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

# TRIBUTE TO WILLIAM F. HIZNAY

```
                      TRIBUTE TO WILLIAM F. HIZNAY

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. JAMES A. TRAFICANT, JR.

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 16, 2001

  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, as the Representative of the citizens of 
the 17th Congressional District of Ohio, it brings me great pleasure to 
pay tribute to William F. Hiznay, as he receives the rank of Eagle 
Scout.
  Ryan is a member of Boy Scout Troop #44 in Poland. He is not only an 
outstanding young man and dedicated Eagle Scout, but he is also a 
dedicated student at Youngstown State University majoring in 
Engineering.
  I join with the citizens of my district in saluting William F. 
Hiznay, and I wish him the best of luck in all his future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```

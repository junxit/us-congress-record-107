# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BARON P. HILL

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 16, 2001

  Mr. HILL of Indiana. Mr. Speaker, on October 16, 2001, due to a 
momentary failure of the House bells system, I missed one vote on the 
House floor.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``yes'' on roll call vote 393 
to pass H.R. 2217, a bill making appropriations for the Department of 
the Interior and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 
30, 2002, and for other purposes.
```

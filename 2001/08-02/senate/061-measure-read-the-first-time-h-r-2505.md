# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 2505

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 2505

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I understand H.R. 2505 is at the desk, and 
I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2505) to amend title 18, United States Code, 
     to prohibit human cloning.

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask for its second reading and object to 
my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```

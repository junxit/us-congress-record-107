# SENATE RESOLUTION 149--ELECTING ALFONSO E. LENHARDT OF NEW YORK AS THE SERGEANT OF ARMS AND DOORKEEPER OF THE SENATE

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 149--ELECTING ALFONSO E. LENHARDT OF NEW YORK AS THE 
             SERGEANT OF ARMS AND DOORKEEPER OF THE SENATE

  Mr. DASCHLE submitted the following resolution; which was considered 
and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 149

       Resolved, That Alfonso E. Lenhardt of New York be, and he 
     is hereby, elected Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the 
     Senate effective September 4, 2001.
```

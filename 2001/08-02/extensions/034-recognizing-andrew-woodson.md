# RECOGNIZING ANDREW WOODSON

```
                       RECOGNIZING ANDREW WOODSON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ERIC CANTOR

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, August 2, 2001

  Mr. CANTOR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a remarkable young 
man and his contributions to the seventh district of Virginia. Andrew 
Woodson has been a servant of the people, tackling any challenge handed 
him during his service in my Washington, DC office.

[[Page 16211]]

  Andrew cares about the people of the district, and it shows in his 
dedication and perseverance. Mr. Speaker, Andrew has been a remarkable 
addition to the office and his service is appreciated.
  Andrew will be leaving Capitol Hill to pursue his law degree at the 
University of Virginia. Mr. Speaker, I hope you will join me in wishing 
Andrew Woodson luck at UVA and to thank him for his hard work and 
dedication during his service to the seventh district.

                          ____________________
```

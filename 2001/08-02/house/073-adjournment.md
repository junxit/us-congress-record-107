# ADJOURNMENT

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. HAYWORTH. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  Accordingly, pursuant to the previous order of the House of today, 
the House adjourned until noon on Monday, August 6, 2001, unless it 
sooner has received a message from the Senate transmitting its 
concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 208, in which case the House 
shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent resolution.
  Thereupon (at midnight) pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 208, 
the House adjourned under the previous order of the House until noon on 
Wednesday, September 5, 2001, if not sooner in receipt of a message 
from the Senate transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent 
Resolution 208.

                          ____________________
```

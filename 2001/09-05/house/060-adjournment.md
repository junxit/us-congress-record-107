# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Weldon, Curt (W000268) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 234

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. WELDON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 
234, I move the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Honorable 
Floyd Spence.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 11 o'clock and 29 minutes 
p.m.) pursuant to House Resolution 234, the House adjourned until 
tomorrow, Thursday, September 6, 2001, at 10 a.m. in memory of the late 
Honorable Floyd Spence.
```

# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 149

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, we will convene at 10:30 a.m., as indicated 
earlier by Senator Sarbanes. I hope to have a unanimous consent 
agreement for the purpose of disposing of S. 149. Senators should be in 
the Chamber by 10:40 to proceed to the House Chamber for a joint 
meeting with President Fox of Mexico. The Senate will recess from 10:40 
until 12 noon for that joint meeting.
  At 12 noon we will begin reconsideration of the Export Administration 
Act. There could be rollcall votes

[[Page S9128]]

throughout the day. Hopefully, we will complete action on the export 
administration bill and move on to the Commerce-State-Justice bill.
```

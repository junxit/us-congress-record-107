# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 2563

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2563

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 2563

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, it is my understanding H.R. 2563, just 
received from the House, is at the desk. I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2563) to amend the Public Health Service Act, 
     the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974, and the 
     Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to protect consumers in managed 
     care plans and other health coverage.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask now for the second reading of the 
bill, and I object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard.
  The bill will be read the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```

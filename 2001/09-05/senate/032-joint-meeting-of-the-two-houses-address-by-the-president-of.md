# JOINT MEETING OF THE TWO HOUSES--ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED MEXICAN STATES

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
JOINT MEETING OF THE TWO HOUSES--ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED 
                             MEXICAN STATES

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the President 
of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the part of the 
Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the House of 
Representatives to escort the President of the United Mexican States 
into the House Chamber for the joint meeting on Thursday, September 6, 
2001.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

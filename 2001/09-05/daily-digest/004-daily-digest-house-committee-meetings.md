POST-CONFLICT OSCE POLICING
Commission on Security and Cooperation in Europe (Helsinki Commission):

deploy civilian police contingents in post-conflict Organization for 
Security and Cooperation in Europe (OSCE) regions, and to

Colorado,on behalf of the United Nations International Police Task 
Force in Bosnia-Herzegovina; and Robert M. Perito, United States 
Institute of Peace, Washington, D.C.

# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 1, 2001

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 1, 2001

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 a.m., Thursday, 
November 1; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of the Labor-HHS 
Appropriations Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

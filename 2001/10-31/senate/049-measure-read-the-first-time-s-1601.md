# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1601

```
[[Page 21219]]

                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 1601

  Mr. REID. Madam president, I understand that S. 1601, introduced 
earlier today by Senators Reid and Ensign, is at the desk, and I now 
ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the first 
time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1601) to provide for the conveyance of certain 
     public land in Clark County, Nevada, for use as a shooting 
     range.

  Mr. REID. I now ask for its second reading, and I object to my own 
request on behalf of the minority.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard.
  The bill will be read the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```

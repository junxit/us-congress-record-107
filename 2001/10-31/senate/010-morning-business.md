# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for not to 
extend beyond the hour of 10:30 a.m. with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 5 minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the Senator from Alaska is recognized to 
speak for up to 20 minutes.

                          ____________________
```

# TRIBUTE TO WEST GENESEE'S WOMEN'S VARSITY LACROSSE TEAM

```
        TRIBUTE TO WEST GENESEE'S WOMEN'S VARSITY LACROSSE TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JAMES T. WALSH

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 25, 2001

  Mr. WALSH. Mr. Speaker, on Saturday, June 9, 2001, the West Genesee 
Wildcats defeated Bay Shore to win the New York State Class A Women's 
Lacrosse Championship. The Wildcats won the Class A final with a 16-10 
victory over Bay Shore to top off an impressive 22-1 season and a 
dominant playoff run.
  This talented group was guided by this year's All-CNY girls lacrosse 
coach, Bob Elmer, who is now in his second year leading the Wildcats. 
The State Champion Lady Wildcats previously won the Section III 
Championship and Upstate Regional to advance to the State Championship 
game.

[[Page 14610]]

  The Lady Wildcats' star player is none other than the CNY Player of 
the Year, Martha Dwyer. West Genesse is also home to three other CNY 
team members: Chrissy Zaika, Meghan O'Connell and Nicole Motondo. The 
2001 Class A Championship team also includes: Eileen Gagnon, Vanessa 
Bain, Shannon Burke, Laura Corso, Lindsey Shirtz, Kelly Fitzgerald, 
Colleen O'Hara, Milly Yackel, Kelly Kuss, Keri Rubeis, Nelli Nash, 
Katie Kozloski, Carolyn Maurer, Kim Capraro, and Eileen Flynn.
  I am very proud of these young women and wish to celebrate the 
outstanding athletic achievements they have made this season. I am 
equally proud of the coaching staff and wish to join them, as well as 
the parents and other family members, teachers and administrators, in 
extending sincere congratulations for a job well done. This strong 
group of fine young athletes deserves special recognition.

                          ____________________
```

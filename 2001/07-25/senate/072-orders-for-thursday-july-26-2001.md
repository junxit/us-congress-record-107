# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 26, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 26, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 12 noon, 
Thursday, July 26. I further ask consent that on Thursday, immediately 
following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and there be 1 
hour of debate equally divided between Senators Daschle and Lott or 
their designees prior to the 1 p.m. cloture vote on the substitute 
amendment to the Transportation Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

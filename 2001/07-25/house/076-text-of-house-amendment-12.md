# Text of House Amendment 12

**Speaking:** Frelinghuysen, Rodney P. (F000372) [R-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2620

```
H.R. 2620

                     Offered By: Mr. Frelinghuysen

       Amendment No. 12: At the end of the bill, after the last 
     section (before the short title) insert the following new 
     section:
       Sec. ____. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the Department of Veterans Affairs to implement or 
     administer the Veterans Equitable Resource Allocation system.
```

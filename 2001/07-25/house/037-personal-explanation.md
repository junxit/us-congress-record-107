# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** DeLauro, Rosa L. (D000216) [D-CT]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 50

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Ms. DeLAURO. Mr. Speaker, I regret to report that on July 19 I 
inadvertently voted the wrong way during rollcall number 255 on House 
Joint Resolution 50, Disapproval of Normal Trade Relations for China.
  I mistakenly recorded my vote as no. My vote should have been an aye 
for disapproval.
```

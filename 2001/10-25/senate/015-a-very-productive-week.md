# A VERY PRODUCTIVE WEEK

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                         A VERY PRODUCTIVE WEEK

  Mr. REID. Madam President, this has been a very productive week for 
the Senate. We have completed two appropriations bills and the 
counterter
rorism bill. We should feel very good about what we have been able to

important work to do in the short period before Thanksgiving. The
```

# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 1502, S. 1546

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                S. 1502

  At the request of Mr. Jeffords, the name of the Senator from 
Louisiana (Mr. Breaux) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1502, a bill to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow a refundable tax 
credit for health insurance costs for COBRA continuation coverage, and 
for other purposes.


                                S. 1546

  At the request of Mr. Roberts, the name of the Senator from Wyoming 
(Mr. Enzi) was added as a cosponsor of S. 1546, a bill to provide 
additional funding to combat bioterrorism.
```

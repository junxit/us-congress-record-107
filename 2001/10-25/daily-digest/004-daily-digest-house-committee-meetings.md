# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 2590

```
Committee Meetings
MEDICARE-ENDORSED PRESCRIPTION DRUG DISCOUNT CARDS--IMPACT ON SMALL 
BUSINESS
Committee on Small Business: Held a hearing entitled ``Medicare-
Endorsed Prescription Drug Discount Cards and Their Impact on Small 
Business.'' Testimony was heard from Glenn Bower, Director, Department 
of Revenue, State of Illinois; and public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
APPROPRIATIONS--TREASURY/POSTAL SERVICE
Conferees agreed to file a conference report on the differences between 
the Senate and House passed versions of H.R. 2590, making 
appropriations for the Treasury Department, the United States Postal 
Service, the Executive Office of the President, and certain Independent 
Agencies, for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2002.
```

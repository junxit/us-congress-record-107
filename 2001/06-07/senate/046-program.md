# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. Madam President, as has been previously announced by our 
leader, Senator Daschle, there will be no rollcall votes on Friday. And 
as he has also previously stated, the next rollcall votes will occur on 
Monday at 5:15 p.m. I do say to everyone, again, within the sound of my 
voice that we did a pretty good job today of adhering to the 20-minute 
rule. We certainly did not adhere to it completely, but we were quite 
close. We are going to continue next week until people are in the habit 
of voting within 20 minutes.
```

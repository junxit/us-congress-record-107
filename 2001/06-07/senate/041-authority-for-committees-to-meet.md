# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                        subcommittee on seapower

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Subcommittee on Seapower of the Committee on Armed Services be 
authorized to meet during the session of the Senate on Thursday, June 
7, 2001, at 2:00 p.m., in open session to receive testimony regarding 
Navy and Marine Corps equipment for 21st century operational 
requirements, in review of the defense authorization request for fiscal 
year 2002 and the Future Years Defense Program.
  The PRESIDING Officer. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 8, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 8, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10:30 
a.m., on Friday, June 8. I further ask consent that on Friday, 
immediately following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate proceed to a period of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

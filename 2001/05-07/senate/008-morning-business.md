# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Also under the previous order, 
there will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not 
to extend beyond the hour of 2 p.m. with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes. Under the previous order, the time until 
1:30 shall be under the control of the Senator from Alaska, Mr. 
Murkowski.
  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Thank you, Mr. President. I wish you a good afternoon.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 8, 2001

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 8, 2001

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Tuesday, May 8. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Tuesday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of the 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of the Bolton 
nomination as under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

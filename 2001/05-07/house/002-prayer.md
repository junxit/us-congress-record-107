# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Join with me in this psalm of praise.
  I will bless the Lord at all times; in all situations praise will be 
on my lips. For my soul boasts only in the Lord. The poor and the 
humble understand this and rejoice with me.
  Together let us glorify the Lord. In unison we will lift up our 
voices in song. As I searched for the Lord I was found; from all my 
terrors I was set free.
  Look toward the Lord and be radiant; set your faces in the direction 
of His voice. When the helpless call out, the Lord answers and rescues 
them from all their fears.
  The Lord alone will shield the Nation which reverences faithfully His 
holy will. Taste and see that the Lord is good. Happy are those who 
take refuge in Him, now and forever. Amen.

                          ____________________
```

# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
  No committee meetings were held.

Joint Meetings
VETERANS PROGRAMS
Joint Hearing: Senate Committee on Veterans' Affairs concluded joint 
hearings with the House Committee on Veterans' Affairs to review the 
legislative recommendations of certain veterans organizations, after 
receiving testimony from Robert L. Smith, III, Blinded Veterans 
Association, Frederick A. Taylor, Jr., Military Order of the Purple 
Heart, David W. Sommers, Non Commissioned Officers Association, Ronald 
Ziegler, Jewish War Veterans, and Joseph L. Fox, Sr., Paralyzed 
Veterans of America, all of Washington, D.C.
```

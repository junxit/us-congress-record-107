# LEGISLATIVE SESSION

```
                          LEGISLATIVE SESSION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
return to legislative session.
  The Democratic leader.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I will use my leader time under the 
agreement and under the rule of the day. It is my understanding the 
time now will be designated primarily for statements related to the

be made about other issues, but it is my intention to make some remarks
```

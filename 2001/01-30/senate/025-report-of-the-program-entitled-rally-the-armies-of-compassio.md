# REPORT OF THE PROGRAM ENTITLED ``RALLY THE ARMIES OF COMPASSION''-- MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 2

```
  REPORT OF THE PROGRAM ENTITLED ``RALLY THE ARMIES OF COMPASSION''--
                    MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 2

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Finance.

To the Congress of the United States:
  Enclosed please find the blueprint for my program to ``Rally the 
Armies of Compassion.'' I look forward to working with the Congress to 
pass reforms to support the heroic works of faith-based and community 
groups across America.
                                                         George Bush.  
The White House, January 30, 2001.

                          ____________________
```

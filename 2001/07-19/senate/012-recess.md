# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate stand 
in recess until 1:30 p.m. today, and that I be recognized at 1:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:16 p.m., recessed until 1:30 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mrs. 
Lincoln).
```

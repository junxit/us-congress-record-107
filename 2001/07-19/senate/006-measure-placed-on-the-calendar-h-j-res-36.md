# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.J. RES. 36

```
              MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.J. RES. 36

  Mr. REID. Madam President, it is my understanding that there is a 
bill at the desk due its second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the 
resolution by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A joint resolution (H.J. Res. 36) proposing an amendment to 
     the Constitution of the United States authorizing Congress to 
     prohibit the physical desecration of the flag of the United 
     States.

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I object.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the rule, the resolution will 
be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```

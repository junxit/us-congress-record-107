# Text of House Amendment 59

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2506

```
H.R. 2506

                       Offered By: Mr. Traficant

       Amendment No. 59:
       SEC.   . None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to award a contract to a person or entity whose bid 
     or proposal reflects that the person or entity has violated 
     the Act of March 3, 1933 (41 U.S.C. 10a-10c, popularly known 
     as the ``Buy American Act'').
```

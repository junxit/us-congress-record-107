# Text of House Amendment 41

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2506

```
H.R. 2506

                     Offered By: Mr. Brown of Ohio

       Amendment No. 41: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec. ____. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the Export-Import Bank of the United States to 
     guarantee, insure, extend credit, or participate in an 
     extension of credit in connection with the export of any good 
     or service to a company that is under investigation for trade 
     dumping by the International Trade Commission, or is subject 
     to an anti-dumping duty order issued by the Department of 
     Commerce.
```

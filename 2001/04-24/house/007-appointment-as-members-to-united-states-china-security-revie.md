# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO UNITED STATES-CHINA SECURITY REVIEW COMMISSION

```
     APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO UNITED STATES-CHINA SECURITY REVIEW 
                               COMMISSION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 1238(b) of the Floyd D. 
Spence National Defense Authorization Act for fiscal year 2001 (PL 106-
398) and the order of the House of Wednesday, April 4, 2001, the 
Speaker on Thursday, April 5, 2001, appointed the following members on 
the part of the House to the United States-China Security Review 
Commission:
  Mr. Stephen D. Bryen, Maryland;
  Ms. June Teufel Dreyer, Florida; and
  Mr. James R. Lilley, Maryland.

                          ____________________
```

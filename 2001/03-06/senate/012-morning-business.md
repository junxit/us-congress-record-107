# MORNING BUSINESS

```

now be in a period of morning business with Senators speaking for up to 
10 minutes each. I think the distinguished Senator from Illinois is 
going to proceed, and then I shall return to follow him.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Illinois.
  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to speak in 
morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```

# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The hour of 12:30 having arrived, under the 
previous order the Senate will stand in recess until the hour of 2:15 
p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:30 p.m., recessed until 2:15 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Enzi).
```

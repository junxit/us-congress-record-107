# REPORT ON THE 2001 TRADE POLICY AGENDA AND THE 2000 ANNUAL REPORT ON THE TRADE AGREEMENTS PROGRAM--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 11

```
 REPORT ON THE 2001 TRADE POLICY AGENDA AND THE 2000 ANNUAL REPORT ON 
    THE TRADE AGREEMENTS PROGRAM--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 11

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred jointly to the Committees on Appropriations 
and Finance.

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 163 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended (19 
U.S.C 2213), I transmit herewith the 2001 Trade Policy Agenda and 2000 
Annual Report on the Trade Agreements Program.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, March 6, 2001.

                          ____________________
```

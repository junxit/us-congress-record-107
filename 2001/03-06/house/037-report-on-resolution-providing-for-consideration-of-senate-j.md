# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE JOINT RESOLUTION 6, PROVIDING FOR CONGRESSIONAL DISAPPROVAL OF THE RULE SUBMITTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF LABOR RELATING TO ERGONOMICS

```
   REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE JOINT 
   RESOLUTION 6, PROVIDING FOR CONGRESSIONAL DISAPPROVAL OF THE RULE 
      SUBMITTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF LABOR RELATING TO ERGONOMICS

  Mr. LINDER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-9) on the resolution (H. Res. 79) providing for 
consideration of the Senate joint resolution (S.J. Res. 6) providing 
for congressional disapproval of the rule submitted by the Department 
of Labor under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, relating to 
ergonomics, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```

# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Becerra, Xavier (B000287) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 223, H.R. 725, H.R. 834, H.R. 861

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. XAVIER BECERRA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 15, 2001

  Mr. BECERRA. Mr. Speaker, on March 13 and 14, I was unable to cast my 
votes on rollcall votes: No 46 on motion to suspend the rules and pass 
H.R. 834; No. 47 on motion to suspend the rules and pass H.R. 223; No. 
48 on motion to suspend the rules and pass H.R. 725 as amended; and No. 
49 on motion to suspend the rules and pass H.R. 861. Had I been present 
for the votes, I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall votes 46, 47, 48, 
and 49.
```

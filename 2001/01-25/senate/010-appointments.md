# APPOINTMENTS

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President pro 
tempore, and upon the recommendation of the majority leader, pursuant 
to 22 U.S.C. 2761, as amended, appoints the Senator from Alaska (Mr. 
Stevens) as Chairman of the Senate Delegation to the British-American 
Interparliamentary Group conference during the 107th Congress.
  The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 
276h-276k, as amended, appoints the Senator from Alabama (Mr. Sessions) 
as Chairman of the Senate Delegation to the Mexico-U.S. 
Interparliamentary Group conference during the 107th Congress.
  The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 
276d-276g, as amended, appoints the Senator from Alaska (Mr. Murkowski) 
as Chairman of the Senate Delegation to the Canada-U.S. 
InterParliamentary Group conference during the 107th Congress.
```

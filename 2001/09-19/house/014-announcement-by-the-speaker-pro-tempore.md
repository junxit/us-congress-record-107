# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair desires to announce that the 
practice of reserving seats prior to the joint session by placard will 
not be allowed. Members may reserve their seats by physical presence 
only following the security sweep of the Chamber.

                          ____________________
```

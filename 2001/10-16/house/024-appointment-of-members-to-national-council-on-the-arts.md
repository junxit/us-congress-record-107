# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO NATIONAL COUNCIL ON THE ARTS

```
         APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO NATIONAL COUNCIL ON THE ARTS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
National Foundation on the Arts and Humanities Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 
(995)(b)), amended by section 346(e) of Public Law 105-83, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's appointment of the following Members of the 
House to the National Council on the Arts:
```

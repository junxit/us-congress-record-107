  So (two-thirds having voted in favor thereof) the rules were 
suspended and the concurrent resolution, as amended, was agreed to.
  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  The title of the bill was amended so as to read:

       Amend the title so as to read: ``Concurrent resolution 
     recognizing the historic significance of the 50th anniversary 
     of the alliance between Australia and the United States under 
     the ANZUS Treaty, recognizing the strong support provided by 
     Australia to the United States in the aftermath of the 
     terrorist attacks on September 11, 2001, including jointly 
     invoking Article IV of the ANZUS Treaty, which commits both 
     countries to act to meet a common danger, and reaffirming the 
     importance of economic and security cooperation between the 
     United States and Australia.''.

  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

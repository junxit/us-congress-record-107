# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE CAROLYN B. MALONEY, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE CAROLYN B. MALONEY, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Hon. Carolyn B. Maloney, Member of Congress:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                 Washington, DC, October 10, 2001.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker, House of Representatives, Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to formally notify you pursuant 
     to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House that I have received a 
     subpoena for testimony and documents issued by the Supreme 
     Court of New York.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that the subpoena for testimony does not 
     comply with the requirements of Rule VIII.
           Sincerely,
                                               Carolyn B. Maloney,
     Member of Congress.

                          ____________________
```

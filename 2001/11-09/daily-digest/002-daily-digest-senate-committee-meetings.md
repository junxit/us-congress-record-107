# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 28, H.R. 3090

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
BUDGET AND EMERGENCY DEFICIT
Committee on the Budget: On Thursday, November 8, committee ordered 
unfavorably reported S.J. Res. 28, suspending certain provisions of law 
pursuant to section 258(a)(2) of the Balanced Budget and Emergency 
Deficit Control Act of 1985.
ECONOMIC RECOVERY
Committee on Finance: On Thursday, November 8, committee ordered 
favorably reported H.R. 3090, to provide tax incentives for economic 
recovery, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute entitled 
``Economic Recovery and Assistance for American Workers Act.''
```

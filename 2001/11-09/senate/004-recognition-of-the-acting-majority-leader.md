# RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

```
               RECOGNITION OF THE ACTING MAJORITY LEADER

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Nevada.
  Mr. REID. Madam President, there will be no rollcall votes today, as 
was announced last evening by the majority leader. This morning the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business with Senators allowed to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each. As under the direction of the majority 
leader, next week is going to be an extremely busy week. We are going 
to work on the economic stimulus package, and there are a number of 
other pieces of legislation we are going to do our very best to 
complete prior to Thanksgiving.

                          ____________________
```

# VA-HUD APPROPRIATIONS

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK], Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

```
                         VA-HUD APPROPRIATIONS


                             water projects

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, the conference report includes funding 
for water projects in the Ketchikan Borough. While the project will be 
located in the borough, technically the funds would be administered by 
the city of Ketchikan. Does the distinguished ranking member share my 
view that EPA should issue the grant to the city of Ketchikan which has 
agreed to administer the funds?
  Mr. BOND. I agree that EPA should make the funds available to the 
city of Ketchikan, not the borough government.
```

# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO UNITED STATES-CHINA SECURITY REVIEW COMMISSION

```
APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO UNITED STATES-CHINA SECURITY REVIEW COMMISSION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to section 
1238(b) of the Floyd D. Spence National Defense Authorization Act for 
Fiscal Year 2001 (Public Law 106-398), the Chair announces the 
Speaker's appointment of the following member on the part of the House 
to the United States-China Security Review Commission to fill the 
existing vacancy thereon:
  Mr. Larry M. Wortzel, Alexandria, Virginia.
  There was no objection.
```

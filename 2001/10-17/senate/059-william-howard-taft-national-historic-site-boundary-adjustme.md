# WILLIAM HOWARD TAFT NATIONAL HISTORIC SITE BOUNDARY ADJUSTMENT ACT OF 2001

**Measures:** H.R. 1000

```
 WILLIAM HOWARD TAFT NATIONAL HISTORIC SITE BOUNDARY ADJUSTMENT ACT OF 
                                  2001

  The bill (H.R. 1000) to adjust the boundary of the William Howard 
Taft National Historic Site in the State of Ohio, to authorize an 
exchange of land in connection with the historic site, and for other 
purposes, was considered, ordered to a third reading, read the third 
time, and passed.
```

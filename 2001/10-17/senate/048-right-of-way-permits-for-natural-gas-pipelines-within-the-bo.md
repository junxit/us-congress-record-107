# RIGHT-OF-WAY PERMITS FOR NATURAL GAS PIPELINES WITHIN THE BOUNDARY OF THE GREAT SMOKY MOUNTAINS NATIONAL PARK

```
 RIGHT-OF-WAY PERMITS FOR NATURAL GAS PIPELINES WITHIN THE BOUNDARY OF 
                THE GREAT SMOKY MOUNTAINS NATIONAL PARK

  The Senate proceeded to consider the bill (S. 1097) to authorize the 
Secretary of the Interior to issue right-of-way permits for natural gas 
pipelines within the boundary of the Great Smoky Mountains National 
Park.
  The amendment (No. 1903) was agreed to.
  (The amendment is printed in today's Record under ``Amendments 
Submitted and Proposed.'')
  The bill (S. 1097), as amended, was ordered to be engrossed for a 
third reading, was read the third time and passed.
```

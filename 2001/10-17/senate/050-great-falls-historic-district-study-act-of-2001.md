# GREAT FALLS HISTORIC DISTRICT STUDY ACT OF 2001

```
            GREAT FALLS HISTORIC DISTRICT STUDY ACT OF 2001

  The bill (H.R. 146) to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to 
study the suitability and feasibility of designating the Great Falls 
Historic District in Paterson, New Jersey, as a unit of the National 
Park System, and for other purposes, was considered, ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```

# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

subject to the call of the Chair.

to the call of the Chair and reassembled at 12:12 p.m. when called to 
order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Torricelli).
```

# EIGHTMILE RIVER WILD AND SCENIC RIVER STUDY ACT OF 2001

```
        EIGHTMILE RIVER WILD AND SCENIC RIVER STUDY ACT OF 2001

  The bill (H.R. 182) to amend the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act to 
designate a segment of the Eight Mile River in the State of Connecticut 
for study for potential addition to the National Wild and Scenic Rivers 
System, and for other purposes, was considered, ordered to a third 
reading, read the third time, and passed.
```

# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--CLOTURE VOTE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the cloture

leader and the majority leader.
```

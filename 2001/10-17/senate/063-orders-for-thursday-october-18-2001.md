# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 18, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2904

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 18, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m., 
Thursday, October 18; that on Thursday, immediately following the 
prayer and the pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and that there be a period of 
morning business until 10:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak up 
to 10 minutes each; further, at 10:30 a.m. the Senate begin 
consideration of the conference report to accompany H.R. 2904, the 
Military Construction Appropriations Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

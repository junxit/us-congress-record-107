# MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2002

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 69

```
     MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2002

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent the Senate now proceed to H.J. Res. 
69, a 1-week continuing resolution, just received from the House of 
Representatives.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the joint resolution by 
title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A joint resolution (H.J. Res. 69) making further continuing 
     appropriations for the fiscal year 2002, and for other 
     purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to the consideration 
of the joint resolution.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that the joint resolution be 
considered read three times, passed, and the motion to reconsider be 
laid on the table, with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The joint resolution (H.J. Res. 69) was read the third time and 
passed.

```

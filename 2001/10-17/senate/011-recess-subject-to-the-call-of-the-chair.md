# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, having said that, and having told Senators 
what is in store, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate recess 
subject to the call of the Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:13 p.m., recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair and reassembled at 1:32 p.m., when called to 
order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Reid).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader is recognized.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Thank you, Mr. President.

                          ____________________
```

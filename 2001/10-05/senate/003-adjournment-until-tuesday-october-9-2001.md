# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 9, 2001

```
               ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 9, 2001

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands adjourned until the hour of 9:30 a.m. on Tuesday, October 
9, 2001.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10:00 and 29 seconds a.m., adjourned until 
Tuesday, October 9, 2001, at 9:30 a.m.
```

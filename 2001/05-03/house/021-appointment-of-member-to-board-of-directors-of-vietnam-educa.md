# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF VIETNAM EDUCATION FOUNDATION

```
   APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF VIETNAM EDUCATION 
                               FOUNDATION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, pursuant to section 
205(a) of the Vietnam Education Foundation Act of 2000 (P.L. 106-554), 
and upon recommendation of the minority leader, the Chair announces the 
Speaker's appointment of the following Member of the House to the Board 
of Directors of the Vietnam Education Foundation:
  Mr. George Miller of California.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

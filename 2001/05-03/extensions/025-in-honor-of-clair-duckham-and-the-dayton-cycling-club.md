# IN HONOR OF CLAIR DUCKHAM AND THE DAYTON CYCLING CLUB

**Speaking:** Hall, Tony P. (H000074) [D-OH]

```
         IN HONOR OF CLAIR DUCKHAM AND THE DAYTON CYCLING CLUB

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. TONY P. HALL

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 3, 2001

  Mr. HALL of Ohio. Mr. Speaker, April 28 marked the 95th birthday of 
Dayton Cycling Club co-founder Clair Duckham of Dayton, Ohio. Mr. 
Duckham still rides his bike 44 miles every Sunday from his Dayton home 
to Troy, where he dines with his friends, the ``Gray Wolves.''
  2001 marks the 40th anniversary year of the Dayton Cycling Club, 
founded in 1961 by Mr. Duckham and Horace Huffman. Today, the Dayton 
Cycling Club has over 700 members, and schedules rides for almost every 
day of the year.
  I would like to salute Mr. Duckham on his birthday. His energy and 
vitality serve as an inspiration to all.

                          ____________________
```

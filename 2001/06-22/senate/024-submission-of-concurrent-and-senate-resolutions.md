            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were

     solution, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Foreign 
     Relations.
           By Mr. BINGAMAN (for himself, Mr. Daschle, and Mr. 
             Lott):
       S. Con. Res. 54. A concurrent resolution authorizing the 
     Rotunda of the Capitol to be used on July 26, 2001, for a 
     ceremony to present Congressional Gold Medals to the original 
     29 Navajo Code Talkers; considered and agreed to.

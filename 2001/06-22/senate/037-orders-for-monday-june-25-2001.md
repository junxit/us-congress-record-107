# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 25, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 25, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 2 p.m., 
Monday, June 25. I further ask consent that on Monday, immediately 
following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
resume consideration of the Patients' Bill of Rights.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

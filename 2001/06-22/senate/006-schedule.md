# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, on behalf of Senator Daschle, the Senate 
is advised that we will have debate, the time equally divided between 
the two managers of the bill, on the McCain amendment. Following a vote 
on that amendment, we will turn to an amendment offered by the Senator 
from New Hampshire, Mr. Gregg, the manager of the bill. That matter 
will be debated this afternoon. We are going to be in session Monday 
afternoon for purposes of debating this matter, with further action on 
this bill Tuesday and the rest of the week until we complete this 
legislation.
```

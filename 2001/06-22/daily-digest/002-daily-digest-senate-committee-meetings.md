# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Armed Services: Committee concluded hearings on the 
nominations of Alberto Jose Mora, to be General Counsel, and William A. 
Navas, Jr., to be Assistant Secretary for Manpower and Reserve Affairs, 
both of Virginia, both of the Department of the Navy, Diane K. Morales, 
of Texas, to be Deputy Under Secretary for Logistics and Materiel 
Readiness, and Michael W. Wynne, of Florida, to be Deputy Under 
Secretary for Acquisition and Technology, both of the Department of 
Defense, and Steven John Morello, Sr., of Michigan, to be General 
Counsel of the Department of the Army, after the nominees testified and 
answered questions in their own behalf. Ms. Morales was introduced by 
Senator Warner.
```

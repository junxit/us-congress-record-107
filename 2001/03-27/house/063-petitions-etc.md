# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       8. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Council of the 
     City of Knoxville, Tennessee, relative to Resolution No. R-
     90-01 petitioning the United States Congress to amend the 
     Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow for the deduction of 
     state sales taxes in lieu of state and local income taxes; 
     which was referred to the Committee on Ways and Means.
```

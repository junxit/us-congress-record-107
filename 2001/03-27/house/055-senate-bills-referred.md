# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 295, S. 395

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 295. An act to provide emergency relief to small 
     businesses affected by significant increases in the prices of 
     heating oil, natural gas, propane, and kerosene, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Small Business in 
     addition to the Committee on Agriculture for a period to be 
     subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case for 
     consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
       S. 395. An act to ensure the independence and nonpartisan 
     operation of the Office of Advocacy of the Small Business 
     Administration; to the Committee on Small Business.

                          ____________________
```

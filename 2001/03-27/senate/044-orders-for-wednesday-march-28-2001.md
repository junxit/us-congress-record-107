# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 28, 2001

**Speaking:** Thompson, Fred (T000457) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 27

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 28, 2001

  Mr. THOMPSON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:15 
a.m. on Wednesday, March 28. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Wednesday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then resume consideration of the Thompson amendment to S. 27, 
the campaign finance reform bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

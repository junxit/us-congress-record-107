# REPORT OF THE CORPORATION FOR PUBLIC BROADCASTING COVERING CALENDAR YEAR 2000 MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 14

```
  REPORT OF THE CORPORATION FOR PUBLIC BROADCASTING COVERING CALENDAR 
              YEAR 2000 MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 14

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
Transportation.

To the Congress of the United States:
  Pursuant to section 19(3) of the Public Telecommunications Act of 
1992 (Public Law 102-356), I transmit herewith the report of the 
Corporation for Public Broadcasting covering calendar year 2000.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, March 27, 2001.

                          ____________________
```

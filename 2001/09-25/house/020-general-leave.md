# GENERAL LEAVE

**Speaking:** Knollenberg, Joe (K000288) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2944

```
                             GENERAL LEAVE

Members may have 5 legislative days within which to revise and extend 
their remarks on the bill (H.R. 2944) making appropriation for the

in whole or in part against the revenues of said District for the

I be permitted to include tabular and extraneous material on the bill.
```

# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 26, 2001

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 26, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
tomorrow morning, Wednesday, September 26. I further ask that on 
Wednesday, following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of the 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and there be a period for morning business until 10 a.m., 
with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 5 minutes each, with 
the following exceptions: Senator Daschle or designee, 15 minutes; 
Senator Lott or designee, 15 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

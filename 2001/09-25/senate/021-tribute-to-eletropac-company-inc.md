# TRIBUTE TO ELETROPAC COMPANY, INC.

```
                   TRIBUTE TO ELETROPAC COMPANY, INC.

● Mr. SMITH of New Hampshire. Madam President, I rise today to 
pay tribute to Electropac Company, Incorporated, of Manchester, NH, on 
the celebration of their 25th year in business.
  For 25 years, Electropac Company, Incorporated headed by Raymond 
Boissoneau, has provided high quality printed circuit boards to 
businesses in New Hampshire, the United States and worldwide markets. 
The company has constantly invested in the latest technology and 
processes and has produced innovative products for their customers.
  Electropac is one of North America's leading suppliers of printed 
circuit boards with $45 million in annual revenue and currently employs 
more than 400 dedicated team members. Electropac has consistently 
prided itself in their dedication to complete customer satisfaction and 
teamwork.
  I commend the leadership and employees of Electropac for their 
exemplary accomplishments in the business world. The contributions of 
Electropac have been of significant benefit to the citizens of our 
state and have provided economic stimulus and employment opportunities. 
It is truly an honor and a privilege to represent you in the U.S. 
Senate.●

                          ____________________
```

# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed without amendment bills of the 
House of the following titles:

       H.R. 132. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 620 Jacaranda Street in 
     Lanai City, Hawaii, as the ``Goro Hokama Post Office 
     Building.''
       H.R. 395. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 2305 Minton Road in West 
     Melbourne, Florida, as the ``Ronald W. Reagan Post Office of 
     West Melbourne, Florida.''

  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 101-549, the 
Chair, on behalf of the Majority Leader, appoints Josephine S. Cooper, 
of Washington, D.C., to the Board of Directors of the Mickey Leland 
National Urban Air Toxics Research Center, vice Joseph H. Graziano.

                          ____________________
```

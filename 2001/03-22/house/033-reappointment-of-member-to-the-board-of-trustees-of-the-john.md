# REAPPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE JOHN C. STENNIS CENTER FOR PUBLIC SERVICE TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

```
REAPPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE JOHN C. STENNIS 
           CENTER FOR PUBLIC SERVICE TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to section 
114(b) of the John C. Stennis Center for Public Service Training and 
Development Act (2 U.S.C. 1103), the Chair announces the Speaker's 
reappointment of the following Member on the part of the House to the 
Board of Trustees of the John C. Stennis Center for Public Service 
Training and Development for a term of six years:
  Mr. Charles W. ``Chip'' Pickering of Laurel, Mississippi.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MARCH 23, 2001

**Speaking:** Gramm, Phil (G000365) [R-TX]

```
                   ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MARCH 23, 2001

  Mr. GRAMM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 8:45 
a.m. on Friday, March 23. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Friday, immediately

[[Page S2793]]

following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then begin the 
pending Helms amendment and there be 15 minutes for closing remarks, as 
previously ordered.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

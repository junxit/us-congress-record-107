Wednesday, March 21, 2001, the Federal debt stood at 
$5,731,169,100,580.51, five trillion, seven hundred thirty-one billion,

$5,062,251,000,000, five trillion, sixty-two billion, two hundred 
fifty-one million.

$3,446,260,000,000, three trillion, four hundred forty-six billion, two 
hundred sixty million.

$1,982,089,000,000, One trillion, nine hundred eighty-two billion, 
eighty-nine million, which reflects a debt increase of almost $4 
trillion--$3,749,080,100,580.51, three trillion, seven hundred forty-

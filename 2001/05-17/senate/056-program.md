# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRASSLEY. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the reconciliation bill at 9:30 a.m. on Monday. 
There will be approximately 8 hours for final remarks on the bill and 
debate on a few amendments. Under the previous order, the Carnahan 
amendment will be the first vote in a series to begin at 6 p.m. on 
Monday. Senators may expect numerous votes to follow, including final 
passage of H.R. 1836, the tax reconciliation bill.
```

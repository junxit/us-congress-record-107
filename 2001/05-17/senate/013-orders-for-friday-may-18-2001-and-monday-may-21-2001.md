# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 18, 2001, AND MONDAY, MAY 21, 2001

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
       ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 18, 2001, AND MONDAY, MAY 21, 2001

  Mr. GRASSLEY. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes 
its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 a.m. on Friday, May 
18, for a pro forma session only. No business will be conducted during 
Friday's session of the Senate. I further ask that, on Friday, the 
Senate immediately adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on Monday, May 21, and 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
then resume consideration of the reconciliation bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

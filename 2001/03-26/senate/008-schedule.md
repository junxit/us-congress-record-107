# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Thomas, Craig (T000162) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 4

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. THOMAS. Mr. President, today the Senate will be in a period of 
morning business until 12 noon. Following morning business, the Senate 
will resume consideration of the campaign finance reform bill, with 
Senator Wellstone to be recognized to offer an amendment. At 2 p.m. the 
Senate will begin consideration of S.J. Res. 4, a constitutional 
amendment regarding election contributions and expenditures. Debate 
will continue for up to 4 hours, with the vote scheduled at 6 p.m. Any 
votes ordered in relation to the amendments to the campaign finance 
reform bill will be stacked to follow the 6 p.m. vote this evening.
  I thank the Chair.

                          ____________________
```

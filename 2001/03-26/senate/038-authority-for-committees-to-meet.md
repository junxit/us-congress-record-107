# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                      committee on armed services

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Armed Services be authorized to meet during the session of the 
Senate on Monday, March 26, 2001, at 4:30 p.m., in closed session to 
receive a briefing from the Department of Defense on Taiwan's current 
request for purchases or defense articles and defense services from the 
U.S.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

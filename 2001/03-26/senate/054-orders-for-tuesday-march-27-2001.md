# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 27, 2001

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 27

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 27, 2001

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:15 
a.m. on Tuesday, March 27. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Tuesday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then resume consideration of the Hagel amendment to S. 27, the 
campaign finance reform bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, further, I ask unanimous consent the 
Senate stand in recess from the hour of 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. for 
the weekly policy conferences to meet.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

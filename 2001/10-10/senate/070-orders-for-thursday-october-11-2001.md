# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 11, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1447

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 11, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
Thursday, October 11; that on Thursday, immediately following the 
prayer and the pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed to have expired, and the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day; that the Senate 
then resume consideration of S. 1447, the aviation security bill; 
further, that the cloture vote on the Daschle for Carnahan amendment 
No. 1855 occur at 12:45 p.m., with the mandatory quorum under rule XXII 
being waived; further, that Members have until 11:45 a.m. to file 
second-degree amendments to amendment No. 1855.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Andrews, Robert E. (A000210) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 3061

```
H.R. 3061

                        Offered By: Mr. Andrews

       Amendment No. 7: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following:

                TITLE VII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 701. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to implement the final regulations of the Secretary 
     of Education under part 361 of title 34, Code of Federal 
     Regulations, relating to the revision of the definition of 
     the term ``employment outcome'' as such term applies to the 
     vocational rehabilitation services program under title I of 
     the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (66 Fed. Reg. 7250-7258).
```

# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent, on behalf of the 
majority leader, that the Senate now enter into a period for the 
transaction of morning business and Senators be permitted to speak for 
up to 10 minutes each, with the exception of my own statement.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```

# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 26, 2001

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, FEBRUARY 26, 2001

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it 
adjourn until the hour of 12 o'clock noon on Monday, February 26. I 
further ask consent that immediately following the prayer and the 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and immediately following the reading of 
George Washington's Farewell Address by Senator Allen of Virginia, the 
Senate then proceed to a period of morning business until 4 p.m., to be 
divided in the following fashion: First, Senator Murkowski will have 
from the completion of the Farewell Address to approximately 2:30 p.m.; 
Senator Miller, 2:30 p.m. to 3:30 p.m.; Senator Cleland, 3:30 p.m. to 4 
p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Sessions). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```

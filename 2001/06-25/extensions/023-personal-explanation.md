# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Roybal-Allard, Lucille (R000486) [D-CA]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. LUCILLE ROYBAL-ALLARD

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, June 25, 2001

  Ms. ROYBAL-ALLARD. Mr. Speaker, due to an unavoidable meeting on 
Thursday June 21, I was not present for rollcall vote No. 177, on 
Representative Slaughter's amendment to increase funding for the NEA 
and the NEH. I am a strong supporter of both the National Endowment for 
the Arts and the National Endowment for the Humanities, and had I been 
present, I would have voted yea on this important amendment.
```

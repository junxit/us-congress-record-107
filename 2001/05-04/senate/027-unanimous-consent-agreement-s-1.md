# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1

```
                   UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate 
resume consideration of S. 1 at 2 o'clock

[[Page 7195]]

on Monday, and any votes ordered with respect to that legislation occur 
in a stacked sequence Tuesday morning, with 2 minutes prior to each 
vote for explanation.
  I further ask unanimous consent, as in executive session, that the 
Senate proceed to executive session at 4 p.m. on Monday for 
consideration of the Bolton nomination, under the same terms as 
outlined in the consent agreement of May 3, 2001.
  Also as in executive session, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate resumes in session on Tuesday at 9:30, the Senate resume 
executive session, that there be 45 minutes remaining for debate on the 
Bolton nomination, to be equally divided between the chairman and 
ranking minority member of the Foreign Relations Committee and Senator 
Dorgan, and a vote occur on the confirmation of Mr. Bolton at 10:15 on 
Tuesday. That is to be followed by a stacked sequence of votes ordered 
from Friday and Monday's session of the Senate with respect to the 
education bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

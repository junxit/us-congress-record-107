```
       COMMUNICATION FROM PAYROLL COUNSELOR, OFFICE OF THE CHIEF 
                         ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Jack Katz, Payroll Counselor, Office of the Chief 
Administrative Officer:

         Office of the Chief Administrative Officer, House of 
           Representatives,
                                   Washington, DC, March 12, 2001.
     Hon. J. Dennis Hastert,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.

     subpoena for records issued by the Calvert County Department

     rights of the House.
           Sincerely,

                                                Payroll Counselor.
```

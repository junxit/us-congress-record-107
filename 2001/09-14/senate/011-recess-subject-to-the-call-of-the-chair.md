# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I move that the Senate stand in recess 
subject to the call of the Chair.

subject to the call of the Chair and reassembled at 10:16 a.m. when 
called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Bayh).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Bayh). The majority leader is recognized.
```

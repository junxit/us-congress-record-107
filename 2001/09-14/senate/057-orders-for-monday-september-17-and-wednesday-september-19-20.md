# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 17, AND WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 19, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 17, AND WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 19, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. Monday, 
September 17, for a pro forma session only; further, that when the 
Senate adjourns on Monday, it stand in adjournment until 10 a.m. 
Wednesday, September 19. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Wednesday, immediately following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

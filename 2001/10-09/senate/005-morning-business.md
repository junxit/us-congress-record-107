# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the same previous order, 
there will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not 
to extend beyond the hour of 10 a.m. with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 5 minutes each. But under the previous order, the 
Senator from West Virginia, Mr. Byrd, is recognized to speak for up to 
30 minutes.
  The Senator from West Virginia is recognized.
  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, I thank the Chair.

                          ____________________
```

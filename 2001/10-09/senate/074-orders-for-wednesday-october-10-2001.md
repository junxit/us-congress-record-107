# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 10, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1447

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 10, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until 9:30 
a.m., Wednesday, October 10; that on Wednesday, following the prayer 
and the pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day; that the Senate then resume 
consideration of the motion to proceed to S. 1447, the aviation 
security bill; and further, that all time during the adjournment be 
counted under rule XXII.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

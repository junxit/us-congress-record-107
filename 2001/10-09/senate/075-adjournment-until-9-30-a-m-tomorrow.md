# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Res. 169

```
                  ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. REID. Madam President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
adjournment in accordance with S. Res. 169, as a further mark of 
respect to the late majority leader, Senator Mike Mansfield.

Wednesday, October 10, 2001, at 9:30 a.m.
```

# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. DeWINE. Mr. President, tomorrow the Senate will begin the day 
with a period of morning business. At 11 a.m. the Senate will proceed 
to the consideration of the pipeline safety legislation. Relevant 
amendments are in order under a previous agreement, and Senators who 
have amendments are encouraged to inform the managers of that fact. It 
is hoped a vote on final passage can occur as early as tomorrow 
afternoon.
```

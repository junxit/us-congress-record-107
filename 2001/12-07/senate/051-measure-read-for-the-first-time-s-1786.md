# MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--S. 1786

```
                MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--S. 1786

  Mr. REID. I understand S. 1786 introduced earlier today by Senator 
Durbin is at the desk, and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1786) to expand aviation capacity in the Chicago 
     area.

  Mr. REID. I ask for its second reading, and I object to my own 
request on behalf of the minority.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard.
  The bill will be read the second time on the next legislative day.
```

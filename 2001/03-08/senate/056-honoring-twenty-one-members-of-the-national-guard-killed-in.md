# HONORING TWENTY-ONE MEMBERS OF THE NATIONAL GUARD KILLED IN CRASH OF NATIONAL GUARD AIRCRAFT

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 22

```
 HONORING TWENTY-ONE MEMBERS OF THE NATIONAL GUARD KILLED IN CRASH OF 
                        NATIONAL GUARD AIRCRAFT

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Armed 
Services Committee be discharged from further consideration of S. Con. 
Res. 22 and the Senate proceed to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered. The clerk 
will report the concurrent resolution by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 22) honoring the 21 
     members of the National Guard who were killed in the crash of 
     a National Guard aircraft on March 3, 2001, in south-central 
     Georgia.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the resolution 
be agreed to, the preamble be agreed to, the motion to reconsider be 
laid upon the table, and that any statements relating to this 
resolution be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 22) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
  (The text of S. Con. Res. 22 is located in today's Record under 
``Statements on Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```

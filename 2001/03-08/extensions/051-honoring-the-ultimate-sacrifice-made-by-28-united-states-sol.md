# HONORING THE ULTIMATE SACRIFICE MADE BY 28 UNITED STATES SOLDIERS KILLED DURING OPERATION DESERT STORM

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 39

```
   HONORING THE ULTIMATE SACRIFICE MADE BY 28 UNITED STATES SOLDIERS 
                  KILLED DURING OPERATION DESERT STORM

                                 ______
                                 

                               speech of

                          HON. HEATHER WILSON

                             of new mexico

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 27, 2001

  Mrs. WILSON. Mr. Speaker, I support H. Con. Res. 39, a resolution 
that honors the ultimate sacrifice made by 28 United States soldiers 
killed by an Iraqi missile attack on February 25, 1991, during 
Operation Desert Storm, and resolving to support appropriate and 
effective theater missile defense programs.
  I was delayed from making it to the House floor last week and unable 
to record my vote in favor of H. Con. Res. 39 due to airline problems 
and delays.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 2001

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that upon the 
conclusion of Monday's session, the Senate stand adjourned until 12:30 
p.m. on Tuesday, November 6; that on Tuesday, immediately after the 
prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for use later in the day, and the Senate then stand in recess 
until 2:15 p.m.; that the mandatory quorum under rule XXII be waived 
and that the Senators have until 1 p.m. on Tuesday to file second-
degree amendments to the Daschle amendment notwithstanding the recess 
of the Senate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. Madam President, for the information of the Senate, by 
virtue of the agreement just entered, the cloture vote on the Daschle 
amendment will occur at 2:15 p.m. on Tuesday, November 6.

                          ____________________
```

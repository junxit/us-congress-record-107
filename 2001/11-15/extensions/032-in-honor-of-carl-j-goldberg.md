# IN HONOR OF CARL J. GOLDBERG

**Speaking:** Menendez, Robert (M000639) [D-NJ]

```
                      IN HONOR OF CARL J. GOLDBERG

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT MENENDEZ

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 15, 2001

  Mr. MENENDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and pay tribute to 
Carl J. Goldberg. On Friday, November 16, 2001, Mr. Goldberg will be 
the Honoree at the Deborah Hospital Foundation's 15th Annual Children 
of the World Humanitarian Award Dinner-Dance. The event will be held at 
the Sheraton Meadowlands Hotel in East Rutherford, New Jersey.
  Carl Goldberg has enjoyed an extensive and successful real estate 
career that spans over two decades. In 1979, he joined the prestigious 
real estate firm Bertram Associates as a Project Manager for the 
development of single-family homes. While at Bertram, he quickly 
climbed the corporate ranks and became Operating Partner. As Operating 
Partner, he was instrumental in the construction of more than 2,000 
homes throughout New Jersey.
  In 1994, Carl Goldberg left Bertram Associates and founded the 
Roseland Property Company. Since its formation, Roseland has played a 
major role in the development of company communities throughout the 
Northeast. Under Carl Goldberg's guidance, Roseland builds over 1,500 
residential units a year.
  Currently, Carl Goldberg serves as a member of the National 
Association of Homebuilders and is the former President of the 
Community Builders' Association.
  Today, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Carl Goldberg for 
his years of distinguished service on behalf of New Jersey residents.

                          ____________________
```

# AMENDMENTS

```
                               AMENDMENTS

  Under clause 8 of rule XVIII, proposed amendments were submitted as 
follows:

                         DoD Apprpriations Bill

                       Offered By: Mr. Traficant

       Amendment No. 5: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:

     SEC.     . USE OF AMERICAN-MADE STEEL.

       Use of American-Made Steel.--None of the funds appropriated 
     under this Act for the construction or reconstruction of any 
     U.S. military facility or any other construction or 
     reconstruction necessitated by the terrorist attacks of 
     September 11, 2001 may be used to purchase steel which is not 
     manufactured in the United States.
```

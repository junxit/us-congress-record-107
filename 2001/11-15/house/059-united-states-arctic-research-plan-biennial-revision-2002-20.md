# UNITED STATES ARCTIC RESEARCH PLAN BIENNIAL REVISION: 2002-2006-- MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
   UNITED STATES ARCTIC RESEARCH PLAN BIENNIAL REVISION: 2002-2006--
            MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on Science:

To the Congress of the United States:
  Pursuant to the provisions of the Arctic Research and Policy Act of 
1984, as amended (15 U.S.C. 4108(a)), I transmit herewith the seventh 
biennial revision (2002-2006) to the United States Arctic Research 
Plan.
                                                    George W. Bush.    
The White House, November 15, 2001.

                          ____________________
```

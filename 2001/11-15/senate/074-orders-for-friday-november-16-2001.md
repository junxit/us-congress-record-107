# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 16, 2001

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 16, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 a.m. 
tomorrow, Friday, November 16; that following the prayer and pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business 
with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

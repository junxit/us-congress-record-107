# PROVIDING AUTHORITY TO THE FEDERAL POWER MARKETING ADMINISTRATIONS TO REDUCE VANDALISM AND DESTRUCTION OF PROPERTY

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2924

```
 PROVIDING AUTHORITY TO THE FEDERAL POWER MARKETING ADMINISTRATIONS TO 
              REDUCE VANDALISM AND DESTRUCTION OF PROPERTY

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, finally, I ask unanimous consent that the
```

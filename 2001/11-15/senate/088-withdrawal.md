# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
November 15, 2001, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nomination:
       Shirlee Bowne, of Florida, to be a Director of the Federal 
     Housing Finance Board for a term expiring February 27, 2004, 
     which was sent to the Senate on September 14, 2001.
```

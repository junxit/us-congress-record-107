# PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

**Speaking:** Kohl, Herb (K000305) [D-WI], Gregg, Judd (G000445) [R-NH], Graham, Bob (G000352) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 1552, H.R. 2230

```
                        PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. KOHL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Dan Dager, a 
detailee on my staff, be granted the privilege of the floor during the 
Senate's consideration of H.R. 2230, the Agriculture Appropriations Act 
for fiscal year 2002.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. GREGG. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that a member of 
my staff, Nancy Perkins, have floor privileges during the pendency of 
this bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. GRAHAM. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that a member of 
my staff, Shawn Fitzpatrick, be granted the privilege of the floor for 
the duration of the debate on H.R. 1552.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

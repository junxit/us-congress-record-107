# REPORT ON THE SEVENTH BIENNIAL REVISION (2002-2006) TO THE UNITED STATES ARCTIC RESEARCH PLAN--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 59

```
   REPORT ON THE SEVENTH BIENNIAL REVISION (2002-2006) TO THE UNITED 
     STATES ARCTIC RESEARCH PLAN--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 59

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Governmental Affairs.

To the Congress of the United States:
  Pursuant to the provisions of the Arctic Research and Policy Act of 
1984, as amended (15 U.S.C. 4108(a)), I transmit herewith the seventh 
biennial revision (2002-2006) to the United States Arctic Research 
Plan.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, November 15, 2001.

                          ____________________
```

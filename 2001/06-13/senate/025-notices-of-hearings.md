# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


                      Committee on Indian Affairs

  Mr. INOUYE. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the 
Committee on Indian Affairs will meet on June 19, 2001, at 10:00 a.m. 
in room 485 Russell Senate Building to conduct a hearing to receive 
testimony on the goals and priorities of the member tribes of the 
Midwest Alliance of Sovereign Tribes for the 107th session of the 
Congress.
  Those wishing additional information may contact Committee staff at 
202/224-2251.


                      Committee on Indian Affairs

  Mr. INOUYE. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the 
Committee on Indian Affairs will meet on June 21, 2001, at 10:00 a.m. 
in room 485 Russell Senate Building to conduct a hearing on Native 
American Program Initiatives at the College and University Level.
  Those wishing additional information may contact Committee staff at 
202/224-2251.

                          ____________________
```

# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will now resume on questions postponed earlier today.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H.R. 2330, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 2541, by the yeas and nays.

tomorrow.

gentleman from California (Mr. Rohrabacher), which was debated on 
Thursday, November 8, 2001, and on which further proceedings were 
postponed.

electronic voting on the second vote in this series.
```

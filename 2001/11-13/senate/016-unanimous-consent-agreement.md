# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 3090

```
[[Page S11678]]
                      UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I have been discussing the schedule for 
the remainder of the day with the distinguished Republican leader. I 
want to propound a request. It is my understanding that there is an 
agreement with our colleagues, having consulted with the Republican 
leader.
  I ask unanimous consent that at 2:30 today the Senate proceed to 
Calendar No. 223, H.R. 3090, the economic recovery/stimulus legislation 
for debate only until 5 p.m., with no amendments in order during this 
period; that this time be equally divided and controlled between the 
chairman and ranking member of the Finance Committee or their 
designees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DASCHLE. I yield the floor.

                          ____________________
```

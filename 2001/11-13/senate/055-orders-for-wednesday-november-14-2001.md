# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 2001

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 2001

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent when the Senate completes its 
business

[[Page 22277]]

today, it adjourn until the hour of 10:30 a.m. tomorrow, Wednesday, 
November 14; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal or 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of the Economic Recovery and 
Homeland Security Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```

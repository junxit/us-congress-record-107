# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 14, 2001

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent when the Senate completes its 
business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10:30 a.m. tomorrow, 
Wednesday, November 14; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal or proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the Economic 
Recovery and Homeland Security Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

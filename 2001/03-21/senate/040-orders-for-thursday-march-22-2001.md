# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 22, 2001

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 22, 2001

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9 
a.m. on Thursday, March 22. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Thursday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and the Senate then resume consideration of the pending Hatch amendment 
to S. 27, the campaign finance reform bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

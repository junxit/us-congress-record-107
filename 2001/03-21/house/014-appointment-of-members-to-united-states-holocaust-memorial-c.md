# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO UNITED STATES HOLOCAUST MEMORIAL COUNCIL

```
[[Page H1017]]
   APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO UNITED STATES HOLOCAUST MEMORIAL COUNCIL

Law 106-292 (36 U.S.C. 2301), the Chair announces the Speaker's
```

# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO UNITED STATES HOLOCAUST MEMORIAL COUNCIL

```
   APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO UNITED STATES HOLOCAUST MEMORIAL COUNCIL

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to Public 
Law 106-292 (36 U.S.C. 2301), the Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment of the following Members of the House to the United States 
Holocaust Memorial Council:
  Mr. Gilman of New York;
  Mr. LaTourette of Ohio; and
  Mr. Cannon of Utah.
  There was no objection.
```

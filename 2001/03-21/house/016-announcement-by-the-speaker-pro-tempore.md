# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 1099

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8, rule XX, the Chair 
announces that he will postpone further proceedings today on motions to 
suspend the rules on which a recorded vote or the yeas and nays are 
ordered, or on which the vote is objected to under clause 6 of rule XX.
  Any record vote on H.R. 1099, the Coast Guard Personnel and Maritime 
Safety Act of 2001, will be taken tomorrow.
  Record votes on remaining motions to suspend the rules will be taken 
today.

                          ____________________
```

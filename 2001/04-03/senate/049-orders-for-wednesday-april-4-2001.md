# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 4, 2001

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 83

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, APRIL 4, 2001

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader, I ask unanimous 
consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it adjourn 
until the hour of 9 a.m. on Wednesday, April 4. I further ask unanimous 
consent that on Wednesday, immediately following the prayer, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use at a 
later time in the day, and the Senate then resume consideration of H. 
Con. Res. 83, the budget resolution.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 23, S.Res. 27, S.Res. 60

```
                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

       By Mr. HELMS, from the Committee on Foreign Relations, 
     without amendment and with a preamble:
       S. Res. 27: A resolution to express the sense of the Senate 
     regarding the 1944 deportation of the Chechen people to 
     central Asia, and for other purposes.
       S. Res. 60: A resolution urging the immediate release of 
     Kosovar Albanians wrongfully imprisoned in Serbia, and for 
     other purposes.
       S. Con. Res. 23: A concurrent resolution expressing the 
     sense of Congress with respect to the involvement of the 
     Government in Libya in the terrorist bombing of Pan Am Flight 
     103, and for other purposes.
```

  Mr. KUCINICH changed his vote from ``yea'' to ``present.''
  So (two-thirds having voted in favor thereof), the rules were 
suspended and the resolution, as amended, was agreed to.
  The result of the vote was announced as above recorded.
  The title of the resolution was amended so as to read:

       ``A resolution strongly supporting the decision of the 
     United States Government to offer and solicit cosponsorship 
     for a resolution at the 57th Session of the United Nations 
     Human Rights Commission in Geneva, Switzerland, calling upon 
     the People's Republic of China to end its human rights abuses 
     in China and Tibet, and for other purposes.''.

  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

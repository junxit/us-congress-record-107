# SENATE RESOLUTION 130--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ELECTION OF A SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 130

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 130--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE 
                 ELECTION OF A SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

resolution; which was considered and agreed to:
```

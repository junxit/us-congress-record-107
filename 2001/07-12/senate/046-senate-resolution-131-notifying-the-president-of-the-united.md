# SENATE RESOLUTION 131--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF THE ELECTION OF A SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 131--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF 
               THE ELECTION OF A SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

  Mr. DASCHLE (for himself and Mr. Lott) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 131

       Resolved, That the President of the United States be 
     notified of the election of the Honorable Jeri Thomson as 
     Secretary of the Senate.
```

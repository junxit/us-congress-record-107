# SENATE RESOLUTION 129--ELECTING JERI THOMSON AS SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 129

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 129--ELECTING JERI THOMSON AS SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

  Mr. DASCHLE (for himself and Mr. Lott) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 129

       Resolved, That Jeri Thomson be, and she is hereby, elected
```

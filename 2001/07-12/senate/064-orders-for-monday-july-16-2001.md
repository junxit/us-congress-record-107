# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 16, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 16, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m., Monday, July 16. 
I further ask unanimous consent that on Monday, immediately following 
the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate begin 
consideration of the energy and water appropriations bill for debate 
only.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

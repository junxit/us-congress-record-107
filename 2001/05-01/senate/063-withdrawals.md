# WITHDRAWALS

```
                              WITHDRAWALS

  Executive messages transmitted by the President to the Senate on May 
01, 2001, withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nominations:
       Kathleen Q. Abernathy, of Maryland, to be a Member of the 
     Federal Communications Commission for a term of five years 
     from July 1, 2000, which was sent to the Senate on April 30, 
     2001.
       Michael Joseph Copps, of Virginia, to be a Member of the 
     Federal Communications Commission for a term of five years 
     from July 1, 1999, which was sent to the Senate on April 30, 
     2001.
```

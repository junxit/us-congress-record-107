# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 9:30 
cloture vote be postponed to occur at 11 a.m. today.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

period of morning business until 11 a.m. with the time equally divided 
in the usual form.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so
```

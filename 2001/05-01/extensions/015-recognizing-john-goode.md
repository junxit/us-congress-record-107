# RECOGNIZING JOHN GOODE

```
                         RECOGNIZING JOHN GOODE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GEORGE RADANOVICH

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 1, 2001

  Mr. RADANOVICH. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize John Goode for 
receiving the Friend of Fowler Award. The Fowler Chamber of Commerce 
will present the award to John at Flowler's annual Community 
Recognition Banquet.
  While Mr. Goode currently lives in the Bay Area, he remains involved 
in Fowler affairs. John regularly contributes to the Fowler High School 
and Sutter Middle School bands. He

[[Page 6660]]

has also recently endowed a scholarship in memory of his parents to the 
University of California, for which preference is given to Fowler High 
graduates. He plans to host the 40th reunion of the Fowler High School 
Class of 1962 in conjunction with the 2002 Fowler Fall festival.
  John credits many of the values he learned growing up in the Fowler-
area as guides for his career. He has been honored as a ``Forbes Honor 
Roll'' winner for his skills in risk management. He has managed the 
Smith Barney Fundamental Value fund for 10 years. John is currently the 
chairman and chief investment officer of Davis Skaggs Investment 
Management.
  Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize John Goode for his Friend of Fowler 
Award presented by the Fowler Chamber of Commerce. I urge my colleagues 
to join me in wishing John Goode many more years of continued success.

                          ____________________
```

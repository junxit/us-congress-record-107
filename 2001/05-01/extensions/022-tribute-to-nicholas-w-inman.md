# TRIBUTE TO NICHOLAS W. INMAN

**Speaking:** Skelton, Ike (S000465) [D-MO]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO NICHOLAS W. INMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. IKE SKELTON

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 1, 2001

  Mr. SKELTON. Mr. Speaker, it has come to my attention that Nicholas 
Inman, of Marshfield, MO, will be retiring as host of the Webster 
County Opry on May 4, 2001.
  In 1997, as a 15 year old boy, Nicholas had a vision to bring quality 
entertainment and a new tradition of Ozark music to Webster County. 
Although he faced many obstacles and challenges Nicholas' dream has 
grown into a reality, known today as the Webster County Opry. His many 
accomplishments include increasing the Opry membership from eight to 
50, welcoming numerous local celebrities and hosting and producing 
every show in the four year history of the Opry. Nicholas has also 
received and deserved praise from the late Governor Mel Carnahan and 
former First Lady Barbara Bush.
  Mr. Speaker, Nicholas Inman dedicated himself to the establishment 
and growth of the Webster County Opry. The joy he brought to so many is 
indeed commendable. I am certain that the Members of the House will 
join me in wishing him all the best.

                          ____________________
```

# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, MAY 2, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING FORMER MEMBERS OF CONGRESS

```
 AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, MAY 2, FOR 
          THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING FORMER MEMBERS OF CONGRESS

  Mr. HEFLEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may be in 
order on Wednesday, May 2, for the Speaker to declare a recess, subject 
to the call of the Chair, for the purpose of receiving in this Chamber 
former Members of Congress.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Colorado?
  There was no objection.
```

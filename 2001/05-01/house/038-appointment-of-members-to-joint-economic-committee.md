# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO JOINT ECONOMIC COMMITTEE

```
           APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO JOINT ECONOMIC COMMITTEE

1024(a), the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of the following

  Ms. Dunn of Washington;
  Mr. English of Pennsylvania;
  Mr. Putnam of Florida;
  Mr. Stark of California;
  Mrs. Maloney of New York; and
```

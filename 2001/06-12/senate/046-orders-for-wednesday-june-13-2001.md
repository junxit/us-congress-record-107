# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 13, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 13, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9 a.m., 
Wednesday, June 13. I further ask unanimous consent that on Wednesday, 
immediately following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 1, the education 
authorization bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1157, PACIFIC SALMON RECOVERY ACT

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1157, PACIFIC 
                          SALMON RECOVERY ACT

  Mr. LINDER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-99) on the resolution (H. Res. 163) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 1157) to authorize the Secretary of 
Commerce to provide financial assistance to the States of Alaska, 
Washington, Oregon, California, and Idaho for salmon habitat 
restoration projects in coastal waters and upland drainages, and for 
other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
```

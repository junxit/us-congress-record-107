# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO NATIONAL COMMISSION TO ENSURE CONSUMER INFORMATION AND CHOICE IN AIRLINE INDUSTRY

```
   APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO NATIONAL COMMISSION TO ENSURE CONSUMER 
               INFORMATION AND CHOICE IN AIRLINE INDUSTRY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to Section 
228(d)(1) of the Wendell H. Ford Aviation Investment and Reform Act for 
the 21st Century (Public Law 106-181), the Chair announces the 
Speaker's appointment of the following Members on the part of the House 
to the National Commission to Ensure Consumer Information and Choice in 
the Airline Industry:
  Mr. Gerald J. Roper, Illinois;
  Mr. Paul M. Ruden, Virginia.

[[Page H3008]]

  There was no objection.
```

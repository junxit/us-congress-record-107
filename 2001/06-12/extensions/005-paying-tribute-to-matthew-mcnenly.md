# PAYING TRIBUTE TO MATTHEW McNENLY

```
                   PAYING TRIBUTE TO MATTHEW McNENLY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 12, 2001

  Mr. ROGERS of Michigan. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate 
Matthew McNenly of Lansing, Michigan on being awarded a Computational 
Science Graduate Fellowship from the U.S. Department of Energy.
  The Computational Science Graduate fellowship is a rigorous, highly 
competitive program that provides numerous benefits to the fellows in 
return for a complete casework in a scientific or engineering 
discipline, computer science, and applied mathematics.
  McNenly graduated from Howell High School in 1994 and is currently 
attending the University of Michigan pursuing his Ph.D. in Aerospace 
engineering.
  Therefore Mr. Speaker, I respectfully ask my colleagues to join me in 
paying tribute to Matthew McNenly for being awarded a Computational 
Science Graduate Fellowship from the U.S. Department of Energy.

                          ____________________
```

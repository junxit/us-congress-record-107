  Mrs. WILSON. Mr. Speaker, the following graduating high school

community service, and participation in school and civic activities. It 
is my pleasure to be able to recognize these outstanding students for 
their accomplishments. Their parents, their teachers, their classmates, 
the people of New Mexico and I are proud of them.

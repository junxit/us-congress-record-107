# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Kennedy of Minnesota). The Chair would 
again remind all Members that it is not in order to characterize Senate 
action or inaction, to encourage action by the Senate, or refer to 
individual members of the Senate, except with respect to sponsorship of 
bills or amendments.
```

# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, this morning the Senate will resume 
consideration of the ESEA conference report with 2 hours and 30 minutes 
of debate prior to the 12 noon rollcall vote on the conference report.
  Following this vote, we hope to have a vote on cloture on the 
substitute amendment to the farm bill.
  There will be a recess following the cloture vote for the weekly 
party conferences.
  Additional rollcall votes are expected as the Senate continues to 
work on the farm bill.
  It goes without saying that we hope this is our last week here before 
the first of the year.
  We expect other votes throughout the day on the farm bill.

                          ____________________
```

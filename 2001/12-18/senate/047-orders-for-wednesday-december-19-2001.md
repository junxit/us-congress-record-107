# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 19, 2001

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 19, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it recess until the hour of 11:30 a.m. 
tomorrow, Wednesday, December 19; that immediately following the prayer 
and the pledge, the Senate resume consideration of the farm bill; 
further, that the vote on cloture on the substitute amendment occur at 
1:15 p.m.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Weiner, Anthony D. (W000792) [D-NY]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ANTHONY D. WEINER

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Wednesday, May 2, 2001

  Mr. WEINER. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably detained in my district on 
Tuesday, May 1, 2001, and I would like the record to indicate how I 
would have voted had I been present.
  For rollcall vote No. 90, the resolution recognizing the important of 
increasing awareness of the autism spectrum disorder, I would have 
voted ``yea.''
  For rollcall vote No. 91, the resolution supporting a National 
Charter Schools Week, I would have voted ``yea.''
```

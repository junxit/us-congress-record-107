# ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

```
    ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. FROST. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Democratic Caucus, I 
offer a privileged resolution (H.R. 129) and ask for its immediate 
consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                          House Resolution 129

       Resolved, That the following named Members be, and are 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committees of the 
     House of Representatives:
       Committee on Resources: Mr. Miller of California to rank 
     immediately after Mr. Rahall of West Virginia;
       Committee on Science: Mr. Honda of California.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```

# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Tiahrt, Todd (T000260) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 10

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. TIAHRT. Mr. Speaker, today, I was unavoidably detained and missed 
rollcall votes Nos. 92 and 93. Rollcall vote No. 92 was on the rule for 
H.R. 10, ``the Comprehensive Retirement Security and Pension Reform Act 
of 2001. Rollcall vote No. 93 was on approving the Speaker's approval 
of the Journal. Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on both 
the rule on H.R. 10 and on approving the Journal.
```

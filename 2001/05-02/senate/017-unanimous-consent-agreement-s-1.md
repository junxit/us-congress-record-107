# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

**Measures:** S. 1

```
                   UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that all time 
under rule XXII be yielded back and the motion to proceed to S. 1 be 
agreed to. I further ask consent that immediately following the 
reporting of the bill, the Senate then proceed to a period of morning 
business, with Senators to speak up to 10 minutes each. Finally, I ask 
consent that the Senate resume consideration of S. 1 at 9:30 a.m. on 
Thursday morning and Senator Jeffords be recognized at that time to 
offer an amendment to the so-called bipartisan amendment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

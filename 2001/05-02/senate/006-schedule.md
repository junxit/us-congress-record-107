# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. THOMAS. Mr. President, today the Senate will resume the remaining 
hours of the postcloture debate on the motion to proceed on the 
education bill.


                            Charging of Time

  I now ask unanimous consent that the time until 10:30 a.m. be equally 
divided in the usual form and that it be charged accordingly under rule 
XXII.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. THOMAS. The Senate is expected to begin full consideration of the 
bill during today's session. Therefore, amendments will be offered, and 
votes on the amendments are expected. Members will be notified as the 
votes are scheduled. Senators are encouraged to work with the bill 
managers if they intend to offer amendments to the bill.
  I thank my colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```

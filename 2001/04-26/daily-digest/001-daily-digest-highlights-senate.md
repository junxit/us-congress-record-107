was then agreed to.

  Farmer Bankruptcy Extension: Senate passed H.R. 256, to extend for 11 
additional months the period for which chapter 12 of title 11 of the 
United States Code is reenacted, clearing the measure for the 
President.

production of records by the Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations 
of the Committee on Governmental Affairs.

# PLAN COLOMBIA SEMI-ANNUAL OBLIGATION REPORT--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
PLAN COLOMBIA SEMI-ANNUAL OBLIGATION REPORT--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT 
                          OF THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on International Relations and the Committee on 
Appropriations and ordered to be printed.
To the Congress of the United States:
  Pursuant to section 3204(e) of Public Law 106-246, I hereby transmit 
a report detailing the progress of spending by the executive branch 
during the first two quarters of Fiscal Year 2001 in support of Plan 
Colombia.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, July 26, 2001.

                          ____________________
```

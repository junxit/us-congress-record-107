# Text of House Amendment 40

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2620

```
H.R. 2620

                       Offered By: Mr. Traficant

       Amendment No. 40: At the end of the bill (preceding the 
     short title) insert the following new section:
       Sec. ____. No funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available under this Act shall be made available to any 
     person or entity that has been convicted of violating the Buy 
     American Act (41 U.S.C. 10a-10c).
```

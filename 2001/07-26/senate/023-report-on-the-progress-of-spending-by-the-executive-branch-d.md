# REPORT ON THE PROGRESS OF SPENDING BY THE EXECUTIVE BRANCH DURING THE FIRST TWO QUARTERS OF FISCAL YEAR 2001 IN SUPPORT OF PLAN COLOMBIA-- MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 37

```
 REPORT ON THE PROGRESS OF SPENDING BY THE EXECUTIVE BRANCH DURING THE 
  FIRST TWO QUARTERS OF FISCAL YEAR 2001 IN SUPPORT OF PLAN COLOMBIA--
                   MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 37

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Foreign Relations.

To the Congress of the United States:
  Pursuant to section 3204(e) of Public Law 106-246, I hereby transmit 
a report detailing the progress of spending by the executive branch 
during the first two quarters of Fiscal Year 2001 in support of Plan 
Colombia.
                                                      George W. Bush.  
The White House, July 26, 2001.

                          ____________________
```

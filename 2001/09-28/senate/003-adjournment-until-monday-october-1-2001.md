# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, OCTOBER 1, 2001

```
               ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, OCTOBER 1, 2001

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands adjourned until the hour of 12 noon on Monday, October 1.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 o'clock and 41 seconds a.m., adjourned 
until Monday, October 1, 2001, at 12 noon.
```

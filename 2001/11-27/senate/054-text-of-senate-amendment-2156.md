# Text of Senate Amendment 2156

**Speaking:** Gramm, Phil (G000365) [R-TX]

```
  SA 2156. Mr. GRAMM submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 2155 submitted by Mr. Enzi and intended to be proposed to 
the bill (H.R. 1552) to extend the moratorium enacted by the Internet 
Tax Freedom Act through 2006, and for other purposes; which was ordered 
to lie on the table; as follows:

       Strike paragraph (1) of Sec. 6(c) and insert in lieu 
     thereof the following:
       ``(1) Adopting states to transmit.--Upon the 25th State 
     becoming a signatory to the Compact, the adopting States 
     shall transmit a copy of the Compact to Congress.''.

```

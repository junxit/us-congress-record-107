# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 28, 2001

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 28, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10:30 
a.m., Wednesday, November 28; that following the prayer and Pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the motion to 
proceed to H.R. 10; further, that the Senate stand in recess tomorrow 
from 12:30 to 2:15 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

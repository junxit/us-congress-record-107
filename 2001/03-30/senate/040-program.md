# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** S. 27

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. KYL. Madam President, again, on behalf of the leader, for the 
information of all Senators, the Senate will reconvene on Monday and 
resume the campaign reform bill for 30 minutes for closing remarks. 
Under the previous order, the Senate will conduct a rollcall vote on 
passage of S. 27, as amended, at 5:30 p.m. Following that vote, 
Senators should expect additional votes to occur immediately. 
Therefore, a late session can be expected with votes. Also, Members 
should expect votes to be limited to 20 minutes only; therefore, 
Members will have to be prompt for these votes and all votes during the 
week of the budget resolution.
```

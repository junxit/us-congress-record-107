# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, APRIL 2, 2001, AT 5 P.M.

```
             RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, APRIL 2, 2001, AT 5 P.M.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will now 
stand in recess until 5 o'clock p.m. on Monday, April 2, in the year of 
our Lord, 2001.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 4:16 p.m., recessed until Monday, April 2, 
2001, at 5 p.m.
```

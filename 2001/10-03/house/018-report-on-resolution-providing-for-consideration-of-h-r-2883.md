```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2883, 
          INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2002

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 107-228) on the resolution (H. Res. 252) providing for
```

# POMONA VALLEY WORKSHOP'S 35TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Miller, Gary G. (M001139) [R-CA]

```
               POMONA VALLEY WORKSHOP'S 35TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. GARY G. MILLER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, October 3, 2001

  Mr. GARY MILLER of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise to pay tribute and 
honor the accomplishments of the Pomona Valley Workshop on its 35th 
Anniversary of dedicated service to individuals with developmental 
disabilities in Western San Bernardino County and Eastern Los Angeles 
County.
  The Pomona Valley Workshop is one of the largest employers in the 
city of Montclair and strives to maintain the highest of standards in 
its provision of traditional and innovative services. As an active 
member of the local community, the Workshop's efforts to improve the 
public's understanding of issues which affect persons with disabilities 
have resulted in strong community support and volunteer efforts.
  I salute the Pomona Valley Workshop on the outstanding role it has 
played in assisting adults with disabilities achieve their highest 
level of employment and community integration. I wish them continued 
success in their exemplary endeavors.
```

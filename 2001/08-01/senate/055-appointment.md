# APPOINTMENT

```
                              APPOINTMENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President of the 
Senate, and after consultation with the Democratic leader, pursuant to 
Public Law 106-286, appoints the Senator from Indiana (Mr. Bayh) to 
serve on the Congressional-Executive Commission on the People's 
Republic of China, vice the Senator from Oregon (Mr. Smith), and 
appoints the Senator from Montana (Mr. Baucus) as Chairman of the 
Commission.
```

# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, AUGUST 2, 2001

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, AUGUST 2, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m., Thursday, August 2. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Thursday, immediately following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate resume consideration of the VA-HUD Appropriations 
Act, with Senator Nelson of Florida to be recognized to offer an 
amendment at that time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

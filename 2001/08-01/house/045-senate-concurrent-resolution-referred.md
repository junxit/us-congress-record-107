# SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION REFERRED

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 45

```
                 SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION REFERRED

  A concurrent resolution of the Senate of the following title was 
taken from the Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as 
follows:

       S. Con. Res. 45. Concurrent resolution expressing the sense 
     of the Congress that the Humane Methods of Slaughter Act of 
     1958 should be fully enforced so as to prevent needless 
     suffering of animals; to the Committee on Agriculture.

                          ____________________
```

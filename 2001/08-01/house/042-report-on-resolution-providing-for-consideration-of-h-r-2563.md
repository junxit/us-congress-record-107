# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2563, BIPARTISAN PATIENT PROTECTION ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 219, H.R. 2563

```
[[Page H5177]]
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2563, 
                   BIPARTISAN PATIENT PROTECTION ACT

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 107-184) on the resolution (H. Res. 219) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 2563) to amend the Public Health 
Service Act, the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974, and 
the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to protect consumers in managed care 
plans and other health coverage, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```

# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 6, 2001, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY VICENTE FOX, PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED MEXICAN STATES

```
 AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 6, 
  2001, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY 
          VICENTE FOX, PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED MEXICAN STATES

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may in order 
at any time on Thursday, September 6, 2001, for the Speaker to declare 
a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the purpose of 
receiving in joint meeting His Excellency Vicente Fox, President of the 
United Mexican States.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from South Dakota?
  There was no objection.
```

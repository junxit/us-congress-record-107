# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Hollings, Ernest F. (H000725) [D-SC]

```

period of morning business with each speaker not to exceed 15 minutes 
in their presentations.

  Mr. HOLLINGS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order
```

**Measures:** H.R. 333

```
 AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN THE ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 
  333, BANKRUPTCY ABUSE PREVENTION AND CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT OF 2001
```

# ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** Hansen, James V. (H000172) [R-UT]

**Measures:** H.Res. 21

```
    ELECTION OF MEMBERS TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. HANSEN. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Republican Conference, I 
offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 21) and ask for its immediate 
consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                               H. Res. 21

       Resolved, That the following named Members be, and are 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committees of the 
     House of Representatives:
       Budget: Mr. Sununu to rank after Mr. Nussle.
       House Administration: Mr. Ney, Chairman.
       Standards of Official Conduct: Mr. Hefley, Chairman.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```

# SENATE RESOLUTION 9--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S.Res. 9

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 9--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF 
                THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  Mr. ROBERTS (for Mr. Lott (for himself and Mr. Daschle)) submitted 
the following resolution which was considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 9

       Resolved, That the President of the United States be 
     notified of the election of Strom Thurmond, a Senator from 
     the State of South Carolina, as President pro tempore.

                          ____________________
```

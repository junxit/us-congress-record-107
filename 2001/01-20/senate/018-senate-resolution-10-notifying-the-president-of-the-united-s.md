# SENATE RESOLUTION 10--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 10

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 10--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF 
         THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE

  Mr. ROBERTS (for Mr. Lott (for himself and Mr. Daschle)) submitted 
the following resolution which was considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 10

       Resolved, That the House of Representatives be notified of 
     the election of Strom Thurmond, a Senator from the State of 
     South Carolina, as President pro tempore.
```

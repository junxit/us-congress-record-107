# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.Res. 126

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       72. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of the 
     State of Ohio, relative to Senate Resolution No. 126 
     memorializing the United States Congress to reintroduce and 
     pass the New Markets for State-Inspected Meat Act as a means 
     of assisting small meat-packing operations and to restore 
     fairness to the meat industry in this country; to the 
     Committee on Agriculture.
       73. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Alaska, relative to Resolution No. 13 memorializing the 
     United States Congress to amend the tax code to eliminate the 
     marriage penalty; to the Committee on Ways and Means.
```

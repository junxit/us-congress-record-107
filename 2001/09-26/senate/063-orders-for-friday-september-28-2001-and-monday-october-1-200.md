# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 28, 2001, AND MONDAY, OCTOBER 1, 2001

```
   ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 28, 2001, AND MONDAY, OCTOBER 1, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 a.m. 
Friday, September 28, for a pro forma session, and that following the 
pro forma session, the Senate stand in adjournment until 12 noon, 
Monday, October 1. Further, on Monday, immediately following the prayer 
and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and there then be a period for morning

[[Page 18113]]

business until 2 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 
minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

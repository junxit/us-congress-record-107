# Text of House Amendment 5

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2311

```
H.R. 2311

                       Offered By: Mr. Traficant

       Amendment No. 5: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) add the following section:
       Sec.   . No funds in this Act may be used to drill for oil 
     and gas, through, in or under, the Mosquito Creek Reservoir, 
     Trumbull County, Ohio.
```

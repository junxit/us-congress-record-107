# Text of House Amendment 19

**Speaking:** Royce, Edward R. (R000487) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2330

```
H.R. 2330

                         Offered By: Mr. Royce

       Amendment No. 19: Insert before the short title the 
     following new section:
       Sec. ____. None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act may be used to award any new 
     allocations under the market access program or to pay the 
     salaries of personnel to award such allocations.
```

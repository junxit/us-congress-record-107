# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF A CONCURRENT RESOLUTION FOR THE ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE AND SENATE FOR THE INDEPENDENCE DAY DISTRICT WORK PERIOD

**Measures:** H.Res. 182

```
   REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF A CONCURRENT 
    RESOLUTION FOR THE ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE AND SENATE FOR THE 
                 INDEPENDENCE DAY DISTRICT WORK PERIOD

  Mr. HASTINGS of Washington, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 107-117) on the resolution (H. Res. 182) 
providing for consideration of a concurrent resolution providing for 
adjournment of the House and Senate for the Independence Day district 
work period, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```

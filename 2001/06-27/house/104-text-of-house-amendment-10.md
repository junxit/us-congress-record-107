# Text of House Amendment 10

**Speaking:** Kaptur, Marcy (K000009) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 2330

```
H.R. 2330

                         Offered By: Ms. Kaptur

       Amendment No. 10: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec. ____. (a) The Secretary of Agriculture shall continue 
     in fiscal year 2002 the Global Food for Education Initiative 
     program implemented in fiscal year 2001, at the level 
     implemented in fiscal year 2001.
       (b) For all purposes under the Congressional Budget Act of 
     1974 and the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control 
     Act of 1985, the Congressional Budget Office and the Office 
     of Management and Budget shall treat the budget authority and 
     outlays associated with continuing the Global Food for 
     Education Initiative at the level implemented in fiscal year 
     2001 as part of the baseline costs of the Commodity Credit 
     Corporation in fiscal year 2002 and shall not attribute any 
     additional new budget authority or outlays to this Act 
     because of the directive contained in subsection (a).
```

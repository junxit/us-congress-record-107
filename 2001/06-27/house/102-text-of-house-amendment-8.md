# Text of House Amendment 8

**Speaking:** Hinchey, Maurice D. (H000627) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2330

```
H.R. 2330

                        Offered By: Mr. Hinchey

       Amendment No. 8: Insert before the short title the 
     following new section:
       Sec. ____. None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act shall be used to eliminate employment 
     positions (or alter the tasks assigned to the persons filling 
     such employment positions) related to the operation of the 
     American Heritage Rivers Initiative.
```

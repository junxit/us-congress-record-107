# Text of House Amendment 18

**Speaking:** Mink, Patsy T. (M000797) [D-HI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2330

```
H.R. 2330

                    Offered By: Mrs. Mink of Hawaii

       Amendment No. 18: Insert before the short title at the end 
     the following new section:
       Sec. ____. Of the amount for the Department of Agriculture 
     provided under the heading ``Agricultural Research Service''-
     ``salaries and expenses'' in title I, the Secretary of 
     Agriculture shall provide $1,603,000, the same amount as was 
     provided for fiscal year 2001, for tropical aquaculture 
     research for the Oceanic Institute of Hawaii for continuation 
     of the comprehensive research program focused on feeds, 
     nutrition, and global competitiveness of the United States 
     aquaculture industry.
```

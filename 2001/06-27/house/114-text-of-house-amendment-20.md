# Text of House Amendment 20

**Speaking:** Sanders, Bernard (S000033) [I-VT]

**Measures:** H.R. 2330

```
H.R. 2330

                        Offered By: Mr. Sanders

       Amendment No. 20: At the end of title VII, insert after the 
     last section (preceding any short title) the following 
     section:
       Sec. 7____. None of the amounts made available in this Act 
     for the Food and Drug Administration may be used for 
     enforcing section 801(d)(1) of the Federal Food, Drug, and 
     Cosmetic Act.
```

# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 28, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 28, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:15 a.m. on 
Thursday, June 28. I further ask consent that on Thursday, immediately 
following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
resume consideration of the Patients' Bill of Rights.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

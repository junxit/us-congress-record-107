# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 12, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 12, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. Tuesday, June 12. I further ask unanimous consent that on Tuesday, 
immediately following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, if necessary, and the Senate resume consideration of S. 1, the 
education authorization bill. I further ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. for the weekly party 
conferences.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

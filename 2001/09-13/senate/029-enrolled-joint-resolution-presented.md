# ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

```
                  ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, September 13, 
2000, she had presented to the President of the United States the 
following enrolled joint resolution:

       S.J. Res. 22. A joint resolution expressing the sense of 
     the Senate and House of Representatives regarding the 
     terrorist attacks launched against the United States on 
     September 11, 2001.

                          ____________________
```

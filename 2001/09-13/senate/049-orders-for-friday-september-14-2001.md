# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 14, 2001

```
                 ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 14, 2001

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9 o'clock 
tomorrow morning, Friday, September 14. I further ask that on Friday, 
immediately following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

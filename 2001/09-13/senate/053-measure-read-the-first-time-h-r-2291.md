# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 2291

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2291

```
                 MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 2291

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand that H.R. 2291, just received 
from the House, is at the desk. I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2291) to extend the authorization of the Drug-
     Free Communities Support Program for an additional 5 years, 
     to authorize a National Community Antidrug Coalition 
     Institute, and for other purposes.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading but object 
to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read for 
the second reading on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```

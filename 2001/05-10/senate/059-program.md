# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BENNETT. For the information of all Senators, when the Senate 
convenes at 12 noon on Monday, there will be 2 hours of morning 
business. Following morning business, the Senate will resume 
consideration of the education bill and the Reid amendment No. 460. 
Under the order, if it is agreed to, there will be up to 1 hour of 
debate on the amendment which will then be laid aside.
  Also on Monday, Senator Cleland will be recognized at 4 p.m. to 
resume debate of his modified amendment No. 376. A vote in relation to 
the Reid amendment will begin at 5:30 p.m. and following that vote and 
some closing remarks, a vote is expected in relation to the Cleland 
amendment. Senators should therefore be on notice that at least the two 
votes will occur on Monday evening at 5:30 p.m.

                          ____________________
```

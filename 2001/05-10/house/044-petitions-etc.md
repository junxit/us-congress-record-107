# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Legislature of 
     Rockland County, New York, relative to Resolution No. 162 
     petitioning the United States Congress to condemn the 
     invitation extended by President George W. Bush to Ian 
     Paisley and the anti-Catholic rhetoric that Ian Paisley 
     espouses; and that the Legislature reaffirms its support for 
     peace and freedom in Northern Ireland; to the Committee on 
     International Relations.
```

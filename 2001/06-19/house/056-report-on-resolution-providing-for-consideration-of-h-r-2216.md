# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2216, SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2001

**Measures:** H.Res. 171, H.R. 2216

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2216, 
            SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2001

  Mr. SESSIONS (during Special Order of Mr. Weldon of Pennsylvania),
```

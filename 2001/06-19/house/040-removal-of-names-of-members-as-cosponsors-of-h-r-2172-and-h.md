# REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 2172 AND H.R. 2118

**Speaking:** Greenwood, James C. (G000439) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2118, H.R. 2172

```
  REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 2172 AND H.R. 2118

  Mr. GREENWOOD. Madam Speaker, on Thursday last week, June 14, 2001, 
the following cosponsors were incorrectly added to H.R. 2172, and I ask

[[Page H3232]]

unanimous consent that they be removed at this time:
  Frank Wolf
  Major Owens
  Carolyn McCarthy
  Frank Pallone
  Richard Neal.
  Also, the following cosponsors were incorrectly added to H.R. 2118, 
and I ask unanimous consent that they be removed at this time:
  Henry Waxman
  Martin Frost.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

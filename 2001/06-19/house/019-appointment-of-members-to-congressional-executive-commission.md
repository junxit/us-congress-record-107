# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO CONGRESSIONAL-EXECUTIVE COMMISSION ON THE PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA

```
  APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO CONGRESSIONAL-EXECUTIVE COMMISSION ON THE 
                       PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Biggert). Without objection, and 
pursuant to section 303(a) of Public Law 106-286, the Chair announces 
the Speaker's appointment of the following Members of the House to the 
Congressional-Executive Commission on the People's Republic of China:
  Mr. Bereuter, Nebraska, cochairman;
  Mr. Leach, Iowa;
  Mr. Dreier, California;
  Mr. Wolf, Virginia;
  Mr. Pitts, Pennsylvania.
  There was no objection.
```

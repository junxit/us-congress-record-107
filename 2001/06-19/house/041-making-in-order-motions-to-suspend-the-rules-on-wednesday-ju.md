# MAKING IN ORDER MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES ON WEDNESDAY, JUNE 20, 2001

```
  MAKING IN ORDER MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES ON WEDNESDAY, JUNE 20, 
                                  2001

  Mr. GREENWOOD. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it be in 
order at any time on the legislative day of Wednesday, June 20, 2001, 
for the Speaker to entertain motions that the House suspend the rules 
relating to the following measures: S. 1029, H. Res. 124, H. Res. 168, 
H.R. 1753, H.R. 819, and S. Con. Res. 41.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

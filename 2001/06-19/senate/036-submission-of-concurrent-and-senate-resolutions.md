# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 51, S.Res. 113

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (for acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mrs. BOXER (for herself and Mrs. Feinstein):
       S. Res. 113. A resolution congratulating the Los Angeles 
     Lakers on their second consecutive National Basketball 
     Association championship; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. BROWNBACK (for himself and Mr. Lott):
       S. Con. Res. 51. A concurrent resolution recognizing the 
     historical significance of Juneteenth Independence Day and

     a means of understanding the past and solving the challenges 
     of the future; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```

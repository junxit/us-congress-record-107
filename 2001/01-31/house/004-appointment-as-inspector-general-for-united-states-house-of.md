# APPOINTMENT AS INSPECTOR GENERAL FOR UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

```
      APPOINTMENT AS INSPECTOR GENERAL FOR UNITED STATES HOUSE OF 
                            REPRESENTATIVES

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to clause 6 of rule II, the Speaker, majority 
leader, and minority leader jointly appoint Mr. Steven A. McNamara of 
Sterling, Virginia, to the position of Inspector General for the United 
States House of Representatives for the 107th Congress, effective 
January 3, 2001.

                          ____________________
```

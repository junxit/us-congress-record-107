# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ALLEN. Tomorrow the Senate will resume debate on the Ashcroft 
nomination at 9 a.m. under the order. Closing remarks will be made 
throughout the morning. Senators should be aware that a vote on 
confirmation will occur at 1:45 p.m. Following the final confirmation 
of the President's Cabinet, the Senate is expected to adjourn in an 
effort to accommodate those participating in the party retreats taking 
place tomorrow afternoon and into the weekend.
```

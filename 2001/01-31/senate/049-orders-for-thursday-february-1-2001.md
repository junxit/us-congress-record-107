# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 1, 2001

**Speaking:** Allen, George (A000121) [R-VA]

```
[[Page S930]]
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 1, 2001

  Mr. ALLEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9 
a.m. on Thursday, February 1. I further ask consent that on Thursday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
then resume consideration of the nomination of John Ashcroft to be 
Attorney General, as under the previous order.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

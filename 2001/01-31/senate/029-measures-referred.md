# MEASURES REFERRED

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bill was read the first and second times by unanimous 
consent and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 93. An act to amend title 5, United States Code, to 
     provide that the mandatory separation age for Federal 
     firefighters be made the same as the age that applies with 
     respect to Federal law enforcement officers; to the Committee 
     on Governmental Affairs.

  The following concurrent resolutions were read, and referred as 
indicated:

       H. Con. Res. 14. Concurrent resolution authorizing the 
     Rotunda of the Capitol to be used for a ceremony as part of 
     the commemoration of the days of remembrance of victims of 
     the Holocaust; to the Committee on Rules and Administration.
       H. Con. Res. 15. Concurrent resolution relative to the 
     victims of the deadly earthquake in the State of Gujarat in 
     western India; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
```

# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 12, 2001

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 12, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m., Wednesday, December 12; that immediately following the prayer and 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the farm bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

     the administration of certain Federal election laws and 
     programs, to establish minimum election administration 
     standards for States and units of local government with 
     responsibility for the administration of Federal elections, 
     and for other purposes (Rept. 107-331). Referred to the House 
     Calendar.
       Mr. GOSS: Committee on Rules. House Resolution 312. 
     Resolution waiving points of order against the conference 
     report to accompany the bill (H.R. 2883) to authorize 
     appropriations for fiscal year 2002 for intelligence and 
     intelligence-related activities of the United States 
     Government, the Community Management Account, and the Central 
     Intelligence Agency Retirement and Disability System, and for 
     the other purposes (Rept. 107-332). Referred to the House 
     Calendar.

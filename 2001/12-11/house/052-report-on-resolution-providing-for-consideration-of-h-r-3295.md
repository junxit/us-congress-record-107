# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3295, HELP AMERICA VOTE ACT OF 2001

**Measures:** H.Res. 311, H.R. 3295

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3295, HELP 
                        AMERICA VOTE ACT OF 2001

  Mr. REYNOLDS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 107-331) on the resolution (H. Res. 311) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3295) to establish a program to 
provide funds to States to replace punch card voting systems, to 
establish the Election Assistance Commission to assist in the 
administration of Federal elections and to otherwise provide assistance 
with the administration of certain Federal election laws and programs, 
to establish minimum election administration standards for States and 
units of local government with responsibility for the administration of 
Federal elections, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```

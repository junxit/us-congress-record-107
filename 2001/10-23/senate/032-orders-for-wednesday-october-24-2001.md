# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 24, 2001

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 24, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10:30 
a.m., Wednesday, October 24; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate resume consideration of the Foreign 
Operations Appropriations Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

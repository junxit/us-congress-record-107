# Text of Senate Amendment 1909

**Speaking:** Leahy, Patrick J. (L000174) [D-VT]

**Measures:** H.R. 2506

```
  SA 1909. Mr. LEAHY (for himself and Mr. McConnell) proposed an 
amendment to the bill H.R. 2506, making appropriations for foreign 
operations, export financing, and related programs for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 2002, and for other purposes; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in the bill insert the following:


                           KENNETH M. LUDDEN

       Sec.  . This Act shall be cited as the Kenneth M. Ludden 
     Foreign Operations, Export Financing, and Related Programs 
     Appropriations Act, Fiscal Year 2002.
                                  ____
```

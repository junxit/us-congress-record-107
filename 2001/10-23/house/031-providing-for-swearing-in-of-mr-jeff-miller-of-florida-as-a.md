# PROVIDING FOR SWEARING IN OF MR. JEFF MILLER, OF FLORIDA, AS A MEMBER OF THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** Young, C. W. Bill (Y000031) [R-FL]

```
 PROVIDING FOR SWEARING IN OF MR. JEFF MILLER, OF FLORIDA, AS A MEMBER 
                              OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. YOUNG of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
gentleman from Florida (Mr. Jeff Miller) be permitted to take the oath 
of office today. His certificate of election has not arrived, but there 
is no contest; and no question has been raised with regard to his 
election.
  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
Florida?
  There was no objection.
```

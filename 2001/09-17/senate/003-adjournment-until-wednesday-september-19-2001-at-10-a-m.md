# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 19, 2001, AT 10 A.M.

```
      ADJOURNMENT UNTIL WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 19, 2001, AT 10 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will now stand in adjournment until the hour of 10 a.m., on 
Wednesday, September 19, 2001.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 o'clock and 40 seconds a.m., adjourned 
until Wednesday, September 19, 2001, at 10 a.m.
```

# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 82, S.Con.Res. 83

                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

announced that the Senate has passed concurrent resolutions of the 
following titles in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. Con. Res. 82. Concurrent resolution authorizing the 2002 
     Winter Olympics Torch Relay to come onto the Capitol Grounds.
       S. Con. Res. 83. Concurrent resolution providing for a

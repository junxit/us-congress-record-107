# MODIFICATION OF ORDER FOR MORNING BUSINESS

```
               MODIFICATION OF ORDER FOR MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I earlier asked unanimous consent that the 
Senate go into a period of morning business tomorrow morning beginning 
at 10 o'clock.
  I want to modify that request. I ask unanimous consent that Senator 
Reid of Nevada and Senator Ensign be allowed to speak for 10 minutes 
each during the morning business time tomorrow.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```

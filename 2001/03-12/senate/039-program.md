# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will convene at 9:30 a.m. and will resume consideration of the 
bankruptcy legislation at 10 a.m. Two votes have been ordered to occur 
at 11 a.m. on the Kennedy amendment and the Feinstein amendment, as 
modified. Following the policy luncheons at 2:15 p.m., there will be 30 
minutes for debate on the Conrad and Sessions amendments with votes 
ordered to occur at 2:45 tomorrow afternoon.
```

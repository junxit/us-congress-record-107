# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business for not to 
extend beyond the hour of 2 p.m. with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the following exceptions: 
Senator Thomas or his designee for 30 minutes; Senator Durbin or his 
designee for 30 minutes.

                          ____________________
```

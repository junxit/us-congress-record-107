# Text of House Amendment 10

**Speaking:** Sanders, Bernard (S000033) [I-VT]

**Measures:** H.R. 2590

```
H.R. 2590

                        Offered By: Mr. Sanders

       Amendment No. 10: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following:
       Sec. __. None of the funds made available in this Act for 
     the United States Customs Service may be used to allow the 
     importation into the United States of any good, ware, 
     article, or merchandise on which the United States Customs 
     Service has in effect a detention order, pursuant to section 
     307 of the Tariff Act of 1930, on the basis that the good, 
     ware, article, or merchandise may have been mined, produced, 
     or manufactured by forced or indentured child labor.
```

# MORNING HOUR DEBATES

```
                          MORNING HOUR DEBATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of 
January 3, 2001, the Chair will now recognize Members from lists 
submitted by the majority and minority leaders for morning hour 
debates. The Chair will alternate recognition between the parties, with 
each party limited to 25 minutes, and each Member except the majority 
leader, the minority leader or the minority whip limited to not to 
exceed 5 minutes, but in no event shall debate continue past 9:50 a.m.
  The Chair recognizes the gentlewoman from California (Ms. Solis) for 
5 minutes.

                          ____________________
```

# Text of House Amendment 11

**Speaking:** Souder, Mark E. (S001143) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2590

```
H.R. 2590

                         Offered By: Mr. Souder

       Amendment No. 11: In title III, in the item relating to 
     ``Federal Drug Control Programs-high intensity drug 
     trafficking areas program'', before the period at the end 
     insert the following:

     : Provided further, That the Director shall reduce by 5 
     percent funds expended in High Intensity Drug Trafficking 
     Areas containing States that permit the use of Schedule I 
     controlled substances under State law in a manner 
     inconsistent with the Controlled Substances Act (Public Law 
     91-513)
```

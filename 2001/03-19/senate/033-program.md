# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
the Senate will begin consideration of another amendment to the 
campaign finance reform bill beginning at 9:30 a.m. tomorrow. A vote is 
expected to occur at approximately noon, prior to adjourning for the 
weekly party conferences. When the Senate reconvenes at 2:15, further 
amendments will be offered. By a previous agreement, there will be up 
to 3 hours of debate prior to

[[Page 3954]]

a vote in relation to amendments. Therefore, Senators may expect votes 
approximately every 3 hours throughout the day.
```

# AUTHORIZING THE USE OF THE CAPITOL ROTUNDA

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 223

```
               AUTHORIZING THE USE OF THE CAPITOL ROTUNDA

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 223, just 
received from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 223) permitting the 
     use of the Rotunda of the Capitol for a prayer vigil in 
     memory of those who lost their lives in the events of 
     September 11, 2001.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.

[[Page S9342]]

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the concurrent 
resolution be agreed to and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 223) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```

# VISIT TO THE SENATE BY THE PRIME MINISTER OF AUSTRALIA, JOHN HOWARD

**Speaking:** Biden, Joseph R., Jr. (B000444) [D-DE]

```
  VISIT TO THE SENATE BY THE PRIME MINISTER OF AUSTRALIA, JOHN HOWARD

  Mr. BIDEN. Mr. President, there has been a request, with which I 
agree, that we recess for up to 3 minutes. We were to hear from the 
distinguished Prime Minister of Australia, one of our

[[Page S9289]]

strongest allies, a man who himself is in deep sympathy and mourning 
for this Nation, in a joint meeting. He is in the Chamber.
```

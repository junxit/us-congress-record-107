# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

```
                  ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
adjournment, under the provisions of S.J. Res. 22 as a further mark of 
respect to the victims of the terrorist attacks.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 7:20 p.m., adjourned until 
Thursday, September 13, 2001, at 9:30 a.m.
```

# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, based on the ruling of the Chair, tomorrow 
morning the Senate will convene at 9:30 a.m., with a period for morning

appropriations act. We hope to complete action on the Commerce-State-

accomplishment for us to be able to do that. There will be votes
```

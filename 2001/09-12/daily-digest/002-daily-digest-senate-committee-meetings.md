# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
GOVERNMENT INFRASTRUCTURE
Committee on Governmental Affairs: Committee concluded hearings to 
examine the security of critical governmental infrastructure, 
challenges in protecting federal systems, development of analysis, and 
warning capabilities, including the Presidential Decision Directive 63, 
a strategy for protecting our nation's critical infrastructures from 
computer-bases attacks, after receiving testimony from Roberta L. 
Gross, Inspector General, National Aeronautics and Space 
Administration; and Joel C. Willemssen, Managing Director, Information 
Technology Issues, General Accounting Office.
```

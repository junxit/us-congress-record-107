# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


          subcommittee on forests, and public land management

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr President, I would like to announce for the public that 
a hearing has been scheduled before the Subcommittee on Forests and 
Public Land Management of the Committee on Energy and Natural 
Resources.
  The hearing will take place on Thursday, April 26, 2001 at 2:00 p.m., 
in room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building In Washington, 
D.C.
  The purpose of this hearing is to conduct oversight on the energy 
implications of the Forest Service's Roadless Area Rulemaking.
  Those who wish to submit written statements should write to the 
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, U.S. Senate, Washington, 
D.C. 20510. For further information, please call Mark Rey (202) 224-
2878.




                          ____________________
```

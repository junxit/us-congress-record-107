# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Sherman, Brad (S000344) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 93

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. SHERMAN. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 93 
of the 107th Congress, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Pence). Pursuant to House Concurrent 
Resolution 93 of the 107th Congress, the House stands adjourned until 2 
p.m. on Tuesday, April 24, 2001.
  Thereupon (at 6 o'clock and 58 minutes p.m.), pursuant to House 
Concurrent Resolution 93, the House adjourned until Tuesday, April 24, 
2001, at 2 p.m.
```

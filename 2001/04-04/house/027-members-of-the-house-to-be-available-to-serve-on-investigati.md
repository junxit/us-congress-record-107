# MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE TO BE AVAILABLE TO SERVE ON INVESTIGATIVE SUBCOMMITTEES OF THE COMMITTEE ON STANDARDS OF OFFICIAL CONDUCT

```
    MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE TO BE AVAILABLE TO SERVE ON INVESTIGATIVE 
    SUBCOMMITTEES OF THE COMMITTEE ON STANDARDS OF OFFICIAL CONDUCT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to clause 
5(a)(4)(A) of rule X, the Chair announces that the Speaker named the 
following Members of the House to be available to serve on 
investigation subcommittees of the Committee on Standards of Official 
Conduct for the 107th Congress:
  Mr. Gekas of Pennsylvania;
  Mr. Chabot of Ohio;
  Mr. LaTourette of Ohio;
  Mr. Shadegg of Arizona;
  Mr. Wicker of Mississippi;
  Mr. Moran of Kansas;
  Mr. Fossella of New York;
  Mr. Green of Wisconsin; and
  Mr. Terry of Nebraska.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```

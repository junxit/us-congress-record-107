# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, I have been asked on behalf of the 
distinguished majority leader to announce that today the Senate will 
immediately resume consideration of the budget resolution with the time 
between now and 10:30 a.m. equally divided for debate on the Grassley 
and Johnson amendments regarding agriculture. At 10:30 a.m. there will 
be two back-to-back votes on these amendments. Senator Harkin will be 
recognized to offer the next amendment on education.
  Further amendments will be offered with votes to occur throughout the 
day.
  Senators will be notified as votes are scheduled. I thank my 
colleagues for their attention.

                          ____________________
```

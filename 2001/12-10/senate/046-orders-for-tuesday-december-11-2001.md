# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 11, 2001

```
                 ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 11, 2001

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m., Tuesday, December 11; that immediately following the prayer and 
pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and the Senate proceed to executive 
session; further, that the Senate recess from 12:30 to 2:15 p.m. for 
the weekly party conferences.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST

**Speaking:** Harkin, Tom (H000206) [D-IA], Thomas, Craig (T000162) [R-WY]

```
                       UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST

  Mr. HARKIN. That being the case, I ask unanimous consent all first-
degree amendments to the farm bill be filed no later than 3 o'clock 
tomorrow afternoon.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. THOMAS. I object. I am afraid there is not time for all 
amendments. I object.
  Mr. HARKIN. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Ms. Stabenow). The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. HARKIN. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum 
call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

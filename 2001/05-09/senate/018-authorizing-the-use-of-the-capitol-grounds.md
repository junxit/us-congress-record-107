# AUTHORIZING THE USE OF THE CAPITOL GROUNDS

**Speaking:** Ensign, John (E000194) [R-NV]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 74

```
               AUTHORIZING THE USE OF THE CAPITOL GROUNDS

  Mr. ENSIGN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate

[[Page S4582]]

proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 74.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 74) authorizing the 
     use of the Capitol Grounds for the 20th annual National Peace 
     Officers' Memorial Service.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. ENSIGN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
concurrent resolution be agreed to and the motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 74) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```

# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 10, 2001

**Speaking:** Ensign, John (E000194) [R-NV]

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 10, 2001

  Mr. ENSIGN. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes 
its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 a.m. on Thursday, 
May 10. I further ask consent that on Thursday, immediately following 
the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and that the Senate then resume 
consideration of the conference report to accompany the budget 
resolution as under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Tiahrt, Todd (T000260) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 146

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. TIAHRT. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 146, 
107th Congress, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Accordingly, the House stands adjourned 
until 2 p.m. on Tuesday, June 5, 2001, pursuant to House Concurrent 
Resolution 146, or, under the previous order of the House, until 2 p.m. 
on Wednesday, May 30, 2001, if not sooner in receipt of a message from 
the Senate transmitting its concurrence in House Concurrent Resolution 
146.
  Thereupon (at 10 o'clock and 19 minutes a.m., legislative day of May 
25, 2001), pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 146, the House 
adjourned until Wednesday, May 30, 2001, at 2 p.m.

                          ____________________
```

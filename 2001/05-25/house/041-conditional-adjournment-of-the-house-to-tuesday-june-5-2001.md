# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE TO TUESDAY, JUNE 5, 2001

```
     CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE TO TUESDAY, JUNE 5, 2001

  Mr. TIAHRT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it stand adjourned until 2 p.m. on Wednesday, May 30, 
2001, unless the House sooner receives a message from the Senate 
transmitting its concurrence to House Concurrent Resolution 146, in 
which case the House shall stand adjourned pursuant to that concurrent 
resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaHood). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Kansas?
  There was no objection.
```

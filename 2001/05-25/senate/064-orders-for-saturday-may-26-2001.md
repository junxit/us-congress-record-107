# ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, MAY 26, 2001

```
                   ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, MAY 26, 2001

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, I now ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 
a.m. on Saturday, May 26. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Saturday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then begin a period of morning business with Senators speaking 
for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

# PROGRAM

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BOND. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will be in 
a period of morning business until the tax reconciliation conference 
report is received from the House of Representatives. It is anticipated 
the Senate will be able to begin consideration of the tax 
reconciliation conference report shortly after convening.
  As a reminder, there are up to 10 hours for debate on the conference 
report. Therefore, a vote is expected to occur late morning or tomorrow 
afternoon.
```

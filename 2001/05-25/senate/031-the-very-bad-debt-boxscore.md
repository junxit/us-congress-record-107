  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business yesterday, 
Thursday, May 24, 2001, the Federal debt stood at 
$5,660,965,921,275.71, five trillion, six hundred sixty billion, nine 
hundred sixty-five million, nine hundred twenty-one thousand, two 
hundred seventy-five dollars and seventy-one cents.

seven hundred sixty-two million.
  five years ago, May 24, 1996, the Federal debt stood at

twenty-five million.

$3,481,461,000,000, three trillion, four hundred eighty-one billion, 
four hundred sixty-one million.

$607,559,000,000, six hundred seven billion, five hundred fifty-nine

$5,053,406,921,275.71, five trillion, fifty-three billion, four hundred

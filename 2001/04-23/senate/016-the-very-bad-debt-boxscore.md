$5,707,061,000,000 Five trillion, seven hundred seven billion, sixty-
one million.

$1,962,745,000,000, One trillion, nine hundred sixty-two billion, seven 
hundred forty-five million.

$604,399,000,000, Six hundred four billion, three hundred ninety-nine 
million, which reflects a debt increase of more than $5 trillion, 
$5,109,232,148,647.61, Five trillion, one hundred nine billion, two

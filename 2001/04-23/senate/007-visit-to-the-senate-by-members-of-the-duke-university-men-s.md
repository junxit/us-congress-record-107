# VISIT TO THE SENATE BY MEMBERS OF THE DUKE UNIVERSITY MEN'S NCAA CHAMPIONSHIP BASKETBALL TEAM

```
   VISIT TO THE SENATE BY MEMBERS OF THE DUKE UNIVERSITY MEN'S NCAA 
                      CHAMPIONSHIP BASKETBALL TEAM

  Mr. HELMS. Madam President, pursuant to the permission given me by 
the majority leader, and with the agreement of the minority leader, it 
is my honor to have invited the Duke University basketball team, the 
NCAA champions of this year, along with the wives of those who have 
wives, and the coaches and their wives, to come to the Senate floor.
```

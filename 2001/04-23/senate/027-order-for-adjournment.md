# ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. VOINOVICH. If there is no further business to come before the 
Senate, I now ask unanimous consent the Senate stand in adjournment 
under the previous order following the remarks of Senator Nelson of 
Florida.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. VOINOVICH. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. NELSON of Florida. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
the order for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Voinovich). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```

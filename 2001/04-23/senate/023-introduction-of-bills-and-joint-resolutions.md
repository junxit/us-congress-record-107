# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 756, S. 757

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. GRASSLEY:
       S. 756. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to extend and modify the credit for electricity produced from 
     biomass, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. SPECTER:
       S. 757. A bill to designate the Federal building and United 
     States courthouse located at 504 West Hamilton Street in 
     Allentown, Pennsylvania, as the ``Edward N. Cahn Federal 
     Building and United States Courthouse''; to the Committee on 
     Environment and Public Works.
```

# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 24, 2001

**Speaking:** Voinovich, George V. (V000126) [R-OH]

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, APRIL 24, 2001

  Mr. VOINOVICH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Tuesday, April 24. I further ask unanimous consent that on 
Tuesday, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then begin a period of morning business, equally divided, with 
Senators speaking for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. VOINOVICH. I further ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand 
in recess from the hours of 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. for the weekly 
policy conferences to meet.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

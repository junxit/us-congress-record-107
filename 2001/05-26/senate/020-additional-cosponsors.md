# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 530, S. 749, S. 756

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 530

  At the request of Mr. Grassley, the name of the Senator from 
Massachusetts (Mr. Kerry) was added as a cosponsor of S. 530, a bill to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide a 5-year extension 
of the credit for producing electricity from wind.


                                 S. 749

  At the request of Mr. Fitzgerald, the name of the Senator from South 
Dakota (Mr. Johnson) was added as a cosponsor of S. 749, a bill to 
provide that no Federal income tax shall be imposed on amounts received 
by victims of the Nazi regime or their heirs or estates, and for other 
purposes.


                                 S. 756

  At the request of Mr. Grassley, the names of the Senator from 
Maryland (Mr. Sarbanes) and the Senator from Maryland (Ms. Mikulski) 
were added as cosponsors of S. 756, a bill to amend the Internal 
Revenue Code of 1986 to extend and modify the credit for electricity 
produced from biomass, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```

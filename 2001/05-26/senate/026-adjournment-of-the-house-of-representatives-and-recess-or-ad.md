# ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND RECESS OR ADJOURNMENT OF THE SENATE

**Speaking:** Ensign, John (E000194) [R-NV]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 146

```
[[Page S5802]]
 ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND RECESS OR ADJOURNMENT 
                             OF THE SENATE

  Mr. ENSIGN. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to the 
immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 146, the adjournment 
resolution, which is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the tile.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 146) providing for a 
     conditional adjournment of the House of Representatives and a 
     conditional recess or adjournment of the Senate.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. ENSIGN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the concurrent 
resolution be agreed to and the motion to reconsider by laid upon the 
table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 146) was agreed to.
```

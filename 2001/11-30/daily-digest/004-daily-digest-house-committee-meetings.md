# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 1, H.R. 2299

```
Committee Meetings
  No committee meetings were held.

Joint Meetings
TRANSPORTATION APPROPRIATIONS
Conferees on Thursday, November 29, agreed to file a conference report 
on the differences between the Senate and House passed versions of H.R. 
2299, making appropriations for the Department of Transportation and 
related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2002.
EDUCATION REFORM
Conferees met to resolve the differences between the Senate and House 
passed versions of H.R. 1, to close the achievement gap with 
accountability, flexibility, and choice, so that no child is left 
behind, but did not complete action thereon, and recessed subject to 
call.
```

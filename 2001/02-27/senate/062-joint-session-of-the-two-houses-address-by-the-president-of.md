# JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES (H. DOC. 107-1)

```
JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED 
                         STATES (H. DOC. 107-1)

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate will proceed to the Hall of the 
House of Representatives to hear the address by the President of the 
United States.
  Thereupon, the Senate, preceded by the Sergeant at Arms, James W. 
Ziglar, the Secretary of the Senate, Gary Sisco, and the Vice President 
of the United States, Dick Cheney, proceeded to the hall of the House 
of Representatives to hear the address by the President of the United 
States, George W. Bush.
  (The address delivered by the President of the United States to the 
joint session of the two Houses of Congress appears in the proceedings 
of the House of Representatives in today's Record.)

                          ____________________
```

# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, the Senate stands in recess 
until the hour of 2:15 p.m.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:46 p.m., recessed until 
2:15 p.m.
  Whereupon, the Senate, at 2:15 p.m., reassembled when called to order 
by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Inhofe).
```

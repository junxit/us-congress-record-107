# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 24, 2001

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 24, 2001

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until the hour of 10 
a.m. on Thursday, May 24. I further ask consent that on Thursday, 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time 
for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the 
Senate then begin a period of morning business with Senators speaking 
therein for up to 5 minutes each, with the following exceptions: 
Senator Thomas, or his designee, from 10 a.m. to 10:45 a.m., and 
Senator Durbin, or his designee, from 10:45 to 11:30 a.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```

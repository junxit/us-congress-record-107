# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1765

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1765

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I understand that S. 1765 is at the desk 
and is due for its second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator is correct.
  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask that S. 1765 be read for a second 
time, and I then will object to any further proceedings at this time on 
this legislation.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the bill by 
title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1765) to improve the ability of the United 
     States to prepare for and respond to biological threat or 
     attack.
  


  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection having been heard, the 
bill will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```

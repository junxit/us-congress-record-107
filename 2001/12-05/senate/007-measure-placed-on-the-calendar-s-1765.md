**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1765

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I understand that S. 1765 is at the desk 
and is due for its second reading.

this legislation.

title.

remaining $1.50 per issue. This price increase is necessary based 
upon the cost of printing and distribution.                          
                                    Michael F. DiMario, Public
```

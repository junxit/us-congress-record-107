# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1731

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the previous 
order with respect to the debate time prior to the cloture vote on the 
motion to proceed to S. 1731 be changed to reflect that the time begin 
at 11:45 a.m. today, and that the time until 11:45 a.m. be a period of 
morning business with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 5 
minutes each, with the remaining provisions of the previous order 
remaining in effect.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. I thank the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Minnesota is recognized.

                          ____________________
```

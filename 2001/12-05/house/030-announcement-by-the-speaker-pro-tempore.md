# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 102, H.Con.Res. 242, H.Res. 298, H.R. 3348

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the Chair 
will now put the question on three motions to suspend the rules on 
which further proceedings were postponed yesterday.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H. Con. Res. 242, by the yeas and nays;
  H.R. 3348, by the yeas and nays;
  H. Con. Res. 102, by the yeas and nays.
  H. Res. 298 will be postponed until later today.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.
```

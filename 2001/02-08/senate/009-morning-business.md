# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. There will now be a period for the 
transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the hour of 11 
a.m. Under the previous order, the time from 9:30 to 10 a.m. will be 
under the control of the Senator from New Jersey.
  The Senator from New Jersey is now recognized.

                          ____________________
```

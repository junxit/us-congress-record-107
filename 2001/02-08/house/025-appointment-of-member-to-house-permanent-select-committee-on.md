# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO HOUSE PERMANENT SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

```
     APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO HOUSE PERMANENT SELECT COMMITTEE ON 
                              INTELLIGENCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection and pursuant to clause 11 
of rule X and clause 11 of rule I, the Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment of the following Member of the House to the Permanent 
Select Committee on Intelligence:
  Mr. Chambliss of Georgia, to rank after Mr. Burr of North Carolina.
  There was no objection.
```
